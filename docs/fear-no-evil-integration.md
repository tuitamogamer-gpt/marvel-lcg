# Fear No Evil, parts one and two: missions, Underlings and modular sets

These imports open Fear No Evil. Its scenarios are missions: a mission main scheme played against a chosen Underling villain instead of a villain's own scenario. Part one added two missions, The Getaway and Art Museum Heist, the Underlings Bullseye, Electro and Hammerhead, and the Cops, Drive and The Owl modular sets. Part two adds the Stop the Presses! mission, the Underlings Purple Man and Typhoid Mary, and the Tombstone and Tracksuit Mafia modular sets. Every modular set of both parts joins every scenario.

## Original source and scope

`src/game/fear-no-evil.ts` registers 108 original faces, 62 from part one and 46 from part two:

- **The Getaway:** both sides of its main scheme, Out Front and its Alongside side, Escort Vehicle, Jackknifed Tanker Truck, Burning Rubber (2) and Pedestrians (2).
- **Art Museum Heist:** both sides of its main scheme, the four Art attachments (Impossible Object, Inspiring Pottery, Replica Shield and Stunning Landscape), Art Thief (2) and Tug of War (2).
- **Bullseye:** his three stages, Adamantium-Laced Spine and its Damaged Spine side, Raise the Stakes, Deadly Sai, Incessant Pursuit (3), Lady Bullseye, Deranged Bloodlust, Everything Is A Weapon and Unerring Desire (2).
- **Electro:** his three stages, Electric Charge, Drained of Power, Whiplash, Charging Up (2), Chain Lightning (2), Energy Overload (2) and Shocking Revelation (2).
- **Hammerhead:** his three stages, Energy Gatling Gun, Hardheaded, Chameleon, Maggia Enforcers (2), Maggia Underboss (2), Consolidate Power, Power Struggle and "Head First!" (2).
- **Cops:** Cop (2), Crooked Cop, Wanted and Police Cordon. **Drive:** Sports Car, Motorcycle, Utility Van, Traffic Jam and Carjacking. **The Owl:** Flight Serum (2), Mister Fish, The Owl and Swoop Down.
- **Stop the Presses!:** both sides of its main scheme, the Daily Bugle, Ben Urich, Betty Brant, J. Jonah Jameson, Robbie Robertson, Exclusive Interview and Breaking News (2).
- **Purple Man:** his three stages, Converted, Firefighter (2), Judge, Security Guard, Skater Punk, "fight.", "protect.", "serve.", Power of Suggestion and Sway the Masses (2).
- **Typhoid Mary:** Typhoid Mary (A) and (B) with their Bloody Mary sides, Disturbed Psyche, Establish Trust and its Mary Walker side, Telekinesis, Psionic Slash (2), Psi Nova, Assembled Armor, Hail of Blades (2), Scavenged Bulwark and Hand Lookout (3).
- **Tombstone:** Cold as Ice, Hard as Marble, Beetle, Tombstone and Hit List. **Tracksuit Mafia:** Ivan Banionis, The Clown, Tracksuit Mafioso, Tracksuit Bro (3) and Tracksuit Mafia.

The imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged. The encounter deck holds the mission's set, the chosen Underling's set, the Standard set and two modular sets, with the Expert set in Expert; Cops and Drive are preselected for The Getaway, Cops and The Owl for Art Museum Heist, and Tombstone and Tracksuit Mafia for Stop the Presses!.

## Native rules

- **Missions:** the lobby chooses the Underling (Random picks one of the five from the game's seed). Its stage (I) is the villain in Standard, (II) in Expert, and its next stage replaces it when it is defeated; defeating the last stage wins. The mission's stage 1B starts with its printed threat per player, and completing it loses the game. The Underling's When Revealed resolves at setup in both difficulties.
- **The Getaway:** setup places 1 speed counter (2 in Expert) and attaches Out Front to the villain. Out Front gives the villain's attacks ranged, and damage the villain would be dealt removes that much threat from the main scheme instead, ignoring crisis icons. With no threat on the main scheme, Out Front flips to Alongside: each player takes 1 indirect damage and the villain 2 damage per player, and from then on the villain takes damage normally. When a round ends with threat on the main scheme, it gains 1 speed counter, and Alongside flips back to Out Front, or the main scheme gains 1 more speed counter.
- **Speed counters:** Escort Vehicle (guard, patrol) gains toughness at 3, quickstrike at 6 and surge at 9. Burning Rubber has you spend a resource or place 1 threat on the main scheme for each speed counter, Pedestrians exhaust a character you control or take 1 indirect damage. Jackknifed Tanker Truck (crisis, hinder 1 per player): the villain cannot be dealt damage.
- **Art Museum Heist:** setup attaches a random Art to the villain and shuffles the other three into the encounter deck. The main scheme's acceleration X is 1 more than the Art attachments on the villain, per player. An Art (surge) attaches to the villain; after it attaches to a character, Impossible Object confuses it, Replica Shield gives it a tough status card and Stunning Landscape stuns it, while Inspiring Pottery gives it stalwart. Each Art's Hero Action spends its resource (mental, wild, physical or energy) or exhausts your hero to attach it to your hero. After an enemy's undefended attack against an identity, an Art on that identity goes back to the villain. Art Thief finds an Art for the villain, or takes one from an identity; its boost takes one from an identity. Tug of War lets you spend 3 physical resources to take an Art from the villain; otherwise an Art goes from an identity to the villain and it surges.
- **Bullseye:** each of his boost cards gains a boost icon, and his attacks gain ranged. Bullseye (I) sets Adamantium-Laced Spine aside, (II) attaches it, and (III) finds and reveals Deranged Bloodlust, which gains threat equal to his stage. Adamantium-Laced Spine (permanent) prevents all but 3 of each damage he would take and holds the prevented damage, flipping to Damaged Spine at 6; after Bullseye schemes, Damaged Spine loses 3 damage and flips back at 0. Raise the Stakes gives him an additional boost card when he attacks and then leaves the game with a character the attack defeats, or goes back into the encounter deck. Deadly Sai: his attacks lose ranged and gain piercing; its Hero Response after you defend against him spends 2 different resources to discard it. Incessant Pursuit attaches to an ally without one (otherwise it surges); the villain attacks that ally instead of its controller, with overkill, and the ally leaves the game if defeated. Lady Bullseye gets an additional boost card for an undefended attack. Everything Is A Weapon deals 2 indirect damage in alter-ego form; in hero form Bullseye activates against you, or you remove an ally from the game. Unerring Desire becomes the villain's facedown boost card unless you remove a Persona support from the game. Their boosts: Deranged Bloodlust is revealed after the activation, Everything Is A Weapon adds a boost card and piercing to Bullseye's attack, Unerring Desire adds overkill.
- **Electro:** Electro (I) and (II) find Electric Charge with 2 charge counters per player, (III) adds 3 per player; after he schemes, 1 more (2 for (III)). As he attacks, Electric Charge spends a charge counter for an additional boost card and overkill; its Hero Action spends an energy resource or 2 resources to remove one. Drained of Power (3 uses) attaches to your identity and exhausts your upgrades; each of them readies only if you spend an energy resource or 2 resources, which uses a drain counter. Whiplash spends a charge counter when attacked to discard the top encounter card and damage the attacker by its boost icons. Charging Up's When Defeated places 3 charge counters. Chain Lightning exhausts each character you control or deals it 1 damage (3 with charge counters, removing one), Energy Overload discards each energy card in your hand or stuns a character (surging without one), and Shocking Revelation discards a card from your deck for each card you control, each energy card among them costing 1 indirect damage or a charge counter.
- **Hammerhead:** after he attacks and damages a character, he stuns it, or deals an already stunned one 1 damage (2 for (III)); (II) and (III) have toughness and retaliate. Energy Gatling Gun makes him attack the same character again and is discarded. Hardheaded gives him stalwart, its Hero Action spends energy, mental and physical resources to discard it, and its boost gives the activating enemy a tough status card. Chameleon gets +X SCH for the highest THW in play and schemes when revealed; Maggia Enforcers' SCH counts the Maggia enemies in play; Maggia Underboss gets an additional boost card against a stunned player. Consolidate Power gives each defeated minion to the villain as a facedown boost card, Power Struggle defeats the minion with the fewest hit points and reveals the next minion discarded from the encounter deck, and "Head First!" has Hammerhead attack you, with +2 ATK and overkill if you are stunned (it surges in alter-ego form).
- **Cops:** Cop (quickstrike, vulnerable) attacks the villain instead of scheming and places 2 threat on the main scheme when defeated. Crooked Cop deals its defeater a facedown encounter card; its boost deals 2 indirect damage. Wanted stays in its player's play area with its acceleration icon; its Hero Action discards cards from the encounter deck until a Police minion, which engages you, and discards Wanted. Police Cordon (hinder 1 per player) gives Police minions guard and patrol and reveals a Police minion from the encounter deck or discard pile.
- **Drive:** Sports Car, Motorcycle and Utility Van attach to the enemy with the highest ATK, the highest SCH or the fewest remaining hit points among those without a Vehicle (otherwise they surge) and take its damage until they hold 4, 3 or 6. Traffic Jam (crisis, hinder 2 per player) takes the threat a character with a Vehicle would place and loses the threat it would remove. Carjacking discards until a Vehicle and reveals it, unless you spend 3 resources of one type to attach it to your identity, where it takes your damage.
- **The Owl:** Flight Serum attaches to a non-Aerial enemy (otherwise it surges) and gives it Aerial; its Hero Action discards an Attack event to discard it. Mister Fish (piercing and ranged attacks) engages the player whose identity has the fewest remaining hit points, The Owl (quickstrike) attacks with piercing, and its boost gives the attack piercing. Swoop Down has the villain and each minion engaged with you attack you, or scheme in alter-ego form, Aerial enemies with +2.
- **Stop the Presses!:** setup puts the Daily Bugle into play and gives each player a random Daily Bugle support with 3 stamina counters (Uses); the other supports leave the game. After an enemy's undefended attack against you, you place 2 threat on the main scheme (3 in Expert) or remove a stamina counter from a Daily Bugle support you control; a support that loses its last stamina counter is discarded, and when a Daily Bugle support leaves play or the main scheme completes, the players lose. The Daily Bugle's Alter-Ego Action exhausts a Daily Bugle support you control to place a stamina counter on it. Ben Urich looks at the top 2 encounter cards and may discard 1; Betty Brant, as a boost card is turned faceup during an enemy activation, cancels its boost icons and Boost ability and gives the enemy another boost card; J. Jonah Jameson deals you a facedown encounter card and draws 2; and Robbie Robertson looks at a card dealt facedown to a player and may discard it to deal another. Each of them exhausts and spends a stamina counter. Exclusive Interview exhausts each Daily Bugle support, which cannot ready while it is in play; Breaking News exhausts a Daily Bugle support and removes a stamina counter from it, or places 2 threat, and its boost exhausts a Daily Bugle support.
- **Purple Man:** each Influenced minion gains guard, villainous from (II) and patrol from (III). Each boost card from his set is dealt to the player as a facedown encounter card after the activation. Converted attaches to the highest-cost ally you control (otherwise it surges): the ally becomes an Influenced minion engaged with you, with a blank text box and SCH equal to its base THW. Firefighter (piercing) discards the highest-cost support or upgrade in play when defeated, Judge places 3 threat on the main scheme, Security Guard discards the ally with the highest ATK, and Skater Punk deals 3 damage to its defeater's identity; each is vulnerable. "fight.", "protect." and "serve." stay in your play area with 3 command counters: each Forced Action exhausts the card and spends a counter to deal 2 damage to a hero or ally, give the villain a tough status card or place 2 threat on the main scheme, and resolves before your turn ends at the latest. Power of Suggestion makes every minion Influenced, and Sway the Masses reveals an Influenced minion from the encounter discard pile (otherwise it surges).
- **Typhoid Mary:** Disturbed Psyche puts her into play on a random side, Typhoid Mary or Bloody Mary, (A) in Standard and (B) in Expert, and Establish Trust starts in play with 8 threat per player (11 in Expert, with its hinder 3 per player). She is never defeated: each time she would be, a damage token goes on Disturbed Psyche and her hit points reset, and with 3 tokens there the players win. As each villain phase ends, she flips to her other side; Typhoid Mary (B) has toughness, and Bloody Mary (B)'s When Revealed deals 1 damage to each identity. When Establish Trust (permanent) loses its last threat, a threat token goes on Disturbed Psyche and it flips to Mary Walker on the villain, who cannot activate; after the villain takes damage or the round ends, each player is dealt a facedown encounter card and Mary Walker flips back to Establish Trust with its threat. Psionic Slash and Hail of Blades flip Mary Walker back, then the villain schemes, or attacks you even in alter-ego form. Psi Nova deals 1 damage to each character you control and stuns you; Assembled Armor takes the villain's damage and is discarded at 5 (8 in Expert); Telekinesis's Hero Action spends 2 physical resources to discard it; Scavenged Bulwark (surge) gives the villain a tough status card; Hand Lookout has guard and toughness. While Typhoid Mary activates, Telekinesis's boost deals 3 indirect damage and exhausts each character it damages, Psionic Slash's deals 3 damage to an ally you control and Psi Nova's stuns you; while Bloody Mary activates, Assembled Armor's confuses you, Hail of Blades' deals you 2 damage and Scavenged Bulwark's gives her a tough status card. Hand Lookout's boost gives the activating enemy an additional boost card.
- **Tombstone:** Cold as Ice and Hard as Marble attach to the minion with the highest base hit points with a tough status card (otherwise they surge); after that minion schemes, Cold as Ice confuses the first player's identity, and after it attacks, Hard as Marble stuns the attacked character, each then discarded. Beetle (villainous) attaches an attachment that can attach to a minion from the encounter deck or discard pile, Tombstone has quickstrike and his boost gives the villain a tough status card, and Hit List deals the first player 3 indirect damage as each villain phase begins, losing 1 threat per player for each ally defeated this way.
- **Tracksuit Mafia:** each Tracksuit minion has teamwork: when it enters play with another Tracksuit minion in play, it activates against its player. Tracksuit Mafia tucks the first Tracksuit minion discarded from the encounter deck, and after a player reveals a Tracksuit minion, that player reveals one of the minions tucked there. Ivan Banionis gains an acceleration token when revealed and after he schemes; The Clown (villainous) gets a facedown boost card after he schemes. A defeated Tracksuit Mafioso (retaliate 2) or Tracksuit Bro is tucked under Tracksuit Mafia; without it, the Mafioso stuns and confuses its defeater and the Bro is shuffled into the encounter deck. Both are vulnerable.

## Interface

- Mission setup chooses the Underling, explains the mission and preselects its modular sets; the scaling summary shows the chosen Underling's hit points.
- The objective reads "Defeat Bullseye · stage 1 of 2" with the speed counters or the Art on the villain. The main scheme's note counts the speed counters or explains the acceleration X.
- Out Front, the Spine, Electric Charge, Drained of Power and the Vehicles show their damage or counters. The Art pieces, Electric Charge, Hardheaded and Flight Serum offer their Hero Actions on the card; Wanted offers its Hero Action in its player's play area.
- Mission setup also offers Purple Man and Typhoid Mary, explains Stop the Presses!, and with Typhoid Mary chosen explains how she is beaten. Her objective reads "Settle Mary's mind · N of 3 tokens on Disturbed Psyche", and Stop the Presses!'s main scheme notes the undefended-attack choice.
- Disturbed Psyche counts its damage and threat tokens. The Daily Bugle explains its Alter-Ego Action and offers it for each ready Daily Bugle support you control. The Daily Bugle supports are captioned with their stamina counters and Purple Man's commands with their command counters, with their Actions and Forced Actions on the card. Establish Trust, Exclusive Interview and Tracksuit Mafia carry captions, the last counting its tucked minions; Mary Walker and Assembled Armor show their state, Ivan Banionis counts his acceleration tokens and a Converted ally is marked.

## Coverage boundaries

- Only standalone missions are playable: the campaign's check marks, Protection Racket, The Raft Breakout, Kingpin, Daredevil, Echo and the other Fear No Evil sets follow in later imports.
- Art Museum Heist's acceleration X counts per player, like The Getaway's printed acceleration.
- Out Front replaces damage before tough and before the Vehicles take it; Vehicles take damage before Adamantium-Laced Spine prevents it.
- Whiplash's interrupt resolves as the attack's damage is dealt, still spending its charge counter even if the attack defeats him.
- Drained of Power also intercepts upgrades readied by card effects.
- Shocking Revelation counts the identity and the player cards in your play area as the cards you control.
- Canceling a payment that a card offers as one of its choices takes the other choice: Tug of War moves an Art to the villain and surges, Burning Rubber places the threat, Carjacking reveals the Vehicle, and Drained of Power leaves the upgrade exhausted.
- Purple Man's commands enter play with their command counters, and a command with none left is discarded, as Uses requires; a Daily Bugle support likewise leaves play with its last stamina counter.
- A Converted ally keeps its owner: it returns to its owner's play area if Converted leaves play, and goes to its owner's discard pile when it is defeated as a minion.
- Firefighter and Security Guard choose among the cards that can leave play: a permanent support, upgrade or ally stays in play.
- Typhoid Mary's damage and threat tokens are tracked on Disturbed Psyche; she flips as the round ends, after the villain phase.
- Betty Brant's Interrupt is offered when a boost card is turned faceup during an enemy activation; Robbie Robertson's Response follows every facedown encounter card dealt to a player.
