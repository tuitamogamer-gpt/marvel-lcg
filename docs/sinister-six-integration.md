# Sinister Motives: The Sinister Six and Guerrilla Tactics

This import adds the fourth scenario of the Sinister Motives campaign expansion: The Sinister Six, with the Guerrilla Tactics encounter set that the scenario always uses. Guerrilla Tactics can also be chosen as the modular set of every other scenario that uses modular sets.

## Original source and scope

`src/game/sinister-six.ts` registers 27 original faces on 32 physical cards. The Sinister Six set has 22 faces: Doctor Octopus, Electro, Hobgoblin, Kraven the Hunter, Scorpion and Vulture, both sides of Sinister Synchronization, of Sinister Beatdown and of Light at the End, Heightened Morale (2), Taunting Presence (2), Team Leader, Take One for the Team, Brute Force Barricade, Frequent Flyers, High Fashion, Robotic Enhancements, Partnership of Pain (3) and Surprise! (3), 17 encounter cards in all. Guerrilla Tactics has Life-Size Decoy (2), Coordinated Effort, Hidden in Shadow, Teamwork Makes the Dream Work and From Every Direction (2), seven cards. The imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged.

The scenario uses no modular set: the encounter deck holds the Sinister Six, Guerrilla Tactics and Standard sets, and the Expert set in expert mode. The six villains have printed hit points that do not scale with the number of players.

## Native rules

- **Setup (Sinister Synchronization 1A):** one more villain than there are players enters play at random; the one with the lowest activation order value takes the active counter and the others are set aside. Light at the End enters play Trap! side up with 10 threat and Hinder 10 per player. Sinister Synchronization (1B) starts with 2 threat per player and advances at 8 per player to Sinister Beatdown, whose 2A side puts a random set-aside villain into play with the active counter and deals the first player a facedown encounter card if none entered, or in expert mode. Sinister Beatdown (2B) starts with 3 threat per player, and completing it at 7 per player loses the game.
- **The active villain:** the villain with the active counter is "the villain" for every activation and every card that refers to it. After a villain attacks and damages you, its Forced Response resolves and the active counter moves to the next villain in the activation order: Doctor Octopus places 1 threat on each scheme, Electro discards the top 7 cards of your deck, Hobgoblin deals you 2 indirect damage, Kraven the Hunter has you choose and discard a support or upgrade you control, Scorpion stuns your identity and Vulture has you choose and discard a card from your hand. The other villains stay in play with their hit points, statuses and attachments; you can attack and damage any of them.
- **Defeat and Ambush!:** a defeated villain discards its attachments, is set aside and removes 4 threat from a side scheme, 7 if no other villain is in play; the active counter passes on if it had it. Ambush! puts a random set-aside villain into play with the active counter and, in expert mode, places 2 threat on Light at the End. When a villain would activate with no villain in play, Ambush! resolves first.
- **Light at the End:** Permanent, and the players cannot win unless they escape. Removing its last threat on the Trap! side resolves Ambush! and flips it to the Chase! side with 5 threat and Hinder 10 per player; removing the last threat from the Chase! side wins the game.
- **Sinister Six encounter cards:** Heightened Morale attaches to the villain with the highest activation order value and gives it +X ATK, X being the villains in play; Taunting Presence attaches to the villain with the most remaining hit points, Team Leader to the lowest activation order value and Take One for the Team to the highest ATK, and you cannot attack villains without a copy of Take One for the Team. With no villain to attach to, each resolves Ambush! and attaches to the active villain. Brute Force Barricade (victory 1) keeps threat from being removed from the other side schemes, and as a boost gives the villain an additional boost card. Frequent Flyers, High Fashion and Robotic Enhancements put their two set-aside villains into play; for each one already in play you take 2 indirect damage (Hobgoblin), discard a random card from your hand (Vulture), discard the highest-cost (Electro) or lowest-cost (Kraven the Hunter) card you control, or your identity becomes confused (Doctor Octopus) or stunned (Scorpion). Partnership of Pain has the villain with the lowest activation order value scheme with the other villains' total SCH added in alter-ego form, or the highest attack you with their total ATK added in hero form. Surprise! resolves Ambush!, or places 3 threat on Light at the End if no villain entered.
- **Guerrilla Tactics:** Life-Size Decoy keeps its engaged player from thwarting side schemes, and as a boost enters play engaged with you. Coordinated Effort and Hidden in Shadow give each enemy an acceleration or a hazard icon; as boosts they place 1 threat on each scheme or deal 1 indirect damage to each player. Teamwork Makes the Dream Work gives each enemy +1 ATK and +1 SCH. From Every Direction places 1 threat on the main scheme for each enemy in play.
- **Expert mode:** Frequent Flyers, High Fashion and Robotic Enhancements gain incite 1 and cannot be canceled, Surprise! gains surge and cannot be canceled, From Every Direction gains surge and Life-Size Decoy gains toughness. Coordinated Effort's boost places 1 more threat on the main scheme, Hidden in Shadow's deals 1 more indirect damage to the first player and Teamwork Makes the Dream Work gets 2 more boost icons as a boost. In Standard these cards can be canceled as usual.

## Interface

- Mission setup notes that the scenario uses no modular set: Guerrilla Tactics and the Standard set join the Sinister Six, defeated villains wait to ambush you again and the only way to win is to escape.
- The villain area lists the villains in play by activation order, each with its artwork, hit points, ATK, SCH, statuses and attachments. The villain with the active counter is marked as the active villain, and a status line counts the villains in play and set aside. The mission objective reads "Escape: clear the Light at the End", and Light at the End explains its Trap! and Chase! sides under its threat.
- A long caption under a side scheme no longer squeezes its threat chip: the browser check found a two-digit threat clipped beside Light at the End's caption, which now takes its own line.

## Coverage boundaries

- Electro and Kraven the Hunter, when High Fashion finds them already in play, discard the first card at the highest or lowest cost; there is no choice between cards that share that cost. Kraven's own Forced Response lets you choose. Permanent cards are never discarded.
- Scorpion's Forced Response and Robotic Enhancements stun or confuse your identity; you cannot choose one of your allies instead.
- Partnership of Pain's activation is resolved like any other activation of that villain, boost cards included.
- Cards that refer to "the villain" use the villain with the active counter; villains out of play cannot be targeted.
- The executable registry contains 2,880 faces, including 2,442 dedicated faces, with 1,671 imported faces still unsupported. There are 57 playable identities, thirty-two scenarios and fifty-eight modular sets. The inventory pins 151 source files.
