# The Wrecking Crew native scenario

This continuation starts from `e629ea5` and adds The Wrecking Crew scenario pack, Breakout: Wrecker, Thunderball, Piledriver and Bulldozer break out of Ryker's Island together, each with his own signature side scheme and his own encounter deck.

## Original source and scope

All 60 original faces `07001a`–`07059` are registered by `src/game/wrecking-crew.ts`: both sides of Breakout, both versions (A and B) of the four villains, the four signature side schemes (Day of Reckoning, Thunderstruck, Pile It On! and Clear the Road) and the 46 different encounter faces of the four 15-card decks. The imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged.

Two printed fields are restored at runtime from the original scans, and the inventory records both corrections: Magic Crowbar (`07006`, `public/cards/catalog/07006.webp`) carries the WEAPON trait that the import left out, and Pile It On! (`07034`) cannot leave play while Piledriver is in play, as printed and as the rules insert's Signature Side Schemes section states; the imported text names Wrecker.

Standard puts the four version A villains into play and Expert the four version B villains. The scenario uses no other encounter sets: no modular set, no Standard or Expert set, and no obligation or nemesis cards, so the setup screen offers no modular choice. Each game uses 68 physical scenario cards: four villains, four side schemes and four encounter decks of 15.

## Native rules

- **Setup (Breakout 1A):** the four villains enter play with their hit points per player and the four side schemes with their printed fixed threat (put into play, not revealed). Each villain's 15-card encounter deck is shuffled on its own. Wrecker has the active counter.
- **The active villain:** only the villain with the active counter activates in the villain phase. "The villain" and "the encounter deck" refer to him and to his deck: boost cards and the encounter cards dealt to the players come from his deck. Players can attack any villain and thwart any scheme.
- **Breakout (1B):** no starting threat, 6 threat per player to complete (the players lose) and 1 threat (not per player) each villain phase. After step one of the villain phase it places 1 threat on each side scheme and moves the active counter to the villain whose scheme has the most threat; the first player breaks a tie. The ability resolves completely before the side schemes' Forced Responses it triggers.
- **The four villains:** each schemes on his own side scheme instead of the main scheme. Wrecker gets +2 ATK when his attack is undefended. Thunderball deals 1 damage to each character you control after he attacks you. Piledriver has retaliate 1. Bulldozer's attacks gain overkill.
- **Four encounter decks:** an encounter card that leaves play goes to its own villain's discard pile, whoever is active. An empty deck reshuffles its own discard pile and places an acceleration token on the main scheme.
- **Signature side schemes:** each cannot leave play while its villain is in play, so it stays without threat. After threat is placed on it, at 10 or more threat its Forced Response resolves and all but 3 threat is removed: Hard Hitter deals 2 damage to each friendly character, Gamma Blast stuns each friendly character, Pile Drive makes each player discard the upgrade or support they control with the highest cost, and Charge! makes each player discard the top 10 cards of their deck.
- **Defeat:** a defeated villain is removed from the game with his side scheme; his attachments are discarded and the encounter cards from his deck that are in play stay. When the active villain is defeated, the active counter moves to the villain whose side scheme has the most threat (the first player breaks a tie) and his deck becomes the encounter deck. The players win when all four villains are defeated.
- **Attachments:** Held Hostage attaches to the active villain's side scheme; threat cannot be removed from it by thwarting, and its Hero Action makes that scheme's villain attack you before it is discarded. Magic Crowbar (+1 ATK; after Wrecker attacks, 1 threat on the side scheme with the least threat), Ball and Chain (+1 ATK; after Thunderball attacks, 1 threat on the main scheme) and Bulldozer's Helmet (+1 ATK; after Bulldozer attacks you, one card of your deck discarded per damage) leave through a Hero Action that exhausts your hero and discards a random card from your hand. Wrecker's Command (+1 SCH; after Wrecker schemes, 1 threat on each other villain's side scheme) leaves for two physical resources. Radioactive Buildup (+3 ATK) places Thunderball's excess damage as threat on Thunderstruck and is discarded after he attacks. Distracting Taunts gives Piledriver +3 hit points per copy and stops attacks on the other villains; after your hero attacks Piledriver, two physical resources discard it. Ramming Speed (+2 ATK) makes you defend against Bulldozer with an ally you control if you can, and is discarded after he attacks you.
- **Minions:** Corrupt Prison Guard has guard. Escaped Convict has surge; as a boost it moves the active counter to the villain whose side scheme has the least threat, and if you are a hero that villain attacks you after this attack, without a boost card.
- **Treacheries:** Buddy System reveals the top card of the deck of the villain whose side scheme has the least threat (two cards when he is the only villain in play). Chaos in the Prison discards an upgrade you control or places 1 threat per upgrade on the active villain's side scheme, and gains surge without upgrades. Crowbar Toss, Lightning Blast, Pummel and Bull Rush make their villain scheme or attack depending on your form, with the printed riders (Lightning Blast places 3 threat on Thunderstruck after an undefended attack, or 3 threat on the side scheme with the most threat against an alter-ego; Pummel gives a tough Piledriver +2; Bull Rush discards a card of your deck per damage or threat). Get Wrecked! makes the villain with the least threat attack a hero, or the one with the most threat scheme against an alter-ego. I've Been Waiting For This! heals the active villain 3 and makes him tough. Mystical Link places 2 threat on each side scheme. You're Dead Meat! deals 1 damage to the hero or ally with the fewest remaining hit points and places 3 threat on Day of Reckoning if that defeats it. Energy Projectiles deals 1 damage to each character you control. Tactical Prowess moves all threat from the side scheme with the least threat to the one with the most and gains surge unless that triggers its Forced Response. Oversized Hands discards your highest-cost support or places 2 threat on the active villain's side scheme. Escape Plan confuses you, and Piledriver schemes if you already were. Uncanny Resilience removes each villain's stunned and confused status cards or gains surge. Headbutt discards a random card from your hand and deals its printed cost as damage to a hero or as threat on Clear the Road for an alter-ego. Leading the Charge discards cards from your deck equal to Bulldozer's ATK and places 1 threat on Clear the Road per card type.
- **Boosts:** Buddy System and I've Been Waiting For This! move the active counter to the villain with the least threat (the latter's villain then schemes); Chaos in the Prison discards an upgrade if the attack is undefended; Mystical Link gives Wrecker +3 ATK unless you place 2 threat on Day of Reckoning; Energy Projectiles deals 1 damage to the defender; Oversized Hands discards a support; Escape Plan gives the active villain a tough status card, or places 2 threat on his scheme if he already has one; Uncanny Resilience confuses you.

## Four villains in a one-villain engine

The engine keeps one native villain, so the scenario keeps the villain in focus native and the other three on a bench, generalizing Tower Defense's pair:

- **At rest** the focus is the active villain, so everything that refers to "the villain" without a choice uses him, as the rules require.
- **Targets:** every villain in play is a legal enemy target (with the same guard restriction), and Distracting Taunts restricts attacks to Piledriver.
- **Focus windows:** an effect that names a benched villain (an attack on him, his activation, damage, healing, a status card, a deferred defeat) resolves with that villain native. A window opens synchronously and closes once everything the effect queued or prompted has resolved; a nested window returns to the outer window's villain and the last one returns to the active villain.
- **Decks:** the native encounter deck and discard pile always belong to the active villain; the other three decks wait in the scenario state and swap in when the active counter moves. A card revealed from another villain's deck (Buddy System) is taken from that deck.

## Coverage boundaries

- The rules insert's mixed difficulty and its extreme challenge (version A, then version B) are not offered; Standard and Expert put all four A or all four B villains into play.
- Player cards whose text names "the villain" without a choice affect the villain in focus: the active villain, or inside a focus window the villain resolving there, as in Tower Defense. Effects that choose an enemy can choose any villain; "each enemy" effects that list only native enemies skip the benched villains.
- Held Hostage stops threat removal from its scheme by every player effect that the engine resolves as a thwart, including "remove threat" effects that are not thwarts.
- I've Been Waiting For This!'s boost makes its villain scheme after the current activation, like Escaped Convict's printed "after this attack".
- When an encounter card picks the villain or side scheme with the most or least threat and there is a tie, the player resolving the card chooses; Breakout and the active villain's defeat use the first player, as printed.
- The executable registry contains 2,661 faces, including 2,222 dedicated faces, with 1,890 imported faces still unsupported. There are 57 playable identities, twenty-six scenarios and forty modular sets. The Wrecking Crew product has all 60 of its faces registered. The inventory pins 144 source files.

The configured source matrix contains 103,584 cases in eight disjoint shards of 12,948. The Wrecking Crew adds 104 cases (52 expansion starter identities × two difficulties, with no modular set). Manifest hashes retain all 103,480 previous mission names and seeds; the hash of the earlier configurations was checked against the full grid of `e629ea5`. Each hero's block now holds 1,992 configurations, a multiple of eight, so every Wrecking Crew case falls in shard indexes 6 and 7, and Crossbones's 78 cases per hero split 468 or 520 per shard.

Rules references: The Wrecking Crew rules insert (Additional Setup, The Active Villain, Multiple Villains and Encounter Decks, Signature Side Schemes, Adjustable Difficulty), [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) (The Wrecking Crew errata for I've Been Waiting For This!, Guard, Surge, Retaliate, Overkill) and the original card texts and scans.
