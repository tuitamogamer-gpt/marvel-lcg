# Loki native scenario

This continuation starts from `37147cd` and adds the fifth and last scenario of The Mad Titan's Shadow: Loki, with the Infinity Gauntlet set and the Enchantress modular set.

## Original source and scope

Loki registers all 18 original scenario faces `21160`–`21176`: the five Loki villain cards, both sides of All Hail King Loki, the four side schemes (Casket of Ancient Winters, War in Asgard, Madness on Midgard and Open the Bifrost), Loki's Staff, Loki's Crown, Loki's Cape, Master of Illusions, Devious Sorcery, Infinite Mischief and The Trickster. The Enchantress modular set registers its three faces `21177`–`21179` (Enchantress, Beguiled and Seduced). All 21 faces are registered by `src/game/loki.ts`. The Enchantress cards reprint Valkyrie's nemesis Enchantress, Beguiled and Seduced with identical rules, so `src/game/valkyrie.ts` now treats both printings of each card the same way while each card keeps its own code. The imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged.

Each game uses All Hail King Loki, the 20 Loki cards (five Lokis and fifteen encounter cards), the Infinity Gauntlet and its six Infinity Stones, and two modular sets: Enchantress and Frost Giants by default (five and six cards), either of which can be replaced. Enchantress is a new selectable modular set for every other scenario, so the selector lists forty sets. The core Standard set and, in Expert, the Expert set are added as in every scenario. The Infinity Gauntlet set is always included and is not shuffled into the encounter deck.

## Native rules

- **Setup (All Hail King Loki 1A):** the five Lokis are set aside and one is revealed at random; War in Asgard is put into play (not revealed, so without its hinder); the encounter deck is shuffled; the top Infinity Stone is revealed. The Infinity Gauntlet attaches to Loki.
- **The God of Mischief:** every Loki is a stage I villain with 20 hit points per player and Victory 1. When Loki is defeated he goes to the victory display and All Hail King Loki's Forced Interrupt advances to a random set-aside Loki, who enters with full hit points and the previous Loki's attachments, status cards and counters. The defeated Loki's When Defeated then discards the encounter deck until a side scheme (stopping at the first exhaustion of the deck) and reveals it.
- **Winning:** the players win when the victory display holds as many Lokis as the victory condition: two in Standard, three in Expert and four in Heroic, as the rule insert suggests. Completing All Hail King Loki (12 threat per player) loses the game.
- **Swapping Loki** replaces the Loki in play with a random set-aside Loki: he keeps his damage, status cards and attachments, nothing leaves or enters play and nothing is revealed, and the swapped-out card is set aside. A Loki who gains stalwart discards his stunned and confused status cards (scenario rules clarification). Effects targeting the swapped-out or defeated card follow the Loki in play.
- **The five Lokis:** 21160 cannot take damage while a side scheme is in play; 21161 has retaliate 1; 21162 and 21163 are stalwart; 21164's attacks gain piercing.
- **The Infinity Gauntlet** works as in the Thanos scenario: after Loki activates against a player, each Infinity Stone in play resolves its Special against that player, or the next Stone enters play. The Stone deck reshuffles its discard pile when it is empty; Thanos's facedown boost when it runs out belongs to Thanos's cards and does not apply to Loki.
- **Side schemes:** each has hinder 1 per player; when defeated it reveals the top Infinity Stone and swaps Loki.
- **Attachments:** Loki's Staff (+1 ATK), Loki's Crown (+1 SCH) and Loki's Cape (after Loki is swapped, a tough status card) leave through a Hero Response after a basic attack against Loki, for energy and physical, mental and physical, or energy and mental; the Staff and Crown also attach as boosts. Master of Illusions resolves once per attack against Loki: it discards the top encounter card, and a treachery prevents all damage from that attack and discards Master of Illusions.
- **Treacheries:** Devious Sorcery stuns you, or if you were already stunned deals 2 damage to a hero or places 2 threat for an alter-ego. Infinite Mischief shuffles the Stone discard pile into the deck and reveals the top Stone; as a boost it discards the top Stone and adds its boost icons. The Trickster swaps Loki and he activates against you; as a boost it gives him another boost card and a tough status card.
- **Enchantress:** Enchantress finds a Seduced in the encounter deck, discard pile or set-aside area and attaches it to the revealing identity. Seduced prevents basic attacks and Attack events until an alter-ego spends energy and mental to discard it. Beguiled turns the highest-cost ally without Beguiled into an Enthralled minion engaged with its controller, or gains surge.

## Coverage boundaries

- Rookie mode (one Loki) is not offered; the app has Standard, Expert and Heroic.
- The campaign (Open the Dungeons, Summoned Back, the Norn Stones and Odin's King side) is not automated.
- The executable registry contains 2,601 faces, including 2,162 dedicated faces, with 1,950 imported faces still unsupported. There are 57 playable identities, twenty-five scenarios and forty modular sets. The Mad Titan's Shadow has 193 of its 213 faces registered; only Odin's King side and the campaign remain. The inventory pins 143 source files.

The configured source matrix contains 103,480 cases in eight disjoint shards of 12,935. Loki adds 4,160 cases (52 expansion starter identities × two difficulties × forty primary modular sets), and Enchantress adds 2,496 cases to the twenty-four earlier scenarios. Manifest hashes retain all 96,824 previous mission names and seeds; the hash of the earlier configurations was checked against the full grid of `37147cd`.

Rules references: The Mad Titan's Shadow rulebook (Scenario #5, The God of Mischief, Swapping Loki, Winning the Game & Modular Difficulty, Rules Clarification), [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) (Hinder, Victory X, Stalwart, Retaliate, Piercing) and the original card texts.
