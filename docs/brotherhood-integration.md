# Brotherhood modular encounter contract

This authored module covers exactly **seven printed faces and eight physical cards**, 32073–32079, from the original Mutant Genesis expansion. Homo Superior 32077 has two copies; Avalanche, Blob, Pyro, Toad, Mutant Terrorists and The Brotherhood each have one. Imported catalog JSON, printing codes, quantities, IDs and physical ownership remain intact. Sabretooth, Robert Kelly, Mystique and the hero/player pools have separate hosts.

References are the [original expansion](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/mutant-genesis/), its seven original English scans beginning with [Avalanche](https://cerebrodatastorage.blob.core.windows.net/cerebro-cards/official/32073.jpg), and [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf). The source review confirmed that The Brotherhood's imported `base_threat_fixed:true` is correct: **fixed base 3**, plus ordinary **Hinder 2 per player**, giving 3+2N at reveal. Its printed Hazard icon remains separate from ability text. The module creates no extra base-threat or Hinder instruction.

## Actual attack receipts

`BrotherhoodAttackReceipt` describes an actual completed enemy attack. `performed:false` represents a Stun replacement, canceled/replaced attack or aborted attack and triggers no after-attack effect. `textBlank` captures the source's text at the forced-response triggering condition. An already-triggered ability is retained when the source later becomes blank or leaves play.

`playerId` is the actual attacked player after cooperative defender changes. `targetId` identifies the actual primary character damaged, including the native Robert Kelly damage replacement. `damageTaken` is actual damage from the attack after prevention and Tough, excluding damage from a boost ability. The host must not derive it by comparing HP before and after other healing or replacement effects. `targetControllerId` records actual control before damage and defeat; it is not the physical owner. This allows Toad to trigger correctly when the damaged ally has already left play and excludes a character controlled by another player.

| Face | Native rule |
| --- | --- |
| Avalanche 32073 | After an actual attack against the player, exhaust a currently ready character controlled by that player, even when the attack dealt zero damage. Its boost ability makes the same actual controlled-character choice independently. |
| Blob 32074 | Retain ordinary printed Guard. After its actual attack damages a character, stun that actual recipient, including Kelly, using native status eligibility and limits. Zero damage does not qualify. |
| Pyro 32075 | After an actual attack, including an attack dealing zero damage, discard the actual top two cards of the attacked player's deck and take indirect damage equal to their printed resource icons. |
| Toad 32076 | After actual attack damage to a character controlled by the attacked player, discard one actual random card from that player's hand. Its boost ability performs the same native random discard without requiring attack damage. |

Forced reactions use serializable records and consume each actual attack receipt once. Avalanche offers actual identity/ally/NPC IDs and revalidates readiness and control when a saved choice resolves. Blob uses native Steady, Stalwart and maximum-status rules; it cannot redirect its Stun to a different character.

Pyro counts each printed Energy, Mental, Physical and Wild icon, including double-resource cards. Generated resources, resource multipliers and assumptions about a card's normal player-deck origin do not count. An actual infiltrating encounter card with the Shapeshifter trait contributes its printed zero. The actual discarded piece is returned by the native player-deck port even if immediate deck exhaustion has already moved it into the recycled deck. Discard-top-two stops at the first native exhaustion boundary, while immediate reset and its windows finish before the later indirect-damage instruction. The native indirect port retains per-character maximum assignment, Tough, prevention and defeat handling.

Toad delegates the random choice to the shared native seeded RNG and hidden-information tracking. An actual physical Shapeshifter card can be randomly selected from hand; its discard destination belongs to the native ownership/zone host and is not decided by this module. An empty hand creates no card and advances no RNG merely to fabricate a discard.

## Physical attachments, search and grants

Homo Superior chooses any actual minion in play. Its ordinary attachment instructions have no printed When Revealed header; the native reveal resolves those instructions directly and keeps the physical source resolving until it actually attaches. It then attempts to give that minion Tough. A minion already at its Tough maximum remains a legal attachment target: attachment and +5 HP succeed, the additional Tough does not, and the reveal does not gain Surge, as confirmed by the [earlier official ruling #134](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings/#134). Native Steady or Stalwart does not prohibit Tough. Each actual unblanked Homo Superior supplies +5 HP; blanking the recipient minion does not remove that external grant, while blanking the attachment itself does.

With no eligible minion, an ordinary reveal gains Surge. The boost ability with no minion completes without adding Surge. The [post-RRG1.5 official Homo Superior ruling #134](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/#134) confirms that the boost still contributes its **one numeric boost icon** while attaching. The host retains that original count and excludes the actual attached card from ordinary boost-card discard cleanup. Attachment does not create a new piece or substitute a different copy.

Mutant Terrorists searches only the actual encounter deck and discard pile for The Brotherhood. The host reveals that physical scheme through ordinary entry, reveal and Hinder windows, then shuffles the encounter deck. Its continuation receives `entered:boolean` captured at the actual entry, rather than inferring success from whether a matching scheme can later be found in play. An already-in-play copy, an absent searched card or a native uniqueness rejection does not count as entering this way. A true entry followed by a later leave remains a true entry receipt.

If the scheme did not enter this way, the native discard-until port finds an actual Brotherhood of Mutants minion. The same physical discarded card is **revealed**, with its ordinary entry, uniqueness, Quickstrike and When Revealed abilities. It is never put into play or recreated. This includes a Brotherhood-trait minion supplied by another modular set, such as Mystique. Native encounter-deck exhaustion/reset handling retains the actual discarded matching ID through the continuation and does not select an earlier unrelated discard.

Rules Reference 1.8, page 17, requires discard-until effects to finish when the encounter deck becomes empty: “Do not continue the discard effect with the newly shuffled encounter deck.” The native adapter stops at that first boundary, while the immediate shuffle and acceleration token still resolve. If the last discarded card matched, the adapter locates and reveals that same physical ID after recycling.

While The Brotherhood is live and unblanked, every live Brotherhood of Mutants minion gains Quickstrike through the native trait and keyword system. The grant follows the side scheme's current presence and text. A recipient's own blanking does not remove the side scheme's grant. Native entry still applies Quickstrike's hero-form condition and ordinary attack/status/replacement handling; granting the keyword to an already-present minion does not itself create a new entry or attack.

## Host interfaces and evidence

`BrotherhoodPorts` supplies actual controlled characters, native exhaustion/status handling, player-deck discard receipts, seeded random discard, indirect assignment, physical resolving/boost attachment, search-and-reveal entry receipts, bounded encounter milling and exact discarded-card reveal. `brotherhoodReveal`, `brotherhoodBoost`, `brotherhoodAfterAttack`, `brotherhoodModifiers` and `resolveBrotherhoodEffect` are the integration hooks. Native printed Guard, Hazard, boost icons and Hinder remain in the common engine.

The focused pure suite passes **70 controls**. It verifies the exact source partition and fixed threat, actual/blanked Quickstrike sources, attachment health and physical movement, existing Tough and Steady/Stalwart limits, reveal-versus-boost Surge, all four actual attack conditions, cooperative control and Kelly targets, frozen receipts and duplicate prevention, Pyro's printed-icon counts/last-card reset/physical Shapeshifter, native seeded random-discard delegation, and actual scheme search/fallback/reveal continuations with JSON reload during choices.

The native suite passes **37 controls**, for **107 focused controls total**. Native commands cross JSON save/reload boundaries, retain every original encounter instance and each original forty-card player pool, and assert physical conservation without deduplicating IDs. They cover the actual zero-damage attack and boost paths, cooperative and NPC control, last-card player and encounter recycling, seeded random discard, actual Shapeshifter ownership routing, both physical Homo copies and their independent health grants, retained numeric boost icons, fixed3+Hinder2N, exact side-scheme searches, and REVEAL fallback including the other modular set's Mystique. Two actual-source Ultron II controls distinguish his current base2ATK from the Drone bonus granted only during his own attack, retaining that grant through a paid Defensive Energy draw and nested Mystique activation. TypeScript also passes.

The shared adapter records the actual primary character's controller before native damage, then supplies the completed attack's damage-taken receipt to the native forced-response group. The side-scheme search consumes a global receipt recorded at actual physical entry, so actor changes and later leave effects cannot falsify success. Sabretooth's shared native attack continuation uses the same completed-attack boundary; its ally-defeat boost stores a serializable physical-card receipt and fires only on actual defeat by that attack.

Browser behavior, source audit, full regression and release verification remain separate evidence coordinated by the release owner; these focused results do not imply release certification.
