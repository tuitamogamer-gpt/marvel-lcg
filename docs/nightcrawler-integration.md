# Nightcrawler native integration

Nightcrawler's original `ncrawler` pack, `48001a/b` through `48038`, contains **39 faces on 59 physical cards**. This includes the unchanged forty-card Protection source deck, one double-sided identity, the obligation, five nemesis cards, six off-aspect player cards and the six-card Crazy Gang module. Imported catalog, deck and artwork JSON remain unchanged. `nightcrawler.ts` implements 22 new player/identity faces and `nightcrawler-encounters.ts` implements eleven encounter faces; the six reprints use existing native handlers while retaining their actual printing IDs.

Nightcrawler is enabled in the native roster, deck builder and all five default aspects. Kurt's search moves an actual deck Bamf! and shuffles; Rapid Teleportation pays one resource and returns an actual discarded copy with its own phase limit. Kurt's Chapel follows a real basic recovery and can draw for another player. Kurt's Cutlasses counts as two Restricted cards; Prehensile Tail increases the allowance and supplies a Wild only for an event's resource cost, including payment cancellation and actual exhaustion.

Bamf! is an optional attack-initiation interrupt controlled by its owner. It discards the actual attached card, declares Nightcrawler as the defender, applies basic DEF and runs the shared basic-defense hooks without changing his exhausted state. This includes Synch's interrupt and subsequent Defense event windows. Co-op interception changes the actual target player. The Merc with the Mouth, text blanking, attachment restrictions and current defense eligibility are checked before offering the ability. Tally Ho! is a separately paid optional response and returns the same discarded Bamf! ID, rather than an arbitrary copy.

'Port and Punch can hit its initial Bamf! enemy twice. Teleport Drop pays its actual attached-Bamf! discard before event effects; 'Port Away pays its hand discard before changing forms and readying. Scout Ahead offers the optional hand discard and another scheme after its initial thwart. Daytripper searches actual deck/discard pieces, attaches legally and deals its non-attack damage as a batch. Northstar's cost is to **deal** damage, so Tough may prevent the damage while paying the cost; the completed cost cancels only numeric boost icons, preserving boost-star abilities. Riposte adds DEF to the actual attack and creates a delayed response that can be ordered with Retaliate. Under Control and Astonishing X-Men use actual completed-defense damage, including Tough and ally defense. Change of Fortune follows the character's controller and the actual villain-phase enemy defeat.

Rogue records the actual friendly character damaged and reads that character's current traits and printed matching powers until round end. The bonus disappears when that physical character leaves play. Gambit tucks an actual encounter card without discarding it, returns the other looked-at cards in chosen order, and uses that card's current boost field for his printed X powers. Existing Moira and Powerful Punch handlers recognize the Nightcrawler printing codes.

Combine Forces and Gunboat Diplomacy pay their additional costs with two distinct ready characters, including co-op Alliance payment and current X-Men/X-Force traits. Gunboat records their actual modified THW when exhausted and separately distributes threat removal and attack damage. A printed dash is an unmodifiable zero when referenced, while exhausting a character for this cost does not use its basic thwart. Printed X powers remain distinct from dash.

## Uncertain minion costs

The host stages `48016` ("Come Get Me, Bub!") and `49017` (Squared Off) before card-play commitment. It discards only up to the encounter deck's original boundary, permits the immediate native reset/acceleration, and puts the first actual minion into play. The engagement receipt is recorded before entry responses, so later damage or Quickstrike does not undo a successfully paid cost. Event effects follow only a successful engagement with the initiating player. Counterspell cancels the effects after that additional cost; a failed cost discards the event without marking it played or granting post-arrow benefits.

The shared receipt contract is `nightcrawlerMinionCostPaid:<eventId>`, `nightcrawlerMinionCostMinion:<eventId>` and `nightcrawlerMinionCostOrigin:<eventId>`. The temporary pending receipt associates the exact initiating player and event with synchronous native entry. Pending effects and continuation data are serializable. Magik can stage the actual public top card: initiation exposes the next card, success resumes the normal play once, and failure removes the pending payment/phase lock without spending a phase use or firing after-play responses. The event's original physical ID is preserved throughout. Squared Off's Magneto implementation consumes this same host contract after merging.

## Encounter integration

The obligation and Shadows of the Past use actual owned physical nemesis cards. Azazel blocks player upgrades while his text is active; Azazel's Sword grants Piercing and offers its real random-hand discard action after the attack. Brimstone Strike finds/reveals the actual Azazel, preserves an existing minion's damage and statuses, and triggers new engagement/Quickstrike only when engagement changes. Its alter-ego branch waits for the actual scheme activation. The Day of Dimension X side-scheme reward uses its defeated-card hook.

Crazy Gang implements Queen's bounded Find/shuffle, Jester's Confuse/support discard, Tweedledope's Stun/upgrade discard, Executioner's minimum-remaining-HP attack/removal and Off With Their Heads' per-minion activations. The Crazy Gang side scheme captures the actual non-Elite minion after it schemes and deals that same physical card facedown, with normal attachment/nested-card cleanup. Find excludes facedown dealt and assigned boost cards, victory and removed zones. Tweedledope uses its corrected numeric boost icon.

Jester and Tweedledope expose `NightcrawlerEncounterPorts.discardEncounterControlled(s, id, source?)`, preserving the actual encounter source ID through the public discard decision. The merged host binds that explicit port to Maria Hill's Front Organization replacement helper. The isolated worker delegates this port to the existing scalar discard until that helper is integrated. Ordinary player costs, including Bamf!, Teleport Drop and the Sword's random discard action, retain ordinary discard ports and never infer encounter origin.

## Rules and verification

Primary sources are the printed pack and [FFG post-1.6 rulings](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/): September 26, 2024 (Bamf! while exhausted and Riposte/Retaliate order), October 21, 2024 (Bamf! is basic defense), November 19, 2024 (Tail, Rogue and Gambit), November 23, 2024 (existing Azazel), March 16, 2025 (earlier defenders versus Melter), and April 8, 2025 (failed uncertain minion costs). [Rules Reference 1.8](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) supplies the deal-versus-take cost distinction, printed dash, Find boundaries, encounter reset and ally consequential-damage timing.

The isolated checkpoint passes **291 tests across seven focused files**: 71 Nightcrawler player/encounter cases and 220 existing Mutant Genesis player, Rogue, Magik and Alliance regressions. Tests execute real native commands and payment/target decisions, including reload, original-source physical conservation, exhausted/co-op Bamf!, Synch, optional Tally Ho!, prevention costs, dynamic Rogue, non-discard Gambit, Alliance costs, printed dash, Counterspell, encounter exhaustion, Magik-top failure cleanup and a paid minion that is subsequently defeated by Hawkeye. TypeScript and formatting are checked before commit. Batch-wide regression, the expanded mission grid, browser acceptance and production verification are separate parent release gates.

The unchanged original source seed `48001` naturally reaches native Bamf! defense using public commands: mulligan, Kurt's search, play/attach the searched Bamf!, flip and end the turn. Its exact log receipt is `Bamf!: Nightcrawler defends without exhausting.` The actual attached copy moves to discard once and remains available to Tally Ho! or Rapid Teleportation.
