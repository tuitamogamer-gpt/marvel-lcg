# Zzzax modular encounter integration

Zzzax is a selectable modular set from the Ironheart Hero Pack. The immutable imported catalog supplies five unique faces and seven physical cards: Feedback Loop (29036, one), Zzzax (29037, one), Haywire (29038, two), Air Static (29039, one) and Zzzap! (29040, two). The module is appended after the ten previously installed modules, preserving their order, default selections and deterministic source configurations. Imported catalog, starter-deck and correction JSON remain unchanged.

## Native rule contract

Feedback Loop starts with a fixed two threat and its printed acceleration icon. Its When Revealed effect adds the total number of Energy resources in each player's hand and on cards that player controls. Multiplayer counts use each player's own cards.

Zzzax has printed ATK 2, SCH 2 and HP 4. Its attack and maximum health each gain the current number of Energy resources on cards controlled by its engaged player. The modifier is derived from the current board and engagement, rather than permanently changing its printed statistics or recording the entrance count. Its boost effect checks the attacked player's hand for at least two Energy resources and puts that same physical boost card into play engaged with that player. Blanking suppresses the text modifier while preserving printed statistics and traits.

Haywire attaches to the revealing player's identity. While its text is active, it treats each printed resource in that player's hand as Energy; cards with multiple printed resources retain their number of resources, and cards without printed resource icons gain none. Effective hand-resource counts and card payments use the replacement; resources generated by abilities remain separate. The attachment does not rewrite catalog entries or the printed resources of controlled cards. Any active player in Hero form can initiate its Hero Action, paying with that acting player's own controlled card with a **printed** Energy resource, or their own two indirect damage, before discarding the same attachment. Either physical Haywire copy remains independently owned and removable.

Air Static's phase-begins Forced Interrupt resolves before villain-phase-begins Responses and normal main-scheme threat. Each affected player's resource eligibility is evaluated when that player's effect resolves, using their current effective hand and controlled-card resources; qualifying players receive two indirect damage. The source must still be in play and unblanked. If multiple physical Air Static sources are present, the first player chooses their interrupt order. Any active Hero can use its Hero Action with the same acting-player printed-Energy-card or two-indirect-damage removal choice. Zzzap! deals indirect damage equal to the revealing player's current effective Energy resources in hand. It gains Surge if that player's identity is **dealt** one or fewer damage by that effect, including when the damage is assigned to allies. Damage dealt is distinct from damage taken: assigning two damage to a Tough identity still deals two, although Tough prevents all damage taken, so that assignment does not grant Surge.

Indirect damage uses native legal assignment among that player's identity and controlled allies. Assignment cannot exceed a character's remaining hit points. The complete single-source assignment resolves simultaneously before resulting defeat responses; native damage-dealt and damage-taken receipts retain the actual results through prevention, Tough and save/reload. Taking two indirect damage as a removal cost requires two damage actually taken across that player's controlled characters; prevented damage does not pay that cost or discard the attachment/environment. A fully paid removal ability still finishes if the acting Hero is eliminated by its damage cost, including discarding the global Air Static environment.

The controlled-card discard cost completes native leave interrupts and records whether that same card was actually discarded. A later response moving the discarded card does not undo its paid receipt, while Pinpoint's shuffle-instead replacement does not pay a discard cost. Printed-resource removal eligibility stays separate from effective resources produced by Haywire. These dealt/taken, allocation and ability-completion distinctions follow RRG1.8's Cost, Damage, Indirect Damage and Player Elimination entries (pages 14, 24 and 34).

## Scope and verification

All five faces are dedicated executable registrations, independent of the previously imported sixty-six Nova/Ironheart hero-scope faces. This adds a modular encounter dependency only: thirty-three registered identities, five scenarios and campaign support remain unchanged. The exact source-deck matrix expands to 3,080 configurations across twenty-eight expansion identities, five scenarios, both difficulties and eleven modules.

The generated inventory identifies the frozen catalog and engine sources by SHA-256, including `src/game/zzzax.ts`. Focused native tests, multiplayer and save/reload checks, original-source missions, production-browser behavior and publication are separate evidence. Registry closure establishes that an adapter exists; only the exercised native interactions establish the corresponding rules behavior.

The current focused native results pass twenty-eight module controls and forty-four dispatcher controls (`output/zzzax/module-performance-final.log`). The independently regenerated inventory records 1,331 executable faces, 858 dedicated faces, 3,220 unsupported faces and 81 matching source hashes. All ten tracked source JSON files and both existing correction overlays are unchanged. Inventory, Nova/Ironheart static metadata and retained Armadillo tests pass seventy-two checks (`output/zzzax-registry-static-final-gmw.log`). These results do not claim complete-suite, browser, remote CI or publication success.

The first complete Zzzax regression recorded 8,556 passing tests, one failure and the existing intentional skip; the same failure appeared in the initial remote verification job. The original `groot/risky_business/expert/zzzax seed21420` mission exposed an existing Hard to Ignore response issue: two ready copies queued offers while the main scheme held one threat, so accepting the first left an invalid second offer. The GMW adapter now rechecks the actual source, remaining main-scheme threat and native removal restrictions when each optional window opens, and again when its cost is accepted. Three new native window controls cover multiple copies and preserve the earlier rules assertions. The corrected focused GMW run passes 103 checks, and the exact original-source mission passes (`output/zzzax/hard-ignore-focused-final.log` and `output/zzzax/groot-risky-hard-ignore-fixed.log`). The corrected full regression and remote CI rerun remain separate pending gates; the initial failure is retained in `output/zzzax/full-tests-initial-source-failure.log`.

Use `npm run audit:engine` to regenerate the inventory, `npx vitest run tests/zzzax-module.test.ts tests/zzzax-engine.test.ts` for focused rules, and `npm test` for the full regression including every original-source mission configuration. `npm run test:zzzax` exercises native browser decisions, payments, any Hero removing a teammate's Haywire, save/reload, source-card ownership, accessibility and responsive layouts. CI configures that command as a separate Zzzax browser group after the shared test/build/format job, retaining all thirteen existing browser groups. Browser and publication reports establish whether those configured gates have actually passed.
