# Hela native scenario

This continuation starts from `f5fed09` and adds the fourth scenario of The Mad Titan's Shadow: Hela, with the Legions of Hel and Frost Giants modular sets.

## Original source and scope

Hela registers 19 of the 20 original scenario faces `21136a`–`21151`: both sides of Hela (A) and Hela (B), both sides of Odin's Torment, Odin's Captive side, the three Journey Through Hel side schemes (Gnipahellir, Gjallerbru and Hall of Nastrond), their minions (Garm, Skurge and Nidhogg), Nightsword, Hela's Crown, Hela's Cloak, Hela's Domain, The Queen of Hel and The Wastes of Niffleheim. Odin's King side (`21139b`) is used only by the campaign and stays unsupported. Legions of Hel registers its four faces `21152`–`21155` (Draugr, Fallen Warrior, No Place for the Living and the Legions of Hel side scheme) and Frost Giants its four faces `21156`–`21159` (Laufey, Frost Giant, Frozen and Unnatural Storm). All 27 faces are dedicated registrations in `src/game/hela.ts`; the imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged.

The imported catalog transcribed Gjallerbru and Hall of Nastrond under each other's numbers. The original scans `public/cards/catalog/21141.webp` and `21142.webp` show GJALLERBRU (Hela 7/19, base threat 3, three boost icons, amplify) as card 141 and HALL OF NASTROND (Hela 6/19, base threat 4, four boost icons, hazard) as card 142. `src/game/printed-card-metadata.ts` restores the printed name, text, boost, base threat and scheme icon of both codes at runtime, so each card's artwork and rules agree; the inventory records both corrections.

Standard selects Hela (A), Expert Hela (B): one double-sided villain card, starting on her Mystic side. Each game uses Hela's card, Odin, the three Journey side schemes and minions, nine further encounter cards and two modular sets: Legions of Hel and Frost Giants by default (seven and six cards), either of which can be replaced. Both are new selectable modular sets for every other scenario, so the selector lists thirty-nine sets. The core Standard set and, in Expert, the Expert set are added as in every scenario.

## Native rules

- **Setup (Odin's Torment 1A):** Odin attaches to the main scheme, Captive side faceup, and does not enter any player's control. Gnipahellir and Garm are revealed; Gjallerbru, Skurge, Hall of Nastrond and Nidhogg are set aside, out of play, and kept as physical cards until they are revealed.
- **Odin's Torment (1B):** when Hela would be defeated while Odin is attached to it, each attachment on Hela is discarded and she flips to her Wounded side instead; status cards stay. Completing it loses the game.
- **Hela's two sides:** flipping resets her hit points like advancing a villain stage. Her Mystic side has printed hit points; her Wounded side has infinite hit points: she takes damage, but no damage is excess and she cannot be defeated. The Wounded side's Forced Response flips her back to Mystic after any side scheme is defeated, with fresh hit points. Defeating her Mystic side while Odin is not attached to the main scheme wins the game.
- **[star] Mystic Hela** gets +1 SCH, +1 ATK and +2 (A) or +3 (B) hit points per player for each side scheme in the victory display. Her maximum and remaining hit points follow the victory display while she is Mystic.
- **The Journey Through Hel:** Gnipahellir, Gjallerbru and Hall of Nastrond (hinder 1 per player; Victory 2, 3 and 4) go to the victory display. Gnipahellir's When Defeated makes the first player reveal Gjallerbru and Skurge; Gjallerbru's reveals Hall of Nastrond and Nidhogg; each then deals every other player a facedown encounter card. Hall of Nastrond's detaches Odin, puts him into play under the first player's control and deals every player a facedown encounter card. These When Defeated abilities, Victory and Wounded Hela's Forced Response are simultaneous; the first player orders them.
- **Garm, Skurge and Nidhogg** (toughness, Victory 2, 3 and 4) always engage the first player, and threat cannot be removed from Gnipahellir, Gjallerbru or Hall of Nastrond respectively while they are in play. Skurge's attacks gain piercing and Nidhogg's overkill.
- **Odin, once free,** is controlled by the first player and follows the first player token, does not count against the ally limit and cannot have player cards attached. If Odin leaves play, the players lose.
- **Nightsword** (+1 ATK, piercing), **Hela's Crown** (+1 SCH; after Hela schemes, a facedown boost card) and **Hela's Cloak** (stalwart) attach to Hela when revealed and also as boosts. **Hela's Domain** places 1 threat plus 1 for each side scheme in the victory display; as a boost it places 2 threat if the attack defeats an ally. **The Queen of Hel** makes Hela scheme (alter-ego) or attack (hero), then places 1 threat on each side scheme. **The Wastes of Niffleheim** deals 1 indirect damage plus 1 for each side scheme in the victory display; as a boost it gains a boost icon for each of them.
- **Legions of Hel:** Draugr (guard) makes its player take 1 damage or place 1 threat on the main scheme. Fallen Warrior discards the player's deck until an ally, stopping at the first exhaustion of the deck, and puts that ally into play as an Undead minion engaged with them, with a blank text box, SCH equal to its printed THW and Fallen Warrior attached; defeated, the ally goes to its owner's discard pile, and if Fallen Warrior leaves play first the ally returns to its owner's control. No Place for the Living makes each player discard their highest-cost upgrade or support or take damage equal to the upgrades and supports they control. The Legions of Hel side scheme gets 2 threat for each Undead minion in play, or surge without one.
- **Frost Giants:** Laufey (toughness) stuns the character his attack damages, and so does a Frost Giant boost when the villain's attack deals damage. Frozen attaches to the revealing player's identity, which cannot ready; as an alter-ego action its player may spend energy and physical resources to discard it. Unnatural Storm exhausts each ally when revealed, and while it is in play player cards cannot ready heroes or allies (the ready step still readies them).

### Fixes made while validating this import

- The Queen of Hel, Draugr and Unnatural Storm print a space inside their bold When Revealed marker, and The Queen of Hel its colon; the reveal window now recognizes those markers. The treachery routing accepts the variant only for Hela's cards, so no other card changes path.
- The Crazy Gang deals a minion that schemes to its player as a facedown encounter card. A player card treated as a minion (Fallen Warrior, Beguiled, Possessed, 'Pool-ized, "Lost" Child) now goes to its owner's discard pile instead, following the ownership rule, rather than becoming an encounter card.
- An ally already leaving play through its own leave-play interrupt (the Spider-Man and Ghost-Spider allies of Sinister Motives) no longer counts against the ally limit, which had reopened the ally limit choice indefinitely.
- When the player advisor spends Maria Hill's Organizational Support for a payment that cannot be cancelled (Avalanche!), it now exhausts matching cards instead of generating nothing and leaving that payment unpaid; cancellable payments keep its earlier choice. It no longer attacks Wounded Hela when another target exists and prefers any other scheme to a Journey side scheme its minion locks.

## Coverage boundaries

- Odin's King side is the campaign's version of the card and is not offered.
- Nemesis attachments that turn an ally into a minion (Beguiled, Possessed, 'Pool-ized, "Lost" Child) choose among allies with their own rules and do not check Odin's restriction on attachments. No Hela, Legions of Hel, Frost Giants, Standard or Expert card does this.
- Unnatural Storm and Frozen use the shared readiness checks; a player card that readies a hero or ally without them is not prevented.
- The executable registry contains 2,580 faces, including 2,141 dedicated faces, with 1,971 imported faces still unsupported. There are 57 playable identities, twenty-four scenarios and thirty-nine modular sets. The Mad Titan's Shadow has 172 of its 213 faces registered; Loki, the Enchantress set, Odin's King side and the campaign account for the 41 pending faces. The inventory pins 142 source files. No official campaign rules are automated.

The configured source matrix contains 96,824 cases in eight disjoint shards of 12,103. Hela adds 4,056 cases (52 expansion starter identities × two difficulties × thirty-nine primary modular sets), and Legions of Hel and Frost Giants add 4,784 cases to the twenty-three earlier scenarios. Manifest hashes retain all 87,984 previous mission names and seeds; the hash of the earlier configurations was checked against the full grid of `f5fed09`.

Rules references: The Mad Titan's Shadow rulebook (Scenario #4, Infinite Hit Points and Journey Through Hel), [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) (Hinder, Victory X, Stalwart, Toughness, ownership) and the original card scans.
