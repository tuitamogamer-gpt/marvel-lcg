# Mutant Genesis hero source and registration contract

The imported product ID is `mut_gen`. The original canonical identities are `colossus` (`32001a` / `32001b`) and `shadowcat` (`32030a` / `32030b`). This integration covers **62 imported faces**, consisting of 16 Colossus identity/signature/obligation/nemesis faces, 18 Shadowcat faces including the two mass-form faces, and 28 player-pool faces. All other Mutant Genesis box scenario, modular, campaign and reward cards remain outside this hero release.

The two identity pairs retain the imported application's hero-`a` / alter-ego-`b` code convention. Original retail numbering uses the reverse convention for these identities: Piotr and Kitty have printed A faces, while Colossus and Shadowcat have printed B faces. The downloaded local assets consequently show the opposite identity face at their matching imported filename. `imageFor` swaps `32001a` with `32001b` and `32030a` with `32030b` when choosing artwork; it preserves card codes, face links, original image bytes and the downloaded image index. Solid `32031a` and Phased `32031b` already match their original images.

## Printed metadata and current errata

The original [Colossus ally art](../public/cards/catalog/32048.webp) explicitly prints **PIOTR RASPUTIN** below the unique title. The imported `32048` omits this subtitle. The playable metadata overlay adds `subname: "Piotr Rasputin"`; it does not modify the original catalog. This connects the ally to Colossus's complete identity for both deckbuilding and in-play uniqueness. The Shadowcat signature ally `32002` already has its correct Kitty Pryde subtitle.

The original uniqueness flags are correct for both identity faces, the unique allies, Piotr's Studio, Kitty's Room, The X-Jet, X-Mansion, Juggernaut and White Queen. The mass-form upgrade is not unique. The nemesis encounter metadata correctly gives Rampaging Juggernaut `32027` and The Hellfire Club `32057` starting threat 2 per player and three boost icons, Unstoppable `32028` a printed +2 ATK modifier and its attack reminder star, and Hellfire Pawn `32058` a boost star with **zero numerical boost icons**. Unstoppable's +2 ATK is a printed stat outside its text box and survives text blanking; its text-derived Overkill, Piercing and delayed discard do not. An absent raw numeric boost field on a star-only card is valid and needs no invented positive boost correction.

The [current Rules Reference 1.8, page 67](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf#page=67) gives Steel Fist `32008` this complete text: “Hero Action (attack): Deal 5 damage to an enemy. You may discard a tough status card from your hero to stun and confuse that enemy.” The imported text already replaced the obsolete cost arrow with “to,” but accidentally omits **(attack)**. The playable errata overlay restores that label. Armor Up `32010` already contains the corrected **would activate** wording. The Attack trait and the attack label serve different purposes; the restored label governs attack completion and status replacement, while the trait remains available to trait-specific payments.

## Setup and separate encounter pieces

Both original alter egos have a six-card hand. Colossus has printed ATK 2, THW 1, DEF 2, 14 HP and a four-card hero hand; Piotr has REC 4. Shadowcat has printed ATK/THW/DEF 2, nine HP and a five-card hero hand; Kitty has REC 3.

[Rules Reference 1.8, pages 32 and 51](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf#page=51) separates Permanent extraction from player Setup abilities. Solid has Permanent but no Setup keyword. Native initialization sets aside its one actual physical piece before shuffling and the opening draw. Kitty's Setup puts that same owned piece into play **Solid after mulligan**, retaining its ID. Later mass flips preserve the same piece, owner, exhaustion and any state attached to that physical card.

Piotr's Setup searches only his remaining deck for a copy of Organic Steel and adds the actual found card to his hand **after the six-card opener and mulligan**. It does not remove a signature from the source composition or generate an extra copy. When the remaining deck contains a copy, the resulting hand can therefore contain seven cards. The normal setup search and any subsequent shuffle preserve all 40 original player IDs.

Homesick `32025` is Colossus's obligation. His five physical nemesis cards are Juggernaut `32026` ×1, Rampaging Juggernaut `32027` ×1, Unstoppable `32028` ×2 and Slammed `32029` ×1. Shadowcat's obligation is Permanently Phased `32055`. Her five nemesis pieces are White Queen `32056` ×1, The Hellfire Club `32057` ×1, Hellfire Pawn `32058` ×2 and Telepathic Restraint `32059` ×1. Obligations enter the encounter deck; the five actual nemesis pieces per identity start set aside. Neither set changes the 40-card player-deck count.

## Timing boundaries for independent review

Colossus's active hero constant permits two Tough cards. Piotr permits one. The specific [official February 15, 2024 ruling, question 145](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/#145) says: “Only Colossus’ hero form can have 2 tough status cards placed on him. If he flips to Piotr when he has 2 tough cards, he must discard 1.” The [March 16, 2025 ruling](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/) explicitly confirms discarding down when an ability allowing additional status cards is lost, including through form change or text blanking. Generic form-change status retention does not preserve cards above the new permitted limit. Native enforcement must preserve the ordinary statuses, apply the new limit, and expose real discard responses.

Iron Will has an unrestricted Response, while Organic Steel has a **Hero Response** with exhaustion and a steel-counter cost. Each actual Tough discard supplies its own triggering occurrence. A simultaneous removal of two Tough cards finishes the removal batch before response windows and can give Iron Will two optional responses. An Organic Steel copy cannot respond twice without paying its actual exhaust cost twice. Uses removes the exhausted upgrade after its final counter and effect resolve. Current RRG page 32 also says that a Piercing attack dealing no damage does not discard Tough; an obsolete pre-1.5 archived answer saying otherwise does not override the current book.

For positive Piercing damage, the discard and incoming damage belong to the same actual damage occurrence. Finish that damage and its defeat checks before opening Tough-discard responses. The [specific archived Colossus answer, question four](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/official-ffg-rulings/#colossus), says Organic Steel can give a new Tough and **keep** it after the Piercing attack discarded the old Tough, and Piercing does not trigger again. This agrees with current RRG page 45's single response window for the conditions created by one occurrence and page 14's damage/defeat-before-responses ordering. The resulting Tough cannot prevent the incoming damage retroactively. Tough discarded as an ability cost has different timing: its response window occurs before the effect after the cost arrow.

Shadowcat's Solid mass flip is optional; Phased's flip is forced. The trigger is an actual completed identity attack or defense, rather than merely playing an Attack- or Defense-trait card. [Current RRG page 63](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf#page=63) expressly splits Powerful Punch's timing: finish its attack-labeled damage and offer the mass flip, continue the enemy attack with Shadowcat defending, then resolve the defense-complete mass flip. Starting Solid can therefore produce optional Solid → Phased, immunity during the defense, and forced Phased → Solid after the enemy attack. Starting Phased forces a flip immediately after the event's attack, so that same defense can instead occur in Solid. Quick Shift's defense-only flip waits until the enemy attack finishes.

The Phased forced flip and a surviving attacked enemy's Retaliate share the mandatory attack-completion window. RRG pages 10 and 20 allow the first player to choose their order; both resolve before optional attack responses. During nested Powerful Punch, Shadowcat can already be defending in Phased, so Retaliate before the flip can be prevented by Phased immunity while the reverse order can cause real damage. An implementation must not silently schedule an optional response ahead of the forced flip or impose one fixed ordering on these materially different mandatory effects.

Phased immunity applies only while **Shadowcat is defending** and blocks all damage then, including non-attack boost damage. Playing a Defense event while her own ally is defending does not by itself make Shadowcat the defender. The Mutant Protectors FAQ on the same RRG page makes the acting player the attack's target and the newly entered ally the defender; if that ally leaves before attack damage, the acting hero becomes the defender without basic DEF reduction and can trigger defense-complete responses. An initiated attack ending early still has attack/defense-completion windows. Stun replacement cancels an entire attack-labeled event effect after its costs, so it cannot ready Shadowcat through Shadowcat Surprise, deal attack-event damage, claim a defense from a canceled attack/defense event, or trigger an attack-complete mass flip.

Team Strike is one attack by the identity. Its exhaust cost requires the ready hero and any chosen ready X-Men allies **controlled by that player**, as confirmed by the [official July 25, 2025 ruling](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/). Allies contribute their current ATK; they do not individually make attacks or take consequential damage. The selected damage allocations form one attack against legal enemies, retain Guard restrictions unless Shadowcat currently ignores Guard, and must be saved as a complete allocation before any packet is applied. Forced defeat/Retaliate windows must not interrupt the still-pending simultaneous allocation to later targets. Retaliate comes from each surviving attacked enemy after the attack completes. The [official June 15, 2023 ruling, question 23](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/#23) confirms that piercing granted to Team Strike applies to each allocated damage packet. Team Strike has one printed “deal X damage” instruction: Aggressive Energy increases that total X once **before division among enemies**, rather than increasing each allocation. An event with separately printed damage instructions, such as Melee, receives the alteration on each separate damage instance. Chosen allies do not make basic attacks through Team Strike. Defensive Energy's drawn card cannot contribute to the resource cost already paid simultaneously.

White Queen's current RRG page 63 FAQ requires her engaged identity's confusion to replenish continuously to its legal cap. A thwart removes confusion and is replaced, then the constant adds it again; confusion remains after White Queen leaves. Telepathic Restraint's equivalent constant stun wording follows the same status application principle. These effects require executable constant maintenance, not a one-time status application on entry.

Magik's non-defeat shuffle preserves the actual owned card and its explicit deck destination. The [official October 14, 2022 answer, question 175](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings/#175), sends a player-owned Beguiled ally treated as a minion to its owner's player deck instead of the encounter deck, following current RRG page 31's equivalent out-of-play ownership rule. An actual player card representing an Ultron Drone likewise returns to its owner's deck for this shuffle; the synthetic drone representation must never enter the encounter deck. Ultron's printed Drone discard response requires defeat and does not turn this non-defeat shuffle into a discard.

The retained catalog and hero-runtime suites pass 105 cases. The mission configuration gate collects 4,160 original-source combinations across 32 expansion identities, five scenarios, both difficulties and thirteen modular sets; this collection check does not claim those missions have executed.

This contract records source and rules requirements. Native identity timing, player-pool execution, mission combinations, browser checks, full regression, build and publication require their separate acceptance evidence; a static source comparison or imported catalog entry alone certifies none of those gates.
