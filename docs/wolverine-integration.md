# Wolverine native Hero Pack

The complete original `wolv` pack has 38 catalog faces and 58 physical cards. `wolverine.ts` supplies native identity, signature, player-pool, obligation, nemesis and Lady Deathstrike rules. Energy, Genius and Strength use the existing exact Core-printing rules. The source JSON is unchanged.

The source starter contains 41 physical cards: forty playable cards (15 signature, 19 Aggression, 6 Basic) plus the Permanent Wolverine's Claws. Setup moves the same Claws instance out of the deck before opening-hand draw and into play after mulligan. The five owned nemesis cards remain set aside: Omega Red, The Carbonadium Synthesizer, two Death Factors and Tentacle Strike. Shadows of the Past reveals the actual minion and side scheme, then shuffles the remaining actual cards. The optional `deathstrike` module contains its original six physical cards.

## Sources and specific rulings

- [Original cards and insert](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/logan-wolverine/).
- [Official Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf).
- [Official FFG rulings, Wolverine section](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/official-ffg-rulings/#wolverine): “Wolverine’s Claws will give piercing to ‘the first attack’ in the event.” Slice and Dice makes two separate attacks; only the first gains Piercing. A repeated Berserker Barrage is a new attack and does not inherit Piercing. A single attack with several damage instances, such as Melee, receives the grant on each instance. Warrior Skill has the same attack scope. Aggressive Energy instead modifies the event and therefore every damage instance of every attack that event makes.
- The same official ruling explains that Claws cannot circumvent a card's Requirement keyword. The existing native ignored-resource-cost PLAY adapter enforces that absolute restriction and retains printed additional costs.
- The official Jubilee ruling confirms that its chosen-enemy lasting effect survives Jubilee leaving play and stacks when she re-enters. Native phase/target receipts preserve each +2 ATK grant; they modify only the matching Wolverine/Jubilee basic attack.
- The downloaded identity scans have the two faces reversed relative to the catalog codes. The image mapping swaps 35001a/35001b, while identity codes and source files remain intact. Colossus 35021's omitted “Piotr Rasputin” subtitle is restored from the original scan in the existing metadata overlay.

## Native timing contract

`WolverinePorts` delegates PLAY, payment, card movement, deck exhaustion, damage, attack programs, status handling, target restrictions and prompts to the established engine. Every continuation consists of JSON-serializable effects; there is no separate runner.

- **Healing Factor:** optional response at the player-phase boundary for every eligible Wolverine seat, independent of whose turn begins. It is unavailable in alter ego or when identity text is blank.
- **Claws:** chooses an actual playable Attack-trait event in hand, exhausts the physical Permanent, takes its full printed cost in damage and performs ordinary native PLAY ignoring resource cost. Cost reductions do not reduce that damage. Tough and compulsory prevention cannot pay a TAKE cost; zero is valid. A lethal cost defeats the identity before the event plays. Native first-attack receipts retain Piercing across a single multi-packet attack and prevent it leaking into later attacks or later plays.
- **Attack events:** Slice and Dice uses two independent `niAttackProgram` instances, retaining the source event for Aggressive Energy. Barrage repeats only after an actual enemy-defeat receipt, with a fresh two-damage cost and new attack. Lunging Strike's Overkill requires that event's actual Claws provenance. Precision Strike heals only after its own target is actually defeated. Track by Scent draws only from an actual positive-to-zero native threat-removal receipt.
- **Skeleton/Frenzy/I Got Better:** Skeleton adjusts actual and maximum HP, hero ATK and basic Piercing. Frenzy responds to actual enemy-attack damage and leaves through a forced alter-ego form response. I Got Better is an optional native defeat replacement restricted to damage attributed to an enemy attack; it sets HP to 5, readies if allowed and discards its physical source. Boost ability damage, retaliation and Claws costs do not qualify.
- **Warrior Skill/Mean Swing:** the native attack interrupt occurs before damage and does not open for Stun replacement. Warrior Skill pays an actual Uses counter, discards its exhausted Uses upgrade and grants +1 to every damage instance of that attack. Separate Slice/Barrage attacks open separate interrupt opportunities. Mean Swing exhausts an actual Weapon, performs an actual zero-cost event PLAY and resumes the retained basic-attack packet after the event resolves.
- **Pool:** Psylocke enters with two psionic counters and interrupts her actual ally attack before damage. Sunfire requires an actual hand PLAY, pays Energy and chooses an attachment whose effective unblanked text includes Hero Action/Hero Response. Weapon X takes actual damage, mills physical cards, stops at deck exhaustion and retrieves the actual identity-specific card even if exhaustion shuffled it. Fastball Special checks the chosen matching identity and both named characters, then uses their current ATK. Longshot checks a real discarded top encounter card's star and the surviving non-Elite attacked minion. Command Center opens for every surviving controller after any ally defeats a side scheme; actor-bound effects exhaust the correct physical support. Battle Fury resolves actual hero damage/discard/ready after a hero attack defeats a minion.
- **Nemesis:** Omega Red's mandatory initiation interrupt damages the controlled identity and allies through the native simultaneous batch before attack boosts and defense. The Synthesizer prohibits actual Omega Red defeat while present. Death Factor retains its identity attachment, damages only at that identity's turn end, and offers a basic-recovery replacement removing one chosen physical copy instead of healing, including at full HP. Tentacle Strike snapshots the existing stunned status before status placement and damage; reveal and star boost share that program.
- **Lady Deathstrike:** original Quickstrike uses native engagement. Its post-attack forced discard uses actual positive damage and the damaged character's original owner, preserved across ally defeat/control changes. Seeking Vengeance activates the actual minion or puts the actual found encounter instance into play. Adamantium Upgrades attaches only to an enemy without another copy, supplies its printed stats and Piercing, and pays all three distinct resource requirements to discard. Blanking the enemy does not blank its attachment. Hack 'n' Slash discards an actual random hand card and counts its printed resource icons in both reveal and boost windows.
