# Psylocke native Hero Pack

The original `psylocke` pack contributes 35 catalog faces and 59 physical cards. All identity, signature, player-pool, obligation and nemesis rules use native engine effects. Original catalog JSON and images are unchanged. This pack includes no encounter modular set.

The source starter has 42 physical cards: forty playing cards (15 ordinary signatures and 25 Justice/Basic cards), plus two physical reversible Permanent Psi-Energy upgrades. Setup removes the actual two Psi-Knives before opening-hand draw and puts them into play after mulligan. Flipping changes the code of that same object; Psi-Katana is not a third or fourth card. The five owned nemesis objects remain set aside until Shadows: Chimera, Interdimensional Plunder, Psionic Illusion and two Telekinetic Dragons. Both identity faces and both weapon faces already have correctly matching source images.

## Printed rules and sources

- [Original cards and insert](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/psylocke/).
- [Official FFG rulings after Rules Reference 1.5](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/), October 5, 2023, ruling 95: Flurry of Blades is one attack; each distinct surviving attacked enemy retaliates once. Repeated damage to the same enemy does not multiply Retaliate. Psionic Illusion checks each new target chosen for the attack, including its confuse choices; selecting a previously checked target does not trigger it again.
- The same official rulings, November 29, 2023, ruling 124: Lay the Trap's damage is dealt by the defeating player, not their identity. The callback retains the physical side scheme as damage source, preventing Rocket's identity-only excess-damage response.
- Psi-Energy Control is an optional Interrupt, not a Forced Interrupt. The flip completes before the basic-power stat snapshot. Stun/Confused replace their basic powers before that opportunity. Mental Detection's draw clause belongs to its Thwart ability and is also replaced by Confused.
- Chimera and Telekinetic Dragon count printed mental resource icons on all cards controlled by the affected player, including that player's side schemes in the common area. Wild and generated resources do not count. Text blanking does not erase printed resource icons.

## Native implementation contract

`psylocke.ts` reads the leaf card-definition table to avoid registry cycles. `PsylockePorts` delegates actual PLAY, typed payment, card movement, shuffling, damage, attack programs and target restrictions to the established engine. Prompts and continuations remain JSON-serializable; tests hydrate between commands.

- **Identity and Psi-Energy:** actual reversible objects retain owner and ID. Hero resource abilities exhaust the selected physical source, generate its current-face resource, and finish an optional flip before the paid effect. Body Swapped prevents Katana-to-Knife flips. Both Katanas count toward Restricted; Permanent weapons cannot be selected for the required discard.
- **Signatures:** Flurry uses one native attack program with independently selected damage/confuse targets. Mental Detection performs native thwarting and draws from current face counts. Psionic Redirect pays and declares defense in the actual enemy-attack damage window. Telepathic Suggestion only cancels a When Revealed effect on a card this player reveals from the encounter deck; entry, keywords and uncancelable text retain their native handling. Angel's ready response requires actual hand PLAY. Training Regimen searches physical Skills, shuffles and discards in hero form. The three Training upgrades attach their printed stats/keywords and pay their real discard response costs.
- **Justice:** Captain Britain's conditional consequential reduction checks the actual power target. Cypher draws only after actual attack damage to a confused enemy. Concussive Blow retains physical-resource payment provenance. Upside the Head opens only after actual basic attack damage. Float Like a Butterfly can be played under another player's control and modifies that controller's character attacks against confused enemies.
- **Lay the Trap:** exports `psylockeSideSchemeDefeated(s, physicalScheme, defeatingPlayerId)`. The shared player-side-scheme framework is owned by the integration task: it supplies 3 threat per player, ownership, global limits, When Defeated interruption and final Victory 0 movement. The callback supplies 5 damage per player with the physical scheme source and defeating actor, before the scheme leaves play. It is not also invoked by a generic encounter-side-scheme hook.
- **Basic and additional cards:** Pete Wisdom responds only after its controller resolves a treachery's When Revealed text. Directed Force requires an actual Overkill/Piercing/Ranged attack and has one PLAY per attack. Soaring Hearts 41020 and Angel reprint 42021 share exactly one provider; both named characters must be in play, Angel/Archangel hero faces count as the same identity, the physical signature event is retrieved and both characters ready. Power of the Mind 41021 and reprint 40028 double only for Psionic payment targets. IPAC pays a real encounter-card cost to its selected player's seat before that player draws. X-Bunker searches only the top X actual cards using side schemes in the victory display. Telepathy and Telekinesis require two actual mental resources before exhausting. Psi-Bow Attack is Ranged. Domino swaps actual hand/deck objects after a basic power. Psi-Flail Strike responds after defense and attacks/stuns the actual attacker.
- **Nemesis:** Body Swapped flips/exhausts both original weapons and pays a real Psionic hand discard to remove the obligation in alter ego. Chimera snapshots printed icon totals for each activation. Interdimensional Plunder counts all upgrades in play for its When Revealed threat. Illusion names a printed resource, discards the real top card and redirects only the failed target choice to a selected friendly character, then discards its actual attachment. Telekinetic Dragon uses native indirect-damage allocation and its boost uses typed mental payment or confusion.

Shared-host compatibility checks include Wolverine, Phoenix, Gamora and pacing tests. The integration task owns the combined four-pack source contracts, mission matrix and public browser checker, including generic player-side-scheme play/defeat.
