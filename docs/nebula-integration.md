# Nebula identity implementation

`src/game/nebula.ts` implements the 16 original identity, signature, obligation and nemesis faces: `22001a/b`, `22002–22010`, and `22027–22031`. Canonical identity ID is `nebu`; the pure module also accepts `nebula`. The separate player-pool module owns fourteen additional native faces and six Core aliases. Original Justice starter composition is 40 cards with fifteen signature cards, including eight physical Techniques. There are no additional setup cards.

## Rules and provenance

- Original physical scans: [Nebula Hero Pack](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/nebula/), including the [original starter deck](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/nebula-starter-deck.jpg). Imported card objects and quantities remain unchanged.
- [Official Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf): labeled abilities, Special, looking at cards, player/encounter deck exhaustion, discard/shuffle, current Old Rivals erratum and Nebula FAQ. The current FAQ explicitly allows responses to a Gamora hero/ally attack and gives an attacking ally consequential damage.
- [Archived official rulings](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/official-ffg-rulings/#nebula): an unresolvable Technique Special remains in play; a Confuse-replaced Cutthroat counts as resolved. The archive's loose “end of turn” wording does not replace the printed start-turn sequence: discard resolved Techniques after **all** Specials finish.
- Archived official Masterplan ruling: when the discarded target is the original deck's last card, immediately reset the deck, then retrieve that same target for the following instruction. Combat Ready uses the same physical-card continuation when its found Technique was the last player-deck card.
- Archived official Self-Preservation ruling: the named modifiers apply to whichever Gamora is in play, including hero and ally. Lethal Weapon can likewise attach to that actual character. Its scan has a printed **+1 ATK** icon, supplied by the shared printed metadata overlay and ordinary attachment stat handling, rather than duplicated as text in this module. Self-Preservation has **2 threat per player**, two boost triangles and no scheme icons.

Nebula's printed stats are 2 ATK/2 THW/2 DEF, 9 HP, hero hand 5; alter-ego REC 3 and hand 6. Cybernetic Upgrades is the optional **alter-ego** response after **playing** a Technique, once per round. Combat Ready **puts** a Technique into play and does not trigger that response. Gamora's signature ally response is after playing her, including legal play from another zone; putting her into play does not qualify.

## Physical Technique sequencing

Combat Protocols snapshots the actual controlled Technique IDs at the beginning of Nebula's own hero turn. A serialized mandatory choice lets the player order those Specials. Separate `remaining` and `resolved` receipts survive JSON save/reload. Successfully resolved Techniques remain in play throughout the complete sequence, preserving Cutthroat's piercing/overkill, Evasive's targeting permissions and other continuous text. Only after all captured Specials finish are successful sources discarded. An unavailable or blank source is retained; a source that has left control is skipped.

Cutthroat and Weapons Master are separate labeled thwart/attack abilities. Their own matching Confuse/Stun replacement consumes the status and counts as successful resolution, even when there is no ordinary target. Later physical copies resolve independently. Evasive is an unlabeled status ability and respects native immunity and Steady capacity. Unyielding remains if Nebula cannot receive Tough. Wide Stance keeps looked-at cards physically in the encounter deck, discards one of the actual top three, and orders exactly its surviving two; its continuation and choices contain physical IDs. A one-card deck resets normally only after the actual discard. No look operation mills or invents cards across a reset.

Lethal Intent receives the **actual paid X**, selects up to X distinct physical Techniques, and asks for their resolution order. Combat Ready recovery selects up to two actual discard sources. Choosing zero does not shuffle the deck. Neither event nor Gamora's ally response discards a successfully resolved Technique: that instruction belongs specifically to Combat Protocols.

## Native host contract

The module is pure and independently tested. Shared host integration and retail coverage totals must be verified before publishing it as executable content.

- Route namespace effects to `resolveNebulaEffect` and events through `nebulaPlayRestriction`/`nebulaEvent(s,p,paidX)`; register all `NEBULA_SCRIPT_CODES`.
- Invoke `nebulaTurnBegan` once for the actual player's turn-begin transition, before ordinary actions. Invoke `nebulaCardPlayed` after an actual Technique play, and `nebulaAllyPlayed` only after actual Gamora play. Entry alone must not call either play response.
- Apply `nebulaStats` only for Technique bonuses. Apply `nebulaNamedCharacterModifiers` separately to every named hero, ally and enemy, once. Use `nebulaAttackKeywords`, `nebulaIgnoresRestrictions`, `nebulaRetaliate` and `nebulaAttackDamageReduction` for the actual receiving/acting Nebula seat. Evasive changes both target prompts and committed Guard/Patrol/Crisis enforcement. Wide Stance reduces only actual attack damage taken by Nebula, per source, before prevention/Tough; it never reduces ally or non-attack damage.
- Register `nebulaResourceSources` and invoke `nebulaResourceSpent` for the exact Ship ID before generic exhaustion. Ship works in either form for its actual current controller.
- `discardUntilTechnique(s,continuation)` stops at the original player deck's first Technique or exhaustion. It queues the supplied serialized continuation with that found physical `id`, after normal exhaustion windows. `putDiscardedTechnique(s,id,after)` retrieves that same card from discard or the immediately reset deck, preserves ownership, performs normal native upgrade entry and then queues `after`. It never synthesizes a card from its code or triggers Cybernetic Upgrades.
- Invoke `nebulaMinionWillEnter` before Gamora enters by any method. It discards all actual Gamora allies across seats and preserves a Gamora identity. Invoke `nebulaGamoraAttackEnded` in the surviving minion's forced-response capture after actual positive attack damage taken by the real victim’s **identity** (including actual Overkill). Damage taken only by an ally does not qualify; neither do prevention, Tough, zero damage or ordinary card damage.
- Route encounter reveals, inert numeric boosts and attachment actions through the exported adapters. Inferiority Complex's optional flip does not spend the ordinary flip. Its discard branch removes as many as two available controlled Techniques and grants native Surge **only when zero were discarded**. Generic encounter cleanup discards the obligation; the removal branch instead uses `removeEncounter` for its exact physical source.
- `gamoraAttack(s,source,recipientId,continuation)` performs a native enemy/hero/ally attack without exhausting player Gamora. It preserves real attack responses, player modifiers, defender/attack windows and ally consequential damage. Queue `continuation` with `performed:boolean` after actual resolution; Stun/cancellation that causes no attack reports false. If startup cannot initiate an attack, return false. The module grants native `surge` with `sourceCode:"22031"` only when no attack was made. This retains reveal-window Surge ordering and ordinary Surge responses.
- `canDiscardUpgrade` must respect physical control and Permanent cards. Lethal Weapon removal pays the chosen upgrade cost before discarding the actual attachment. Its numeric +1 ATK remains ordinary printed attachment metadata even while text is blank.

Tests exercise independent physical copies, status replacements, failed Specials retained, continuous sources surviving until final discard, ordered X selections, card entry versus play, deck exhaustion and last-card retrieval, controlled/owner-aware multiplayer choices, native attack and Surge receipts, and JSON hydration at pending choices. Host/native engine tests and source browsing remain the integration owner's responsibility.
