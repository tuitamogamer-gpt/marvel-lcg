# Mutant Genesis original player pool

The native player pool contains the exact **28 printed faces** supplied in the two original decks: Colossus 32011–32024 and Shadowcat 32041–32054. Identity, signature, obligation and nemesis cards have separate hosts. The source data names 32043 **Attack Training** and 32047 **Aggressive Energy**. Moira MacTaggert and Combat Training are not part of these original decks. Imported JSON remains unchanged.

All physical pieces retain their printing codes, IDs, owners and artwork. Six mechanically verified Core resource aliases are 32022/32052→01088, 32023/32053→01089 and 32024/32054→01090. Four explicit native aliases are Bait and Switch 32015→15030, Perseverance 32016→13033, Toe to Toe 32046→10015 and Ready to Rumble 32051→21022. Their old hosts read `rulesCode` at the affected predicates, including form windows and Ready to Rumble's control and one-per-player limit. Bait and Switch reads effective native Patrol, including Gatekeeper, and respects Shadowcat's Phased permission; the actual printing remains 32015. Shadow and Steel has dedicated adapters for both equivalent printings, 32021 and 32050.

References are the [original expansion cards](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/mutant-genesis/) and [Rules Reference v1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf). A module receipt describes an actual native triggering condition; it does not create an attack, copy a card or infer damage from an HP difference.

## Payments and attack windows

Powerful Punch belongs to the enemy's **attack initiation** interrupt window. Its Attack/Defense ability declares the actual hero defender after native status replacement checks, retaining that declaration through the later defense step. A Stun replacement consumes the actual played event and its trigger receipt without declaring a defender. Its 4 damage can defeat a minion before that enemy reaches boost or damage steps; the native host must abort only that actual incoming attack and preserve its continuation.

Mutant Protectors and Shadow and Steel belong to the later **enemy attacks** interrupt window. Shadow and Steel requires the player's chosen identity to be Colossus or Shadowcat, and both named friendly characters in play, represented by identities or allies. Its same actual incoming attack receives lasting all-damage prevention, including overkill, before its 4 damage Attack resolves.

Mutant Protectors PUTs the selected physical X-Men ally from the actor's hand. Native uniqueness, ally limit and all entry abilities resolve before the host exhausts that actual ally and declares it defender under its actual controller. PUT is not PLAY: Magik cannot trigger from this entry. The selected hand ally is recomputed after payment interrupts, allowing an ally drawn by Defensive Energy to be chosen.

Nightcrawler is an ordinary Interrupt usable in either form. The pending enemy attack must actually deal damage to an X-Men character. Native payment spends a real Energy resource, allowing a Wild allocated as Energy, and then returns the same physical Nightcrawler to its owner's hand as the other cost. Ordinary attached-card and leave processing complete before all damage to that actual pending recipient is prevented. A return to hand is not a defeat or discard and cannot trigger a discard-only replacement such as Pinpoint. The native host retains the original damage packet, attacker and original continuation across save/load and cooperative control.

Defensive Energy and Aggressive Energy inspect actual committed hand-card IDs spent for an actual event PLAY. Generating a matching resource, paying an ability SPEND cost or spending on a non-event does not qualify. Each applicable resource offers its optional Hero Interrupt after the physical payment commits and before the event's PLAY effects. Defensive Energy draws for the actual spender. Aggressive Energy stores a bonus tied to that exact physical event's resolution; two actual resource cards can each add 1. The bonus follows the event even with cooperative contributions and is cleared at resolution end. It changes each printed damage amount once. For Team Strike it increases the total before allocation, so dividing among two enemies does not duplicate the bonus.

Team Strike's preliminary choice stages the physical IDs of the actual hero and any number of distinct ready **controlled** X-Men allies. It does not exhaust them. RRG1.8 Costs (pp.13–14) and the initiating-ability sequence (p.24, step5) require all costs to be paid simultaneously. Native PAY therefore preflights every staged character before mutating any resource source, then commits resource spending and every exhaust cost together before payment interrupts or PLAY. Canceling before PAY clears staging and leaves all characters and resources ready; Stun replacing the effect after successful PAY retains the paid costs. A zero-resource payment still commits the actual exhaust cost through native PAY. Their actual physical IDs are retained at that payment; X uses the current ATK of the hero and the selected allies still in play when the effect resolves. The allies do not perform attacks or basic uses and receive no consequential damage. The event initiates one hero Attack dividing the total simultaneously among legal enemies, using the native distributed-attack program. Stun replaces the whole Attack after its exhaust cost is paid. There is one native Attack program and one Stun replacement, with damage placed before defeat, Retaliate and optional response continuations.

## Allies, upgrades and supports

Polaris responds to any actual entry, including PUT, and can give Tough to any legal X-Men identity or ally, including a teammate's. Professor X's entry choice is forced: confuse the villain, stun a minion or ready an X-Men character. His entry ability creates a saved delayed end-of-round discard for that physical instance. Later text blanking does not cancel that delayed effect. Leaving play clears the marker, so a new entry cannot inherit an old instance's delayed effect.

Wolverine's actual unblanked attacks gain Piercing; his optional healing responds to the beginning of his controller's turn. Magik responds only to actual PLAY from hand, then pays a separate real Mental resource. Her target must remain a non-Elite minion engaged with a current X-Men hero. The native host shuffles the same physical minion into the encounter deck through ordinary leave and attachment processing; the minion is not defeated. The [official Magik ruling #175](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings/#175) and RRG1.8 Ownership and Control require a player-owned card treated as a minion to go to its owner's equivalent player deck instead. The host applies this both to an actual player ally treated as a minion and an Ultron Drone's actual owned facedown card, removes the synthetic Drone wrapper and shuffles the owner's player deck.

Protective Training and Attack Training attach to an actual friendly X-Men ally, including a teammate's, while remaining controlled by the playing player. The printed maximum of one Training per ally uses the Training trait across printings and still applies when an attached Training's ability text is blank. The unblanked upgrades provide +3 HP or +1 ATK/+2 HP respectively.

Gatekeeper attaches to an actual minion. Each unblanked copy grants +2 HP and Patrol. Its optional defeat interrupt belongs to the upgrade's actual controller and removes 4 threat from the main scheme as an unlabeled ability, before the attached minion and upgrade leave play. Multiple attached copies are independent physical interrupts. A discard, shuffle or other non-defeat leave cannot trigger it.

Colossus ally costs 1 less when the playing identity currently has Mutant or X-Men, and uses native Toughness on entry. The X-Jet's resource availability checks the identity of the actual **payer**. A teammate's ready, unblanked physical X-Jet can generate one Wild for that X-Men payer; source controller and generated resource retain their native ownership.

X-Mansion's ready, unblanked source provides an Alter-Ego Action that heals one damaged Mutant or X-Men character. Its controller can trigger that action in alter ego. Another player may trigger it only while in alter ego with the Mutant trait. Targets include actual teammate identities and allies, and actual modified maximum HP is used to determine identity injury.

## Host contract and verification

`MutantGenesisPlayerPackPorts` supplies owner-aware native moves, typed payments, actual pending attack and damage mutation, entry completion, defense declarations and native simultaneous attacks. `output/mutant-genesis/pool-host-fragment.ts` is a root-insertable adapter using existing native helpers; the release coordinator integrates its hooks into the engine. `mutantGenesisPlayerPackPaymentPreflight` validates all staged physical exhaust-cost IDs before any resource mutation. `mutantGenesisPlayerPackPaymentCommit` supplies the native synchronous cost commit; the committed receipt is checked before Team Strike resolves. `attack-paid` consumes an interrupt receipt only after payment commits, preserving canceled payment retries. `eventDefense` retains paid Defense declarations through the ordinary defense step.

The focused pure suite has **74 passing controls**, including all printing equivalences, inherited reprint host paths, saved choices, actual typed source movement, cooperative targets/control, entry timing, delayed Professor discard, payment-before-PLAY ordering, Team Strike cost/division/Stun, Training maximum, Gatekeeper defeat timing and X-Mansion actor legality. Existing Wasp/Hulk and Scarlet Witch/Mad Titan pool regressions also passed after alias normalization. The dedicated native player-pool suite adds **30 passing controls** using original starter pieces and native PLAY/PAY/ABILITY/form/turn/round commands with JSON reload at every decision. It covers actual Training modifiers and limits, Wolverine attack/healing, Magik typed payment and exact encounter/player/Drone destinations, Gatekeeper Patrol/defeat, Professor X forced choices and delayed discard, Colossus ally, cooperative X-Jet/X-Mansion, all four inherited printings, and Team Strike's staged cancellation, full atomic PAY before spending interrupts, Stun, typed-payment rejection, stale-character rejection and zero-resource cost. The Bait and Switch controls also verify Gatekeeper's effective Patrol blocks Colossus before payment, while printed Patrol blocks Solid Shadowcat and permits the same actual event after native Phase Control changes her to Phased. Native attack/defense and Nightcrawler boundaries have a separate independent host suite. Browser, source audit and full release verification remain coordinated separately.
