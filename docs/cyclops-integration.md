# Cyclops source and native integration contract

The retail product `cyclops` contains **36 original faces on 59 physical cards**. Native registration covers its two identity faces, nine distinct signature player cards, obligation, four distinct nemesis encounters, and all retail player-pool printings. `33024`–`33026` retain exact Core Energy, Genius and Strength aliases. Teamwork `33017` retains its physical code while using the installed Thor-pack interrupt. The exact Psychic Rapport reprint `34023` shares the native `33023` implementation; Effective Leadership `36021` and Utopia `36024` use the same rules as `33018` and `33020` without replacing physical printing IDs.

The original [source deck](../src/data/catalog-decks.json) is pinned to [DragnCards](https://github.com/hone/dragncards-mc-plugin/blob/11c6ee54909e01910434dfa1cd890524b760843e/src/cli/decks.rs#L747). It has **40 cards: 15 hero, 12 Leadership, one Aggression, one Protection, one Justice and ten Basic**. Its imported `multi` designation describes the four colors physically present. Cyclops chooses one ordinary aspect, with permission to include X-Men allies from any aspect. The native deck validator and editor enforce that narrower exception and retain mandatory signature quantities. Generated starters for all four ordinary aspects remain legal.

The 15 mandatory signatures are Phoenix `33002`, Ruby Quartz Visor `33003`, Field Commander `33004` and Full Blast `33008` ×1; Exploit Weakness `33005`, Practiced Defense `33006`, Priority Target `33007` and Ricochet Beam `33009` ×2; Tactical Brilliance `33010` ×3. The rest of the original source consists of Beast, Dust, Rockslide, Blindfold, Angel, Utopia, Danger Room and Psychic Rapport ×1; Danger Room Training, Coordinated Attack, Teamwork and Game Time ×3; Effective Leadership ×2; and Energy, Genius and Strength ×1.

Cyclops has 10 HP, printed THW 2 / ATK 1 / DEF 2, hero hand size 5, and Scott Summers has REC 3 and hand size 6. There are no additional Permanent player cards or post-mulligan setup searches. Lost Visor `33027` enters the encounter deck. Five actual nemesis pieces start set aside: Mister Sinister `33028`, Genetic Manipulation `33029`, Gene Therapy `33030` ×2, and Concussive Force `33031`.

The original [Hall of Heroes gallery](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/scott-summers-cyclops/) and current [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) supply the printed text and timing baseline. Downloaded catalog, deck and image JSON remain unchanged. The cached `33001a` picture shows Scott, and `33001b` shows Cyclops, opposite the imported identity-type convention. `imageFor` swaps those two artwork lookups while preserving both original source files and codes.

## Native boundaries

Constant Training searches only the active player's remaining deck for an actual Tactic upgrade, adds the chosen physical card to hand and shuffles. It is once per round, independent of Optic Blast's once-per-round limit. Optic Blast spends one resource, attacks an enemy with any upgrade attached, and consumes its attack limit even when Stun replaces the attack. Ruby Quartz Visor appears as a resource source only for that actual ability payment; its generated energy gives the same attack Piercing and Ranged. Full Blast is offered only during the Optic Blast interrupt, uses native paid event resolution, exhausts Cyclops, and adds eight damage plus Overkill. It cannot be played as an ordinary action.

Cyclops's three enemy Tactics remain player-owned upgrades, including when attached across player seats. Exploit Weakness modifies each attacked enemy's damage packet; Practiced Defense reduces that enemy's ATK; Priority Target gives the player who defeated the enemy an optional draw-two interrupt. Temporary expiration occurs at round end. Field Commander removes Temporary only from Cyclops upgrades attached to minions, and changes the first player-phase turn without moving the first-player token or changing encounter order.

Ricochet Beam uses one attack program with two separate printed damage instructions; its second target requires an attached upgrade. Tactical Brilliance performs its thwart before retrieving a discarded Tactic; Confusion replaces the complete thwart-labeled effect. Native attack completion retains existing Tough, Guard, Retaliate, Piercing, Ranged and Overkill paths.

Beast searches the entering ally controller's deck and discard for one actual resource; Phoenix retrieves a discarded Cyclops card; Blindfold exposes up to the actual top five encounter cards, discards one, and preserves the remaining order. Each entering X-Men ally opens a saved optional support-response window. Utopia can ready an X-Men character after paying its exhaust cost. Danger Room allows its controller or another qualifying Mutant alter ego to search their own actual cards and attach Training to the entering ally. Training enforces one Training upgrade per ally and modifies current stats and HP. Game Time requires a physical Training attachment. Honorary X-Men grants the trait and HP to its actual friendly target.

Dust's optional interrupt makes one attack against every minion and pays one consequential-damage occurrence with the printed extra damage. Coordinated Attack reduces consequential damage for the attacked minion, including when that minion has just been defeated. Marked grants Overkill, Befuddle offers the current THW substitution at the basic attack window, and Pinned Down reduces its attached minion's ATK. Effective Leadership records the actual ally being paid for and applies its optional phase bonus after that physical ally enters play. Teamwork exhausts an actual controlled ally without making that ally attack or take consequential damage.

Lost Visor stores the same actual Visor from hand, deck, discard or play under the obligation, blocks Cyclops attacks, and returns that piece when Scott pays the printed exhaust cost. Mister Sinister uses native Stalwart, Toughness and Villainous and his printed boost replacement. Genetic Manipulation searches for and reveals actual Gene Therapy; Gene Therapy chooses among enemies with the lowest printed ATK, gives its actual attack Piercing and Overkill, and discards at that attack's end. Concussive Force activates the actual Sinister when present or resolves its form-dependent fallback.

## Verification

[The native suite](../tests/cyclops-native.test.ts) checks physical source quantities, all four aspect compositions, face artwork, actual paid actions, physical-card movement across JSON reloads, multiplayer ownership, enemy upgrades, ally responses, pack reprints and nemesis encounters. It runs through public commands and the native dispatcher. The independent boundary suite is kept separately. Combined release build, full regression, browser and deployment evidence belong to the four-pack release, not to this source contract.
