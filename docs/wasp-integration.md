# Wasp native integration — 7 October 2026

Wasp's retail pack now has executable support for all **36 printed faces**: 18 native identity/signature/nemesis registrations, 13 native pack registrations, four exact Core reprints and one compiled Boot Camp rule. Nadia Van Dyne, Tiny Wasp and Giant Wasp are three printed faces of one identity. The canonical runtime identity and catalog set are `wsp`.

The 40-card Aggression source preconstructed list retains its original `13` printing codes, quantities and actual physical card IDs. Shared rules reuse existing implementations without replacing those printings. This includes Swarm Tactics and Quincarrier, the Core resources and The Power of Aggression. The signature Ant-Man is Scott Lang; the pack's aggression Wasp is Janet Van Dyne. Identity, ally and uniqueness checks preserve these distinct characters.

## Forms, basic powers and timing

`player.form` remains `hero` or `alter`; `player.heroForm` records `tiny` or `giant`. Nadia has REC 3 and hand size 6. Tiny has THW 1, ATK 1, DEF 2 and hand size 5; Giant has THW 2, ATK 2, DEF 3 and hand size 5. All three faces share 11 hit points. Printed identity code, traits, stats and the board face follow the selected form. Voluntary changes offer the other two forms, support cancellation and retain the pending choice through JSON reload. Effect-driven changes do not spend the normal voluntary form change.

Giant's basic attack and thwart, Giant Help and Giant Wasp Sting collect a complete allocation before applying it. The legal target snapshot retains initial Guard, Patrol and Crisis restrictions. Defeating one of those blockers does not open the villain or main scheme within the same divided ability. Each target receives its assigned packet as part of one simultaneous ability. All attack damage is placed before defeat interrupts resolve; each surviving attacked enemy's Retaliate then has its native aftermath window. Wasp's player chooses the order of the divided attack's retaliation, including when Wasp is the second player. Threat removal likewise commits the simultaneous batch before side-scheme defeat responses.

Rapid Growth is a paid interrupt to an actual basic ATK, THW or DEF use. Stunned or Confused replaces an attack or thwart before this interrupt becomes available. Its form change and +2 are independent effects: an already Giant identity still gets +2, and a Tiny identity prevented from changing form gets +2 while remaining Tiny. Current form statistics and upgrades are recomputed after the change. Both physical copies can add their bonuses to the same use; the bonus does not persist into another use. Shared basic-power interrupts contribute their own native additive modifiers. Choosing Rapid Growth commits the interrupt; its resulting payment cannot be canceled, and rejecting cancellation leaves the card, resources, form and unresolved bonus intact.

Small but Mighty is optional after an actual minion or side-scheme defeat while Wasp is Tiny. It attributes the defeat to Wasp, a played event belonging to her player, or an applicable controlled upgrade acting as an extension of her identity. Ally and support defeats do not qualify, nor does another player's event. Its damage to the villain is nonattack damage and waits for the attack's mandatory aftermath when appropriate.

## Signatures and retail cards

G.I.R.L. uses a saved native selection of zero, one or two actual discarded cards with a printed mental resource. Wild resources alone do not qualify. It moves those same instances into Nadia's normal deck, shuffles through the native hidden-information boundary and records the once-per-round use. No replacement card or duplicate printing is created.

Pinpoint Strike deals eight damage with Overkill in Tiny form and seven otherwise. Wasp Sting uses five damage against one enemy in Tiny form and four divided damage in Giant form. Giant Help uses three threat removal against one scheme outside Giant form and four divided threat removal in Giant form. Each keeps native status replacement and target legality.

Red Room Training gives Tiny basic attacks Piercing and gives Giant Wasp Retaliate 1. Event attacks do not acquire its basic-attack Piercing. Bio-Synthetic Wings grants Wasp Aerial and, in Tiny form, can exhaust to prevent one positive damage packet, including direct damage and ally Overkill. Tough has priority and prevents the damage without exhausting Wings. Tech Theft blanks Wings' granted Aerial and prevention, plus Wasp's Helmet's form bonuses, while retaining printed traits, icons and card identity. Pym Particles opens an optional response for each actual spent copy in the applicable hero form. Wasp's Helmet and the Ant-Man ally use the controller's current size for their printed bonuses.

Thor's hand-play response examines the actual resources paid. Janet's Wasp counts only excess energy resources, to her printed counter cap. Ironheart's draw requires play from hand. Miles's chosen basic-power bonus expires at the phase boundary. Into the Fray uses actual attack excess damage for its main-scheme removal. Surprise Attack and Perseverance use the shared form-change response window; Lie in Wait uses the actual minion-engagement window and discards its physical source as the cost. Boot Camp respects its per-player limit and controller, and The Power in All of Us doubles only while paying for a Basic card. Supplementary Running Interference, All for One and Athletic Conditioning use native trait, exhaustion and physical-status rules.

## Obligation and nemesis

Red Dreams routes to Nadia's player and offers its printed alter-ego change. Its exhaustion branch removes the actual obligation from the game. Its other branch discards every hand card with a printed mental resource and then deals one damage, preserving wild-only cards and the native damage-prevention window.

Mother's Orders adds a real resource cost to every hero's basic attack. The additional payment occurs before exhaustion, so canceling that payment does not exhaust the identity. Successful payment commits exhaustion before spent-resource responses open, including Pym Particles. Status replacement still requires paying the basic attack's costs.

Beetle retains Guard. Its forced defeat interrupt follows the Rules Reference erratum: **the player who defeated Beetle** chooses and pays the physical resource, or shuffles that same actual Beetle into the encounter deck. Native defeat processing still discards attachments and opens eligible defeat responses; the shuffle does not copy Beetle or remove the next encounter card. Beetle Armor MK IV attaches to Beetle when possible and otherwise the villain, granting four hit points until the attachment leaves play. Its villain HP adjustment persists through saves and is reversed on removal. Beetle Mania adds one ATK to Beetle's actual attack and gains Surge if no attack occurs, including status replacement; its alter-ego branch gains Surge.
