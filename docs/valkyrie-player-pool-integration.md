# Valkyrie supplementary player-card contract

The 19 original supplementary faces are `25013`–`25027` and `25033`–`25036`. Fourteen have dedicated actual-code adapters in `src/game/valkyrie-pack.ts`: Thor, Throg, Angela, Hall of Heroes, Quick Strike, Smash the Problem, The Best Defense…, Audacity, The Bifrost, Godlike Stamina, Problem Solvers, Leadership Training, Anticipation and Cosmic Alliance. The five mechanically equal Core reprints are Combat Training `25017`→`01057`, The Power of Aggression `25022`→`01055`, and Energy/Genius/Strength `25025`–`25027`→`01088`–`01090`.

Angela `25015`, Hall of Heroes `25016` and Audacity `25021` remain dedicated adapters for their original physical codes. No runtime mutation rewrites the source face, adds a resource or changes its printed stats. Audacity is one wild, Thor is 3 ATK with two consequential attack icons, and the source starter remains 41 physical cards including its counted Death-Glow signature.

This records the pure module, native port contract and independently tested engine integration. Browser and release checks remain separate validation requirements.

## Host integration

Register the stable `VALKYRIE_PACK_SCRIPT_CODES` and `VALKYRIE_PACK_CORE_ALIASES` exports. Route `valkyrie-pack:` effects through `resolveValkyriePackEffect`. Add ordinary card-entry, ally-entry, actual SPEND, identity-attributed minion defeat, play restriction, ability, event, hero defense, minion engagement and ally attack hooks. Do not restrict the pool to Valkyrie; any legal deck may use these supplementary cards.

- `valkyriePackCardEntered` initializes Hall with zero glory and Leadership Training with two Uses counters. Training's last use discards the physical support through normal leave-play cleanup.
- `valkyriePackAllyEnter` handles Throg's optional Tough response after either PLAY or PUT, only if a minion is engaged with his actual controller. Generic native Toughness handles Thor. Angela's forced response searches the actual top ten encounter cards, selects an actual legal minion and PUTs it into play engaged with her controller. Run native engagement, entry and Quickstrike, without revealing that minion. Always shuffle the encounter deck; if no minion enters this way, discard the same Angela. Hidden searches may fail voluntarily, and entry restrictions remain authoritative.
- Hall of Heroes' glory belongs to the identity that defeated the minion, including identity-attributed nonattack damage. Ally/support defeats and discard/defeat replacements do not award glory. Optional glory is recorded only when its response is chosen. Its AE action commits exhaustion and three actual glory before native draw 3.
- `valkyriePackResourcesSpent` handles Audacity only for an actual spent `25021` while its payer is in Hero form. Its 1 villain damage is not an attack and does not consume Stun or Confuse. Preserve the printed SPEND response on legal overpayment, including a zero-cost card's optional resource payment. Use the actual Alliance contributor's actor ID, not the event player's actor. Generated resources and spending this card are separate concepts.
- The Bifrost may enter only while the identity has Asgard. Its later Action has no printed form restriction: exhaust it before searching, then select a legal payable Asgard ally in the actual deck. The `playDeckAlly` port performs real paid PLAY from the **deck**, not PLAY from hand and not PUT. Apply uniqueness, actual discounts, card resources, ally limit, source-sensitive PLAY/entry windows and cancellation. The selected ally remains in its deck until play commits. Complete the saved shuffle on success, failed/declined search and payment cancellation; never refund Bifrost's already paid exhaustion. Official FFG specifically confirms Jane Foster's “played from your hand” response does not trigger from Bifrost.
- Quick Strike is a Hero attack event using current ATK against its actual selected enemy. Include target-conditioned ATK such as Dragonfang's marked-enemy increment; do not exhaust the hero or treat this as a basic attack. Native Guard, cannot-attack restrictions, damage prevention, Tough, defeat and Retaliate still apply. Stun replaces the attack once before targeting.
- Smash the Problem pays real hero exhaustion before resolving its thwart or Confuse replacement. Snapshot ordinary ATK, without Dragonfang's additional “while attacking” increment. Its emitted native thwart packet has `action:true`, `basic:false` and `thwartInitiated:true` so actual hero-thwart responses resolve. Crisis/Patrol still restrict the scheme.
- Problem Solvers is printed Alliance. Resource contributions may come from any surviving player. Its additional arrow cost exhausts two **distinct** ready characters: an Avenger and a Guardian. The characters can belong to different players and may be qualifying alter-egos, whose unprinted THW is zero. Snapshot their combined current THW when the exhaustion cost commits; Confuse replaces the entire thwart only after these costs are paid. Use the `thwartBatch` port for one simultaneous thwart against the legal initial scheme snapshot: commit all threat removal before scheme defeat/optional response windows. A Crisis scheme removed by this same batch does not retroactively make the initially blocked main scheme eligible. Preserve ordinary thwart attribution and mandatory defeat windows.
- Cosmic Alliance is also printed Alliance. It chooses distinct Avenger and Guardian characters, can use other players' characters and qualifying alter-egos, and readies each that can ready. One partner may already be ready if the other receives a real ready benefit; a single dual-trait character cannot fill both selections. Native ready locks remain effective. It is not an attack or thwart and does not consume Stun/Confuse.
- Godlike Stamina is an Action in either form, requiring Asgard only when played. Heal 2 through the actual identity dial before offering its optional status discard. Discard exactly **one actual status card** chosen from Stun, Confuse or Tough; with Steady, removing one of two Stun/Confuse cards leaves one card and clears the active status boolean. A full-health identity may play it to remove a status, but a full-health, status-free identity has no playable effect. The player may keep all status cards.
- The Best Defense… is offered only when the Hero identity actually defends against the current attack, including a defense-event declaration such as Shieldmaiden. Pay/resolve the actual event before its substitution. `useAttackForDefense` persists for that attack and recalculates current **ordinary, nonattacking ATK** after boost changes. Replace DEF completely, including Spear's target-based DEF and event DEF modifiers; do not add Dragonfang's “while attacking” increment. Do not exhaust again or turn an undefended/ally-defended attack into a hero defense.
- Anticipation is an optional Hero Interrupt on an actual new minion engagement with its controller, including native entry/PUT and engagement transfer. It pays its own actual discard before readying that hero. Require a real ready benefit and honor native ready/discard prohibitions and blanking. An already engaged minion remaining with the same player does not create a new engagement trigger.
- Thor's printed interrupt is offered when his one actual ally attack attacks a minion. It spends one actual energy resource (wild may satisfy it) through native ability-cost payment. Select the order of actual minions engaged with that target's player; no other player's minion or later entrant is added to the saved snapshot. `allyAttackBatch` resolves the **same one attack** against those targets in the chosen order, preserving native ATK/keywords, per-target damage, Tough and defeat windows. Retaliate resolves for each surviving attacked enemy **after the complete attack**, per Rules Reference 1.8 p. 10; it cannot prevent the attack's later ordered damage packets. Pay Thor's two consequential damage once after the attack/Retaliate/response windows, rather than once per minion. Stun replacement happens before offering this actual attack interrupt, so its paid resource is not a workaround for Stun.

For Smash/Problem, the additional-cost receipt is `valkyriePackCostPaid:true` plus `valkyriePackReceipt.amount` on the resumed native event. Resource and arrow costs must commit before the attack/thwart benefit or status replacement. Keep actual payment cancellation atomic through the host; a pending choice is not a grant to skip its physical costs.

Pure effects use the engine's actual protocol: `draw.amount`, ordinary nonattack `damage`, and `thwart.action` only for a real thwart attempt. Every saved choice contains actual IDs and actor ownership; JSON hydration must preserve these before resuming.

## Native engine evidence

`tests/valkyrie-pack-engine.test.ts` has **28 passing native tests**. Each command reloads ordinary JSON before dispatch, traverses actual payment and review windows, and checks that every original player source ID remains exactly once with its original owner. The Valkyrie source contains 41 counted cards; setup sets aside its same Death-Glow, leaving 40 playing cards. Separately created nemesis reserves are not counted as imported source cards.

The native cases cover actual Throg PLAY/Tough, Angela's actual top-ten PUT without When Revealed, Hall's glory and three-card draw, target-conditioned Quick Strike versus ordinary-ATK Smash, arrow costs before Confuse, Bifrost paid deck PLAY and cancellation shuffle, actual status-card removal, both original and legacy Audacity SPEND attribution, Anticipation before entry Quickstrike and on existing-minion transfer, final Training Use cleanup, multiplayer Alliance resources and trait-qualified alter-ego THW zero. Problem Solvers commits every legal scheme before its actual Turn the Tide response and offers Precision to the event-playing Gamora. The Best Defense survives boosts and recalculates current ordinary ATK after a physical modifier leaves. Thor resolves ordered targets before surviving enemies' Retaliate and pays consequential damage once; Stun prevents its interrupt entirely.

On 2026-10-08, the pool's **28 native + 54 pure = 82 tests passed**. Running these with Valkyrie's **18 native + 55 pure identity tests** and **31 native Thor regressions** gave **186 passing tests**:

```sh
npx vitest run tests/valkyrie-pack-engine.test.ts tests/valkyrie-pack.test.ts tests/valkyrie-engine.test.ts tests/valkyrie.test.ts tests/thor-engine.test.ts --maxWorkers=2
```

Independent engine and saved-deck starter checks also passed for all **112 hero/aspect combinations each**, using the printed source starter size rather than a universal forty-card expectation. TypeScript validation: `npx tsc --noEmit` passed.
