The Leadership source is exactly **40 playing cards: 15 identity-specific, 17 Leadership and eight Basic**. Its obligation and five physical nemesis cards retain their original codes; the five-face/five-card Hellfire module is separately selectable. This hero lives in `magneto-hero.ts`, independently of the existing Magneto villain in `magneto.ts`. Imported catalog, source-deck and artwork bytes remain unchanged.

The original English scans and [Magneto gallery](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/magneto/) establish the printed source. The ordinary effect uses an actual discard followed by its gated continuation; it never defeats the minion.

## Physical cards, timing and ownership

The shuffle and facedown encounter deal occur normally, and the recovered ID appears in hand exactly once. The November8,2024 [FFG answer33](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/) distinguishes initiating Pull from resolving it: a failed search still spends the round limit but offers no Armor/Cape responses. Old Grievances says “use,” so it counts the cards actually discarded on that attempt. Armor reads printed Mental/Physical/Energy presence, including multiple icons on the found card; Wild is not a substitute during this non-payment check. The player orders eligible Armor and Cape responses. Survivor shuffles the actual top three discard cards after the form change.

Helmet's native resource ability is restricted to Magnetic payment targets. Its Steady and Cape's Aerial are upgrade-granted constants and survive blanking of the identity. Its forced interrupt, the optional responses, Survivor and Helmet retain Superpower Feedback timing. A Bubble that would replace a TAKE-damage cost makes that cost unavailable. Wrapped in Metal blanks the actual non-Elite minion and prevents its activation before status replacement or boosts.
