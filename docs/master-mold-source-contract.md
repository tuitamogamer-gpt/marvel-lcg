# Master Mold standalone original-source contract

This import adds **15 English retail faces representing 19 physical box cards**: fourteen Master Mold faces (`32109`–`32120`, with both main-scheme reverses) on eighteen cards, and the required **Magneto ally `32172b`** on one shared card. The ally is a standalone setup dependency even though its printed classification is Campaign. Its other face, **Enemy of My Enemy `32172a`**, remains unsupported campaign content. This import does not automate the Mutant Genesis campaign, Find the Prisoners, Future Past, role cards, Mansion Attack, or the Magneto villain scenario.

The evidence is the [original Mutant Genesis gallery](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/mutant-genesis/), all fifteen original English faces in `public/cards/catalog`, the [original rulebook, page 11](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/mutant-genesis-rulebook.pdf#page=11), and [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf). Every imported face was visually inspected. No discrepancy requiring a printing-metadata correction was identified for these fifteen faces. The Master Mold shuffled portion is **13 cards**: two each of `32114`–`32118`, one `32119`, and two `32120`. The three villain stages and two main schemes are staging pieces. Required Sentinels adds seven cards and recommended Zero Tolerance adds seven. Including Core Standard's seven and one obligation per player, the recommended setup begins with **34 + N** encounter pieces in Standard or **37 + N** in Expert. Main-scheme setup moves some of those actual pieces into encounter discard and engaged-minion areas, so the remaining deck length depends on the shuffle. A fixed post-setup deck length would be incorrect.

## Required sets and setup

Main 1A and the original rulebook require **Sentinels**, plus exactly **one replaceable modular slot**, recommended **Zero Tolerance**. Sentinels cannot also occupy the selectable slot. Zero Tolerance is shuffled normally: this scenario does not reveal Operation Zero Tolerance at setup or grant it Permanent, and it does not inherit Wideawake's optional setup captures. Campaign instructions on the rulebook's right column are outside this standalone import.

Under RRG 1.8 Appendix II, page 51, main 1A puts the actual Magneto ally into play under the first player's control before main 1B resolves its When Revealed ability and before opening hands. Each player discards encounter cards until they discard a Sentinel minion, then puts that same minion into play engaged with them. RRG “Each Player,” page 16, lets the first player choose the order in which players resolve that instruction. Both main stages give all Sentinel minions Guard while their text is active. The grant does not apply to Master Mold itself or non-Sentinel minions, and the player need only clear Guard minions engaged with them before attacking the villain.

On advancing the main scheme, resolve 2A's explicit encounter-discard shuffle, then its each-player discard/put-into-play instruction, then proceed with 2B. Its starting threat is N; 8N completion loses. Villain-stage progression applies the next stage's Toughness and Stalwart, with no invented When Revealed ability.

Putting a minion into play is **not revealing it** (RRG pages 34 and 38). Do not resolve a When Revealed ability such as Sentinel Mark V's attachment search. Entry keywords and engagement triggers still resolve: Toughness, Quickstrike against a hero, and Mark VIII's engagement Forced Response. Initial setup identities are alter-egos, so Quickstrike normally has no attack at that point. [Official answer 281, February 13, 2023](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings/#281) specifically confirms that Mark VIII's Forced Response resolves when it engages during setup.

## Discarding, scheming and physical cards

Master Mold's Forced Interrupt occurs when it actually schemes against a player. Discard encounter cards until a Sentinel minion is discarded, put that same card into play engaged with that player, and do not give Master Mold a new boost card for the activation. [Official answer 278, February 13, 2023](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings/#278) explicitly confirms the no-new-boost instruction still applies if no Sentinel was discarded. Already assigned boost cards remain and resolve normally: RRG “Boost,” page 11, retains cards dealt outside an activation until the enemy activates, and “Scheme,” page 39, separately instructs giving a card and resolving each assigned card. The spawned minion participates in the later minion-activation step of the normal villain phase, as the original rulebook states. A separate treachery-induced villain scheme does not create an extra normal minion-activation step.

RRG “Encounter Deck,” page 16, bounds a discard-until effect at the encounter deck's current exhaustion: immediately shuffle the discard and add an acceleration token, but **do not continue the same discard effect in the new deck**. If the final discarded card satisfies the condition, the subsequent put-into-play instruction still moves that exact piece out of its current zone. It cannot also remain in the reshuffled deck. If no matching card was discarded, no minion is created. With each-player instructions, after one player's discard stops at exhaustion, the remaining players use the reset deck. If both encounter deck and discard are genuinely empty, RRG's empty-encounter-pool loss applies rather than an infinite loop.

The current [Armor Up erratum in RRG 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) uses “would activate.” This window precedes Master Mold's “when ... schemes” interrupt: switching to hero before its activation is determined produces an attack with its normal boost and no scheme mill. [Official answer 208, November 17, 2022](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings/#208) corrects the older timing explanation. A replaced or canceled scheme is not a completed scheme receipt.

## Attachments and attack timing

Mark VIII attaches the **topmost Sentinel attachment** from the encounter discard after it engages, including during setup and after engagement changes. Moving that exact attachment directly onto the specified minion bypasses the attachment's “attach to” phrase (RRG page 8). Thus Energy Barrier gives no Tough through this route, and Stun Beam's ordinary “without Stun Beam attached” entry restriction does not exclude an already attached copy. The engine must not skip the topmost matching attachment merely to find an attachment whose ordinary reveal instruction could resolve. [Official post-1.6 answers 53 and 66](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/#53) expressly confirm the Energy Barrier distinction.

Ordinary Unit Upgrade can attach to any Sentinel minion or Surge if none exists. Its boost also attaches the same physical card to a Sentinel minion; it does not invent Surge from the ignored ordinary text. Its printed +1 SCH and +1 ATK remain when the text box is blanked. The text's +2 HP and Retaliate 1 disappear when blanked; preserve existing damage when maximum HP changes. RRG “Keywords,” page 25, adds the numbers across multiple instances of a numeric keyword, explicitly including Retaliate X. Duplicate Unit Upgrades or Learning A.I. therefore produce one aggregate Retaliate value, rather than separate damage instances. Ordinary Stun Beam chooses a Sentinel minion without any physical Stun Beam already attached, even if that existing copy is blanked; otherwise Surge. Its +1 ATK is printed outside the text box.

Stun Beam's Forced Response requires that the minion's actual attack damage a character. Tough, full prevention, a zero-damage attack, or damage from a separate boost ability does not satisfy that trigger. RRG page 9 gives Sonic Converter's identical “attacks and damages a character” wording as the specific example excluding separate boost damage. The [official June 17, 2025 clarification](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/) includes **Overkill damage**: a character damaged by the attack qualifies even if it was not the attack's target. Resolve the response after the completed attack and only for still-valid characters.

## Treacheries, side schemes and Magneto

Master Mold's Children activates **every minion engaged with the resolving player**, including non-Sentinels, as a scheme in alter-ego or an attack in hero. Only if that player has no engaged minion does Master Mold perform the appropriate activation. Shields Up gives each Sentinel minion engaged with that player Tough. Its Otherwise Surge applies when no Tough can be given, including when every eligible minion already has Tough (RRG “Otherwise,” page 31). Its boost instead gives the villain Tough; the ordinary reveal text is ignored.

Intruder Alert!'s When Defeated belongs to the player who actually defeated the scheme, including through one of their allies or non-thwart threat removal. Resolve the discard/engagement for that player rather than assuming the first player or currently displayed seat. Insert Virus Program starts at **2 + 2N**, and its When Defeated deals 2 unlabelled damage to each Sentinel enemy, including the active Master Mold. This is not an attack and therefore ignores Guard and does not trigger Retaliate. Its Victory keyword sends the same defeated physical side scheme to the victory display rather than encounter discard (RRG page 46).

Magneto is an ordinary usable ally after scenario setup, follows the first-player token without leaving play, and ignores the ally limit while his text is active. His Response is optional, is not restricted to hero form, and heals 1 only after **his actual attack defeats a Sentinel minion**. Defeating another enemy, a separate ability's damage, or merely losing the target from play is insufficient. RRG page 10 places forced after-attack abilities first, ordinary after-attack Responses next, and consequential damage last. A Magneto with one remaining HP can therefore accept the heal, rise to two remaining HP, then survive consequential damage with one HP; declining the heal permits normal defeat.

His printed Victory 1 provides the normal When Defeated victory destination, with no ordinary discard for Regroup or a discard interrupt to replace. [Official answer 216, December 1, 2022](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings/#216) explicitly names **both Jubilee and Magneto**: after an enemy attack defeats them, they enter Victory first, then move immediately beneath Operation Zero Tolerance. This direct movement follows the specific official ruling. A separate earlier move into the removed-from-game area is not recoverable by OZT. RRG page 17 sends a double-sided card entering any out-of-play area other than Victory or set-aside to the removed-from-game area. Thus ordinary hand/deck/discard movement, or defeat with Victory blanked, removes Magneto rather than discarding him into a player's deck. Enemy of My Enemy is not revealed as an automatic reverse-side replacement. His printed dash cost means he cannot be ordinarily played from hand (RRG page 15), even though the imported JSON represents that unavailable cost by omitting a numeric value.
