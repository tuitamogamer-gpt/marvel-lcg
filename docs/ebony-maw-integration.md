# Ebony Maw native scenario

This continuation starts from `f3e23a7` and adds the first scenario of The Mad Titan's Shadow.

## Original source and scope

Ebony Maw registers all 16 original scenario faces `21071`–`21084`: Ebony Maw (I)–(III), both sides of Attack on Knowhere and The Power Stone, the four Spell environments Fireball, Manipulation, Pacification and Rubblestorm, Agent of Thanos, Channeling Trance, Abjuration, Restrained and Reactor Overload. The Black Order modular set registers its four faces `21085`–`21088` and Armies of Titan its three faces `21089`–`21091`. All 23 faces are dedicated registrations in `src/game/ebony-maw.ts`; the imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged.

Standard selects Ebony Maw (I) and (II), Expert (II) and (III). Each game uses 21 physical scenario cards: two villain stages, two main schemes, two copies of each Spell, two Agent of Thanos, three Channeling Trance, Abjuration, Restrained and two Reactor Overload. The scenario uses two modular slots, as printed on Attack on Knowhere (1A): Armies of Titan (six physical cards) and Black Order (four physical cards) are the defaults, and either can be replaced. Both are also new selectable modular sets for every other scenario, so the selector lists thirty-six sets. The core Standard set and, in Expert, the Expert set are added as in every scenario.

## Native rules

- **Spells:** a Spell environment enters the play area of the player who reveals it, with its printed invocation counters (Fireball 4, Manipulation 2, Pacification 3, Rubblestorm 3), and is shown on the table as "Spell · hero name". Revealed Spells surge. A Spell put into play by another card's text does not, because only a revealed card resolves its Surge keyword (scenario rules clarification).
- **Ebony Maw:** his Forced Interrupt removes an invocation counter from each Spell of the player he activates against. A stunned or confused Ebony Maw removes the status card instead of activating, so his interrupt does not trigger then (scenario rules clarification). After the last counter is removed, the Spell is discarded and resolves: Fireball deals 4 damage to your identity; Manipulation discards a random card from your hand and confuses you; Pacification exhausts each upgrade you control and stuns you; Rubblestorm deals 2 damage to each character you control.
- **Setup and main schemes:** Attack on Knowhere (1B) gives each player a Spell, discarding from the top of the encounter deck until one is found, then shuffles the discard pile into the deck. Expert's Ebony Maw (II) gives each player a Spell first. Ebony Maw (II) and (III) and The Power Stone (2A) give each player a Spell when revealed; The Power Stone first shuffles the discard pile into the deck. Completing The Power Stone (9 threat per player) loses the game. A discard-until search stops at the first exhaustion of the encounter deck, like other native searches.
- **Treacheries:** Agent of Thanos places 1 threat on the main scheme per Spell in your play area in alter-ego form, or deals 1 damage per Spell to your hero in hero form, and surges if it had no effect. Channeling Trance removes a counter from each of your Spells, or puts a Spell into your play area if you have none.
- **Attachments:** Abjuration attaches to Ebony Maw and prevents all damage to him, as a constant prevention before Tough. After it prevents 2 or more damage from a single attack it is discarded; smaller attacks and non-attack damage leave it in play. Restrained attaches to the friendly character with the highest ATK (heroes and allies of every player; alter-egos have no ATK), exhausts it and stops it readying; ties are chosen by the revealing player, and with no candidate it is discarded. Its Hero Action spends an energy and a physical resource to discard it.
- **Reactor Overload** (crisis, 3 threat regardless of players) makes each player, in player order, choose to take 2 damage or place 2 threat on it.
- **Black Order:** Black Dwarf's attacks gain overkill. Supergiant (quickstrike) stuns the character its attack damages. The Black Order (hazard) keeps its threat while any Black Order minion is in play, including the Armies of Titan minions. Blood to Spare activates each minion engaged with a player against that player, then each player not engaged with a minion searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Black Order minion of their choice and puts it into play engaged with them; the deck is then shuffled.
- **Armies of Titan:** Black Order Infantry (guard) gives the villain a tough status card when defeated and as a boost. Outrider discards a random card from your hand when revealed and as a boost. Landing Craft (acceleration) puts the next minion discarded from the encounter deck into play engaged with the player who defeated it.
- **Elimination:** a defeated player's Spells and a Restrained on their identity are discarded.

## Coverage boundaries

The executable registry contains 2,504 faces, including 2,065 dedicated faces, with 2,047 imported faces still unsupported. There are 57 playable identities, twenty-one scenarios and thirty-six modular sets. The Mad Titan's Shadow has 96 of its 213 faces registered: its 72 hero, player-card, obligation and nemesis faces, the 23 faces above and the campaign card Shawarma `21183`, which has no rules text. Tower Defense, Thanos, Hela, Loki, their modular sets and the campaign account for the 117 pending faces. The inventory pins 139 source files. No official campaign rules are automated.

The configured source matrix contains 78,104 cases in eight disjoint shards of 9,763. Ebony Maw adds 3,744 cases (52 expansion starter identities × two difficulties × thirty-six primary modular sets), and Black Order and Armies of Titan add 4,160 cases to the twenty earlier scenarios. Manifest hashes retain all 70,200 previous mission names and seeds; the hash of the earlier configurations was checked against the full grid of `f3e23a7`.

Rules references: The Mad Titan's Shadow rulebook (Scenario #1 encounter sets, Spell Environment Cards and its rules clarifications), [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf), and the original imported card texts.
