# Black Widow and Doctor Strange integration audit

This audit distinguishes imported card faces, authored handlers, installed handlers and actual dispatcher verification. A product is executable only when every imported face is covered and its ordinary deck, special zones and encounter cards run through the native engine. Registration alone does not prove those behaviors. No campaign support is implied.

The table partitions every product face among installed dedicated handlers, exact Core aliases and retained declarative programs. The live [machine inventory](engine-expansion-inventory.json) separately records compiler recognition and installed registration. The full registry has 706 executable face registrations, of which 221 belong to dedicated modules, across 11 heroes and five scenarios. The 3,845 unsupported faces and 58 remaining identities are still imported for inspection; campaigns are not automated.

## Current primary rules

[Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) is the current general and errata source. Preparation followers trigger after resolution, not merely initiation (Black Widow and Synth-Suit errata, p. 66). Widow's Bite follows Quickstrike (p. 60). Status replacement precedes other abilities; canceled effects do not count as resolved (pp. 37, 41). Enemy attacks finish with forced responses before optional responses (p. 9). Overkill is attack damage (p. 31). A Defense ability while one's ally defends does not make the identity the defender (pp. 15–16). Invocation abilities resolve rather than play (p. 60). Iron Fist's ability is an Interrupt; Warning lacks the Defense trait and label (p. 66). Night Nurse discards any status type, including Tough (p. 60). Simultaneous AND effects, legal targets and source-in-play requirements govern return/status operations (pp. 4, 7, 23, 43).

The [official Doctor Strange rulesheet](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/6c/40/6c4068b6-00ee-4060-9855-335b826de33d/mc08_doctor_strange_rulesheet.pdf) defines a separately shuffled five-card Invocation deck, a faceup top card during play, a separate discard and an empty-deck reset without the ordinary player-deck penalty. Those cards never enter the ordinary hand, player discard or resource pool through normal drawing/payment.

FFG-authored designer answers are also preserved on third-party archival pages. [Alex's October 18–24, 2024 and September 15, 2025 rulings](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/) establish Preparation completion before Widowmaker, no follower after status cancellation, Attacrobatics' remaining legal icon cancellation under Guard, Counterspell remaining after a Stunned attack event, and Master of the Mystic Arts' discard/reset before returning its original Invocation to the top. [Alex's May 10, 2024 ruling 169](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/) permits Vapors of Valtorr without a status target because its self-discard is a valid effect. This supersedes the older contrary answer. [Archived FFG designer answers](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/official-ffg-rulings/) clarify Target Acquired's pre-boost-ability timing, full-card reveal cancellation, Espionage before Surge, Rapid Response's actual defeated ally and Unflappable's identity defense requirement. Older damage/Retaliate timing answers are superseded by the current attack sequence.

## Verification status

Both retail products have installed face-registration closure and accepted original source starter lists. Native dispatcher fixtures in `tests/black-widow-engine.test.ts` and `tests/doctor-strange-engine.test.ts` exercise actual play, payment, target prompts, encounters, boost interrupts, preparation completion, statuses, special decks and saved decisions. Separate module tests verify contract rejection and costs; `tests/hero-pack-integration-audit.test.ts` checks whole-product closure, physical supplementary cards and high-risk timing combinations. `tests/invocation-information.test.ts` verifies that planning preserves only the public Invocation top and only the encounter top known to the acting observer.

`scripts/hero-pack-check.mjs` verifies those mechanics through the browser: product provenance and source-deck launch for both heroes, the faceup Invocation inspector, Spell Mastery payment and physical-zone movement, Preparation completion followed by ordered optional responses, saved decisions, keyboard focus and responsive layouts.

The source-precon mission suite runs six packs across five scenarios, both difficulties and all nine modular sets (540 configurations), counting Strange's 40 ordinary cards plus five supplementary cards by physical ID. The default mission suite adds all 55 registered hero/scenario pairings. These tests establish progression, source-list acceptance and the named invariants. They do not certify arbitrary multiplayer interactions or perfect automation of the full 69-hero collection.
