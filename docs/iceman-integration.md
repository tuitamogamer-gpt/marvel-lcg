# Iceman Hero Pack

The original pack is implemented as 33 executable faces (`46001a/b` through `46032`), representing 59 physical cards. The original Aggression starter contains exactly 40 playing cards. Its six additional Frostbite upgrades (`46002`) begin set aside and never count toward deck size. The Sauron module has four faces and six physical cards. Downloaded catalog records, deck records and scans remain unchanged.

`src/game/iceman.ts` contains native player, nemesis and Sauron programs. Engine hooks cover basic attack/defense interrupts, actual activation completion, damage replacement, physical enemy departure, encounter boosts, resources, attachment legality and scheme icons. All original printing codes remain on their physical instances. Looking for Trouble, Team-Building Exercise, Recuperation and The Power in All of Us share existing native handlers through explicit printing aliases.

## Physical cards and timing

- Every Frostbite is an actual, distinct card. It moves from its owner's `player.setAside` to that owner's `player.inPlay`, with `attachedTo` identifying the enemy. Its controller remains its owner even if another player's copied effect attaches it. Its forced response returns the same card to its original owner's reserve. Multiple Frostbites stack their constant −1 ATK and −1 SCH modifiers; no extra copies are created when the six-card supply runs out.
- Freeze interrupts the basic attack or basic defense before it resolves. Cryokinetic Perception responds immediately inside that interrupt window. Basic defense has already paid its exhaust cost, so drawing an Ice card can leave Iceman ready. Surprise Move's eligibility is recomputed after Freeze attaches an upgrade in their shared window.
- Frostbite returns after an actual attack or scheme activation finishes. Stun, Confuse and Frozen Solid replace an activation, so existing Frostbites remain. Frozen Solid consumes its own physical upgrade and attaches a new available Frostbite. Activation continuations receive `performed: false` when it replaces an activation.
- Arctic Attack's first bullet attaches Frostbite alongside its damage before enemy defeat cleanup. Hot-Headed still resolves if that enemy is defeated. Ice Blast attaches to the chosen player's enemies and damages every enemy with Frostbite, including enemies engaged with other players.
- Ice Wall replaces the whole incoming damage packet and can exceed eight damage. Tough takes priority. Groot's growth-counter prevention and Ice Wall offer their legal forced-interrupt order. Breaking Ice Wall records a serialized receipt tied to that attack, then attaches Frostbite after the attack finishes and its previous Frostbites return. This also works for Pyro's indirect attack allocation and retained attack snapshots. Ice Wall modifies damage taken without changing the original amount dealt.
- A physical Mansion Attack villain's departure returns its Frostbites before a different villain enters. A villain stage transition that keeps the same enemy retains its attachments. Snow Clone's printed THW dash prohibits a basic thwart even when instructed by another effect; its consequential-damage reduction and upgrade prohibition remain independent constants.
- Keep Up the Pressure retains actual Attack-event provenance, including Dive Bomb's secondary damage clause. Shadowcat suppresses all four side-scheme icon types for the round without blanking text; central engine, legacy Crisis checks and advice read the same mask.
- Pyro's Flamethrower and The Eye of Sauron count every printed resource icon, including multiple icons on one card. Burn! and Sauron have star-only boost areas with zero numeric boost icons. Burn!'s additional icons participate in the ordinary boost-counting pipeline. Life Drain uses highest printed minion HP and initiates its actual activation.

## Restricted player triggers

The Merc with the Mouth blocks another controller's triggered player abilities during its controller's turn. This applies to Freeze, Cool Off, Power Belt and the forced triggers on Frostbite, Frozen Solid, Ice Wall and Ice Slide. Permanent and the Frostbite stat modifiers are constant abilities and remain active. Hot-Headed is an encounter ability and remains active. The forced-trigger restriction follows Rules Reference ability categories and FFG's June 13, 2024 clarification about triggered versus constant abilities; no published Frostbite-specific Merc example was found.

A narrow unresolved interaction occurs if an enemy leaves play while Merc prevents Frostbite's return response. Permanent forbids generic discard, so the implementation retains that same Frostbite in its owner's play area referencing its departed host. It does not invent a new return trigger, retarget it to a new physical villain or manufacture another card. This preserves the physical card and both stated restrictions pending a specific ruling. A normal, unblocked departure always returns the card. Vivian cannot target this player upgrade, and Permanent also prevents outside-set text blanking.

## Validation

`tests/iceman-engine.test.ts` checks original registration, source composition, every default aspect, the six-card reserve, physical conservation through reload, native player cards, co-op ownership, interrupt windows, replacement priority, actual activations, Mansion villain departure, Merc restrictions and original nemesis/module effects. `tests/iceman-retail-review.test.ts` checks every reprint, source-specific encounter boosts, indirect damage, searches and all Eye of Sauron resource branches. Targeted regression suites cover the affected legacy heroes, Groot, X-23, Mansion Attack, Deadpool, Star-Lord, Black Widow and the reprint handlers. The final targeted run passed 729 tests across 19 suites (one worker), including 63 Iceman tests. Type checking and formatting passed locally. Batch-wide matrix, browser and deployment verification belong to the parent import batch.
