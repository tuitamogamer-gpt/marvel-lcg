# Cable and NeXt Evolution player cards

This import installs native behavior for `40001a/b` and `40002`–`40036`: Cable's complete fifteen-card signature set, the original forty-card Leadership starter, its additional player cards, one obligation, and five physical nemesis cards. The raw catalog, source-deck records and original scans are unchanged. Nathan Summers permits player side schemes from other aspects without selecting those aspects as the deck's primary aspect.

The native leaf is `src/game/cable.ts`; its engine adapter uses the cycle-free card definitions and saved actor-bound effects. Setup searches the original deck and discard after the opening hand/mulligan, preserving the selected card's physical ID and owner. Nemesis setup reserves the original five cards. A side scheme in the shared victory display powers Mind Scan, Telekinetic Blast, Plasma Rifle and Precognition; Technovirus Purge supplies its continuous stat and Psionic bonuses only while there.

The shared side-scheme defeat continuation resolves printed rewards and Victory before Cable, Graymalkin, Mission Leader and Forced Amnesia response windows. Windows recheck readiness and phase limits when reached. Call for Backup, Lock and Load, Establish Perimeter and Build Support use the common side-scheme limit, player order and physical victory display. The exported `nativeCanPutIntoPlay` and `nativePutIntoPlay` helpers apply uniqueness, attachment constraints, original ownership, native entry counters/hooks, maximum-health changes and Restricted limits without paying costs or firing after-you-play responses. Ally entry uses the existing `allyEnter` continuation with `fromHand: false`.

Plasma Rifle and Askani'son commit an energy payment and exhaust the actual upgrade. Psimitar responds to another played Psionic card. Telekinetic Force Field protects friendly characters, including other seats; the enemy attachment prevents damage and discards after preventing at least two. Temporal Leap searches the actual victory display, removes its original upgrade from the game, puts the chosen scheme into play and moves four existing threat before pending threat placement resumes. Crisis blocks moving threat. Pending threat can still complete the main scheme after Temporal Leap.

Caliban uses the native discard batch and can recover a qualifying ally after deck exhaustion by its original physical ID. Fantomex finds the actual E.V.A.; E.V.A. leaves when Fantomex is absent. Sunspot uses actual energy spent on its from-hand play. Mission Planning and Uncanny X-Force modify real consequential-damage packets. Deadpool accounts for the entire consequential packet before healing three, adds one acceleration token, and can still be defeated if damage remains lethal. Deathlok attaches a legal upgrade from any player's discard, retaining its owner. The Sidearm reprint shares the original native attachment, stat, ranged and maximum-per-ally behavior. The Power of the Mind uses the existing Psionic resource rule.

The nemesis implements Villainous Stryfe's Psionic-event cancellation, Back to the Future's player-specific damage/threat restrictions, both scaled treacheries and all three boost stars. Technovirus Resurgence finds the actual Purge in the specified zones, applies the first player's excess-side-scheme choice, and only attaches if that physical scheme remains in play.

Rules evidence: original checked-in English scans and [NeXt Evolution gallery](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/next-evolution/), [RRG v1.8](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf), [FFG rulings after v1.5](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/), and [FFG rulings after v1.6](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/). Specific clarifications include August 10, 2023 #62 (Cable's events/upgrades in hero form can remove Purge threat), August 31 #78 (supports are independent of their identities), September 7 #79 (Deathlok still obeys attachment constraints), and May 10, 2024 #182 (Temporal Leap resolves before impending threat is placed). The original physical source evidence is not rewritten to implement these rules.

`tests/cable-engine.test.ts` covers source composition and all four primary aspects, post-mulligan setup ownership, phase limits and save/reload continuations, each imported native card family, shared entry/reprint behavior, multiplayer targets, payments, defensive windows, victory, obligations and original nemesis conservation. The full source mission and browser gates run at batch integration.
