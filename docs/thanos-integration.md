# Thanos native scenario

This continuation starts from `1e03888` and adds the third scenario of The Mad Titan's Shadow: Thanos with the Infinity Gauntlet.

## Original source and scope

Thanos registers all 16 original scenario faces `21111`–`21124`: Thanos (I)–(III), both sides of The Infinity Stones and Balance the Scales, Sanctuary, Thanos's Armor, Thanos's Helmet, Master of the Stones, Avatar of Death, Deviant Syndrome, "I Am Inevitable", The Mad Titan and The Titan's Throne. The Children of Thanos modular set registers its four faces `21125`–`21128` (the minions Corvus Glaive, Proxima Midnight and Ebony Maw, and Tribute) and the Infinity Gauntlet set its seven faces `21129`–`21135` (the Infinity Gauntlet and the Mind, Power, Reality, Soul, Space and Time Stones). All 27 faces are dedicated registrations in `src/game/thanos.ts`; the imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged.

Standard selects Thanos (I) and (II), Expert (II) and (III). Each game uses 18 Thanos cards (two villain stages, two main schemes and fourteen encounter cards), the Infinity Gauntlet and the six Stones, and two modular sets: Black Order and Children of Thanos by default (four and five cards), either of which can be replaced. Children of Thanos is also a new selectable modular set for every other scenario, so the selector lists thirty-seven sets. The Infinity Gauntlet set is always included in Thanos and is not shuffled into the encounter deck. The core Standard set and, in Expert, the Expert set are added as in every scenario.

## Native rules

- **The Infinity Gauntlet** (Permanent, Setup) attaches to Thanos during setup (+1 ATK, +1 SCH). After Thanos activates against you (attack or scheme), each Infinity Stone in play resolves its Special ability against you, in the order the Stones entered play, and goes to the Infinity Stone discard pile; if no Stone is in play, the top Stone enters play instead.
- **The Infinity Stone deck** holds the six Stones, shuffled, with its own discard pile. An empty deck reshuffles its discard pile. Whenever the deck runs out, Thanos's Forced Response gives him a facedown boost card.
- **Stone Specials:** Mind — you are confused, or if you already were, discard a random card from your hand. Power — you are stunned, or if you already were, take 3 damage. Reality — discard an ally, upgrade or support you control (you choose). Soul — Thanos heals 3 damage and gets a facedown boost card. Space — discard encounter cards until a minion, which engages you (stopping at the first exhaustion of the encounter deck, like other native searches). Time — discard the top 4 cards of your deck and place 1 threat on the main scheme for each different card type among them.
- **Setup and main schemes:** The Infinity Stones (1B) puts the top Stone into play and searches the encounter deck for Sanctuary, which is revealed. Thanos (II) and (III) search the encounter deck and discard pile for Thanos's Helmet and reveal it, at setup in Expert and when they are revealed later. Balance the Scales (2B) makes each player shuffle their discard pile into their deck and remove the top half of it (rounded down) from the game; completing it loses the game.
- **Thanos** is stalwart; stages (II) and (III) have toughness.
- **Sanctuary** (hinder 1 per player, Victory 1): Thanos cannot take damage from player cards (Rules Reference 1.8 errata) — the heroes, identities and cards that belong to a player. When it is defeated, each player in turn may spend up to 3 physical resources from their hand (wild resources count as physical), and Thanos takes 2 damage for each, ignoring tough. Its When Defeated and Victory 1 are ordered by the first player.
- **Thanos's Armor** reduces each amount of damage Thanos would take by 1 (before tough). **Thanos's Helmet** gives him retaliate 1. After a hero makes a basic attack against Thanos, that hero may spend energy and physical (Armor) or mental and physical (Helmet) to discard it.
- **Master of the Stones** (+1 ATK, +1 SCH): when Thanos activates, the top Stone enters play, and the attachment is discarded at the end of that activation.
- **Avatar of Death:** Thanos schemes (alter-ego) or attacks you with overkill and piercing (hero). **Deviant Syndrome** (incite 1) gives Thanos a tough status card, or places 2 threat if he already has one; as a boost it gives him tough. **"I Am Inevitable"** gives him a facedown boost card; as a boost it discards the top Stone and adds its boost icons (plus amplify) to the activation. **The Mad Titan** puts the top Stone into play; as a boost it does so if the attack defeats an ally. **The Titan's Throne** (amplify) discards the Stone in play the revealing player chooses.
- **Children of Thanos:** Corvus Glaive (retaliate 1, toughness) and Proxima Midnight (her attacks gain piercing) make you discard an ally or support, or an ally or upgrade, you control as boosts. Ebony Maw is villainous; as a boost he gives the activating enemy one more boost card. Tribute (crisis) deals the player who defeats it a facedown encounter card. The unique rule keeps these minions out of play while a villain with the same title is in play.

## Coverage boundaries

- The Infinity Gauntlet set is offered only in Thanos. The rulebook also allows it in other single-villain scenarios; that use is not offered.
- Damage to Thanos from an encounter effect that names no source counts as damage from a player card while Sanctuary is in play. No Thanos, Black Order, Children of Thanos, Standard or Expert card does this.
- The executable registry contains 2,553 faces, including 2,114 dedicated faces, with 1,998 imported faces still unsupported. There are 57 playable identities, twenty-three scenarios and thirty-seven modular sets. The Mad Titan's Shadow has 145 of its 213 faces registered; Hela, Loki, their modular sets and the campaign account for the 68 pending faces. The inventory pins 141 source files. No official campaign rules are automated.

The configured source matrix contains 87,984 cases in eight disjoint shards of 10,998. Thanos adds 3,848 cases (52 expansion starter identities × two difficulties × thirty-seven primary modular sets), and Children of Thanos adds 2,288 cases to the twenty-two earlier scenarios. Manifest hashes retain all 81,848 previous mission names and seeds; the hash of the earlier configurations was checked against the full grid of `1e03888`.

Rules references: The Mad Titan's Shadow rulebook (Scenario #3 and The Infinity Gauntlet Encounter Set), [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) (Sanctuary and Infinity Gauntlet errata, Amplify Icon, Stalwart, Villainous), and the original imported card texts.
