# Sabretooth standalone scenario native contract

This module authors the sixteen Sabretooth faces from Mutant Genesis, 32060–32072, including both main-scheme fronts/backs and Find/Protect the Senator. The original source contains seventeen physical cards: three villain stages, two main schemes, Find/Protect as one double-sided card, Robert Kelly, and ten cards for the scenario encounter deck. Brotherhood and Mystique are the printed recommended modular sets; their authored modules remain separate. This work does not implement the Mutant Genesis campaign or its campaign setup/rewards.

The unchanged [catalog](../src/data/catalog-cards.json) and [official printing scans](https://cerebrodatastorage.blob.core.windows.net/cerebro-cards/official/32060.jpg) define the source faces. In particular, Medical Emergency's base threat is **fixed two**, followed by Hinder 2 per player on reveal (2 + 2N total); Feral Rage's base threat is **fixed four**. The original catalog already preserves these flags and Kelly's unique symbol; no metadata correction is needed. Kelly has nine hit points and no printed ATK or THW power. [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) supplies current activation, defense, ownership, Hinder, Victory, status, damage and response timing. [Official ruling #282](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings/#282) confirms that Sabretooth must discard the top encounter card after an actual activation even at full health.

## Physical state and setup

`GameState.sabretooth?: SabretoothState` is the serialized scenario state. `kelly?: Piece` is one NPC zone containing the actual 32066 piece; `kellyId` remains after that piece leaves so the loss condition can identify it. `startingDamage` records the chosen setup amount. `controllerId` is control metadata, not a second player-zone copy. Completed activation IDs and used defense-response IDs persist in this scenario state, rather than round-cleared seat flags.

If an encounter effect such as Beguiled temporarily treats Kelly as a minion, the host moves that same piece into the native minion zone and clears the live NPC pointer, retaining `kellyId` and control metadata. `sabretoothKelly` then locates that existing ID in the minion zone; this lookup is not an additional ownership zone. Kelly's ally control/interrupt is inactive while he is treated as a minion. Removing the conversion restores the same piece to the NPC zone and reevaluates his first-player constant. Zone transfer for a type conversion is not a leave-play event; an actual departure from either live zone still loses the game. Physical audits must count the actual NPC pointer or the minion entry once, rather than also counting the lookup result.

The host extracts the existing Find and Kelly pieces from the original setup source before calling `sabretoothSetup(s, find, kelly, ports, startingDamage?)`. There is no module card factory. Find enters with 5 threat per player and its printed Crisis icon. Kelly attaches to Find, remains in play under no player's control, and has a blank textbox while main scheme 1B is active. He is still an ally character and can suffer encounter effects. Page seven of the [original scenario booklet](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/mutant-genesis-rulebook.pdf) recommends one starting damage for Standard, two for Expert and three for Heroic; the optional group adjustment accepts an integer from zero through eight. These values are setup damage on the same nine-HP piece, not changes to printed maximum HP. A repeated setup call does not reset a live saved Kelly.

The host must include the NPC zone exactly once in physical-ID audits, `find`, character HP/damage/status handling, friendly-character targeting, damage prevention, defeat and leave-play routing. A controlled Kelly contributes to ally/controlled-character views without being copied into a player's `inPlay`. He has no basic attack/thwart power, ignores the ally limit while his text is active, cannot receive player-card attachments while his text is active, and cannot receive upgrades or healing from player-card effects while unblanked Find/Protect supplies those restrictions. Encounter-card healing remains legal. `sabretoothKellyCanHeal` and `sabretoothKellyCanAttach` distinguish these origins. Generic blanking after rescue does not erase control already established by Find's defeat instruction; it disables Kelly's printed constant/interrupt while blank.

The stage faces retain native printed stats and keywords: stage II has Toughness, stage III has Toughness and Retaliate 1. Entering a stage grants its native Tough card; Retaliate uses the native post-attack forced response and disappears if its printed textbox is blank. Adamantium Claws' printed +1 ATK uses the host's existing attachment-stat sum exactly once. `sabretoothModifiers` supplies only attachment text grants: Claws' Piercing and Animal Ferocity's Stalwart. Blanking an attached donor removes its text grant without removing its printed statistic; blanking the recipient does not remove a grant from another active source.

Medical Emergency's healing and Victory 1 are both mandatory When Defeated abilities in the same native window. The first player orders them under Rules Reference 1.8; there is no general rule that its heal must precede or follow adding the physical card to the victory display. Both must resolve, and the card never enters the encounter discard pile as its defeat destination.

## Native reveals, choices and payments

`sabretoothReveal` handles 2A, the two attachments, Strikes and Unrelenting Savage; other faces retain their native continuous/entry/defeat behavior. The host resolves ordinary Hinder on revealed Medical Emergency, rather than adding it on setup/put. `sabretoothBoost` attaches the same physical Claws/Ferocity boost card to Sabretooth, preserving it from ordinary boost discard. Numeric icons stay in the native activation packet. Regeneration's discard is not a boost reveal and cannot attach these cards.

Strikes offers `exhaust` and `damage` options only when the actual revealing player's hero can exhaust. Exhaust prevents that exact one-damage Kelly effect. Alter ego, exhausted heroes and native cannot-exhaust restrictions proceed to damage without an invalid choice. A save during the choice retains the revealing actor and original source.

Savage snapshots form and sustained villain damage before queuing one native explicit attack or scheme. Its `activation: {enemyId, playerId, kind, modifier}` payload grants +1 only when Sabretooth is at full HP at that initiation; it never permanently changes ATK/SCH. The later post-activation heal does not change this already captured modifier.

Attachment actions use option ID `discard:<physicalAttachmentId>` and native payment cost three with energy, mental and physical requirements. Wild assignment and resource abilities remain the host payment engine's job. Protect uses `protect:<physicalEnvironmentId>:<actualAttackId>` with two resources of any type. `pay` must preserve real spent-card ownership, ability-cost context, spend responses and an optional cancellation continuation. The attachment is discarded or the hero readied only after payment commits. A canceled payment neither grants the effect nor consumes Protect's response. A committed Protect response is once per actual attack and source, and a later attack can offer a new response.

## Verification boundary

The authored module is exercised by [pure tests](../tests/sabretooth.test.ts). Those tests check physical identity, setup defaults/overrides, JSON continuations, exact threat scaling, Find-to-Protect timing, neutral-control behavior, undefended versus Overkill classification, real activation receipts, numeric-only regeneration, encounter exhaustion, native payment boundaries, Strikes and Savage choices, and actual defeating-seat routing. Native engine and browser integration provide separate evidence for damage prevention, stage entry, defense legality, real resource payments and original-source conservation; pure ports do not certify those host behaviors.

`npx vitest run tests/sabretooth.test.ts tests/sabretooth-engine.test.ts` passes **61 focused controls: 46 pure and 15 native dispatcher controls**. The [native tests](../tests/sabretooth-engine.test.ts) move original-source instances and cross JSON hydration on every command. They cover full-health and boost-star regeneration, actual Claws/Ferocity boost retention and keywords, energy/mental/physical payment and cancellation, Strikes' reveal choices and actual ally-defeat trigger, Protect paid with actual Web-Shooter resource abilities and cancellation, and stage Toughness/Retaliate. `npx tsc -b` also passes. Full regression, the independent host-boundary suite, browser proof, and publication are separate gates owned by the host integration work.
