# Hawkeye and Spider-Woman native integration — 5 October 2026

Both Rise of Red Skull heroes now have installed native engine adapters and can launch their original 40-card source decks. Hawkeye uses Leadership; Spider-Woman uses Aggression and Justice. Actual printing codes and physical card IDs survive payments, control changes, stored-card play, discard and JSON reload. Alternate supported starters also validate; Spider-Woman counts colored signature cards within the two chosen aspects, while retaining all required signatures from the other aspects.

The account deck editor exposes both selected aspects and locks every required signature, including off-pair colored cards. Saved deck metadata preserves the chosen pair when the customizable portion contains only Basic cards; older lists infer their pair from the actual customizable cards. Loading a deck, saving a mission and rematching preserve that pair.

The installed registry has **743 executable face registrations, 3,808 unsupported faces and 264 dedicated registrations**. Automated missions offer **13 identities, five scenarios and nine modular sets**. The two hero halves contain 31 and 28 faces, covered by their dedicated modules, exact Core reprints, existing Avengers Tower adapter and complete compiler programs. This does not enable the five Rise of Red Skull scenarios or any campaign.

## Native behavior

- **Hawkeye:** Weapon of Choice and Quick Draw use native identity commands and payments. Bow grants the printed ATK and Ranged benefits. Expert Marksman resources only pay for Arrow events. Quiver searches preserve the same public Arrow instances; those arrows can be inspected and played, cannot be spent as resources, and remain tucked through canceled payments. Every Arrow uses native damage, status, Guard/Patrol/Crisis and attack timing. Bow exhaustion still pays the cost when Stunned or Confused replaces the power.
- **Hawkeye's allies and upgrades:** Goliath's maximum applies to the whole team and expires at the phase boundary. Team Training respects its shared title limit and its controller's ally HP. Sky Cycle recognizes gained Avenger traits, transfers control to the attached ally's controller and retains its physical owner. Mockingbird's initiation interrupt has cancelable payment; prevention remains bound to that attack through boosts, defender changes and Overkill. Boost abilities still resolve. Criminal Past, Crossfire, his Rifle and the current Marked for Death errata use native encounter zones and ownership.
- **Spider-Woman:** Superhuman Agility is an explicit play interrupt, including aspect signatures and reaction events. Its bonus affects the same basic attack or Counter-Punch where relevant, survives form changes and ends with the round. Spending resources and putting cards into play do not trigger it. Finesse can pay for aspect cards and their abilities, including Jarnbjorn and Vision. Jessica Drew's look ability exposes one selected deck top; Apartment searches and shuffles the physical deck.
- **Spider-Woman's events and allies:** Pheromones only offers targets that can receive at least one printed status. Inconspicuous allocates one thwart atomically, resolves attached defeat interrupts before removing the schemes, then opens responses after all defeated schemes have left play. Spider-Girl and Spider-Man distinguish play from hand from Make the Call. Captain Marvel's optional draw precedes consequential damage. Ultron Drones use the runtime enemy definitions.
- **Nemesis timing:** Viper affects only its engaged identity. Hail Hydra tracks the actual attacked player after a defensive redirect, excludes replaced attacks, preserves physical searched cards, processes Quickstrike without When Revealed or Incite, and continues after the revealing player is eliminated. The first player chooses simultaneous minion attack order; each unattacked player makes their own search decision.

## Verification

Active native suites are `tests/hawkeye-engine.test.ts` and `tests/spider-woman-engine.test.ts`; their former pending files have been removed. Separate module suites verify rejected costs and contract boundaries. The source-precon simulation matrix now covers eight expansion heroes across five scenarios, two difficulties and all nine supported modular sets: **720 configurations**. It checks progression to a result and conservation of every physical player card; it is not a proof of win rate or every rules interaction.

Final local validation: **2,212 tests pass across 55 files**, with one intentionally opt-in advisor measurement skipped. This includes the 720 configurations, 65 registered hero/scenario pairings, 158 targeted native/module tests, account API persistence and editor regressions. TypeScript/production build, native Node production entry, formatting and diff whitespace checks pass.

`scripts/rise-heroes-check.mjs` exercises actual browser controls for Quiver search, inspection/focus return, payment cancel/reload/replay, Arrow resolution, Quick Draw, Glide, Jessica Drew's saved choice, source-deck launch and Spider-Woman's second-aspect selection. Its account path creates a local test account, edits both aspects, saves/reloads the pair and launches the exact deck. Four behavior groups, ten responsive layouts and four accessibility audits pass without browser errors. It checks 320, 390, 1280 and 1440 px. Evidence is saved under `output/rise-heroes/`. The existing action-flow and viewport-fit suites also pass; the standard web-game client screenshot and text state were inspected.

The generated inventory hashes the new modules and the actual native engine, payment, deck and registry implementations. These implementation checks were completed locally; publication additionally requires remote SHA parity and verification of the production deployment.

## Rules sources

The printed hero/deck rules come from [FFG's Rise of Red Skull rulebook](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/92/1d/921ddef8-c28c-4096-a9e9-63142230a602/mc10_the_rise_of_red_skull_rules_web.pdf). Timing, status, ownership, limits, Finesse, Hail Hydra and Marked for Death follow [FFG Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/08/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed-1.pdf), including its current FAQ and errata. Immutable imported card text and printing provenance remain unchanged.

The larger 69-hero collection remains unfinished: **56 imported identities**, further scenarios and every automated campaign still require implementation. Ant-Man and Wasp are the next chronological hero products; their additional identity forms require native form handling before registration.
