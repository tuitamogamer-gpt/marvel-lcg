# Nova original retail integration

The identity/signature/obligation/nemesis module implements fifteen catalog faces: `28001a`, `28001b`, `28002`–`28009`, and `28021`–`28025`. The original starter has forty physical player cards, including fifteen signature cards (Ms. Marvel ×1, Forcefield Projection ×2, Lightspeed Flight ×3, Pot Shot ×3, Unleash Nova Force ×2, Connection to the Worldmind ×2, Jesse Alexander ×1 and Supernova Helmet ×1). There are no setup player cards. The five actual nemesis cards are Bring the War ×1, Warbringer ×1, War Delivery ×2 and The War's Been Brought ×1. The separate Armadillo modular set is outside this identity contract.

Sources: [original retail gallery](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/sam-alexander-nova/), [RRG 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf), [official Nova rulings](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/official-ffg-rulings/#nova), and the catalog's RRG 1.5 erratum adding “from your discard pile” to the Nova Ms. Marvel ally. Original gallery images n0a/n0b, n1/n2, n6/n9/n10, n12/n14/n15 and a12–a17 were visually reviewed. RRG Ability, Cost, Ownership and Control, Qualifiers, Wild Resource, and End of Round apply to the native adapters.

## Payment and resources

`novaResourceSources` returns exactly one generated Wild from the actual unexhausted, unblanked Supernova Helmet in hero form. `novaResourceSpent` exhausts that exact piece. The Helmet does not intrinsically double resources. `novaPaymentResources` doubles each generated Wild only when paying for the printed Lightspeed Flight or Pot Shot card, once per source after that source's own resource generation; other resource types and all other payment targets stay unchanged. This applies to Wild resources from hand cards and resource abilities alike.

`NovaPaymentReceipt.generatedForCard` contains only the original generated types allocated to the actual cost, before Wild conversion. Overpayment is excluded. Sam's search and Forcefield Projection use this field for their printed Wild qualifier. Converted Wild remains an originally generated Wild; an overpaid Wild cannot satisfy either qualifier. Native payment cancellation must retain the current reaction window and commit no resource/card mutation.

Sam's alter-ego action spends one resource, searches the actual deck and discard pile, then moves the same physical Helmet to hand or puts that exact piece into play when an allocated Wild paid the ability. Search shuffles the searched deck; put-into-play entry windows resolve before that shuffle. It neither exhausts Sam nor consumes a form-change allowance. Jesse exhausts as the cost for the entire ability, selects one actual Worldmind discard copy, shuffles it into the deck, then draws one. If the shuffle clause has no eligible copy but drawing can resolve, the independent draw can resolve after the exhaustion cost; exhausted Jesse never draws for free.

## Identity, hand size and response timing

`novaTraits` grants Aerial to hero-form Nova only while the actual Helmet text is active. `novaHandSize(s, base, ports)` returns the final hand limit, adding one for each actual active Connection to the Worldmind in hand. During the mulligan/setup phase the modifier is suppressed: RRG setup forbids ordinary player-card ability resolution, and opening Draw Cards is a fixed printed alter-ego draw. The same retained Worldmind becomes active after Keep completes. Native refill must recompute this after every draw, including a Worldmind drawn as the otherwise final card. Stored or discarded Worldmind does not increase hand size.

`novaAfterBasicPower` consumes a typed receipt for an actual completed basic THW, ATK or DEF use. It never triggers for REC, a teammate, an attempted basic action replaced by Stun/Confuse, or a canceled/aborted action. Basic DEF completes after attack damage and ordinary forced cleanup. The optional identity response readies the actual exhausted Helmet, once for the same basic-use instance. Identity blanking suppresses the response; blanking the Helmet's own text does not suppress an identity-granted ready.

Weight of the World is given to the actual Sam Alexander player and remains in play. It offers no invented reveal-time free flip or choice. `novaCanReadyPiece` enforces its global named-Helmet ready prohibition for identity responses, generic ready effects and phase refill, even when the actual Helmet transfers to a teammate. Unrelated cards can still ready. Blanking the obligation removes that prohibition. Its alter-ego action exhausts Sam and removes the same obligation ID from the game.

## Events and lasting effects

Lightspeed Flight and Pot Shot use the ordinary paid native thwart/attack programs, legal Crisis/Patrol/Guard targets and whole-ability Confuse/Stun replacement. Printed numerical values are three threat and four attack damage. Their generated-Wild doubling applies while paying, before any status replacement.

Unleash Nova Force has **no ATK or THW bonus**. Its printed duration is **until the end of the round**, including the villain phase. Native actual PLAY commit records its maximum once per round across all players and copies, including an ability canceled after PLAY; canceled uncommitted payment consumes nothing. Once its ability resolves, each actual Nova-attributed enemy defeat or positive-to-zero threat removal produces one ready and one draw. These effects resolve independently if ready/draw is prohibited. Each actual target in a simultaneous batch has a distinct achievement key; a later separate occurrence involving the same target is distinct. Ally damage, attempted/prevented damage, removing no threat, and leaving play from HP-stat loss do not count. Nova's nonattack damage and actual identity-controlled encounter Hero Actions can count. `novaRoundEnded` expires the lasting effect; player-phase end does not. Established lasting effects survive later blanking of their already resolved event/identity text.

The Nova Ms. Marvel ally responds to the actual native event PLAY instance after it enters the owner's discard pile. It exhausts and pays one full damage before moving that exact event ID back to hand. Damage may defeat Ms. Marvel: leave-play cleanup precedes returning the event and does not cancel the already paid response. Tough or other prevention which makes the full damage cost unavailable blocks initiation. A legitimate replay has a new PLAY token and can trigger the ally again after an actual ready.

Forcefield Projection is a Hero Interrupt in an actual friendly-character would-take-attack-damage window. It prevents up to three damage on the retained instance, preserving the target and current defender. It is not a Defense event and never designates a defender. An allocated originally generated Wild enables its separate three nonattack damage to any legal enemy; Guard does not restrict this damage, Stun does not replace it and attack modifiers/Retaliate do not apply. All ordinary Tough/prevention/defeat rules still apply to the damage. It has no printed limit per window, so the same physical event may be legitimately replayed after recovery while positive damage remains. Native hand/George storage adapters expose stored events only as owned PLAY sources, never as resources or unrelated hand costs.

## Nemesis host boundaries

Initialization creates exactly five missing fresh-game reserve pieces once and preserves their IDs through reveal/shuffle and save/reload. Migration must not recreate pieces already played out of a legacy reserve. Shadow of the Past reveals the actual reserve Warbringer and side scheme, then shuffles the three remaining actual IDs into the encounter deck; repeated Shadow surges.

Warbringer's forced attack interrupt counts cards with one or more **printed** Wild icons in the actual attacked player's hand; a two-Wild card counts as one card. The bonus is snapshotted for that attack and that attack gains Overkill. Blanking Warbringer disables this text. War Delivery offers an actual Wild payment or serial villain and surviving Warbringer attacks, even in alter ego; a stunned villain does not suppress the independent Warbringer attack. Its boost counts printed-Wild cards in the affected player's actual hand and places that much main-scheme threat.

Bring the War asks each player, in player order, to discard an actual discardable in-play controlled card with a printed Wild resource. Abilities default to in-play targets even though Ownership and Control also recognizes out-of-play ownership/control; hand, deck, discard and George stored cards are not eligible for this unqualified discard. Each actual discard adds one threat to that exact side scheme. The War's Been Brought counts **printed Wild icons** across hand, in-play controlled cards and discard once each, then discards exactly that many encounter cards through native exhaustion/reset handling. Surge is the printed generic keyword and remains the native encounter host's responsibility.
