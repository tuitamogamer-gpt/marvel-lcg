# Colossus native integration

`src/game/colossus.ts` implements the two identity faces, all 15 signature cards, Homesick and the five physical nemesis cards. The host owns ordinary card PLAY, resource payment, attack resolution, encounter reveal, deck exhaustion, status placement and prompts. The module does not supply an alternative game runner.

## Confirmed sources

- [Original Mutant Genesis cards and starter lists](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/mutant-genesis/); original physical card metadata remains in the catalog.
- [Official Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf), Appendix V: Steel Fist is a **Hero Action (attack)**. Apply the expansion errata overlay rather than retaining the catalog's omitted `(attack)` label. Armor Up's current “would activate” text is already in the catalog.
- [Official post-1.5 rulings, question 145, February 15, 2024](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/): changing to Piotr with two Tough cards requires discarding one.
- [Official post-1.6 rulings, March 16, 2025](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/): losing permission to hold multiple status cards requires discarding to the allowed limit; Armor Up resets the changed activation to its initiation and checks statuses again.
- [Earlier Colossus/Piercing ruling](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/official-ffg-rulings/#colossus) confirms Piercing acts once and permits Organic Steel's response. Its older zero-damage conclusion is superseded by RRG1.8 p32: an attack that would deal no damage discards no Tough cards.

## Required dispatch and timing

1. Initialize the actual nemesis reserve with `colossusInitializeNemesis` during setup. `32026`, `32027`, two distinct `32028` and `32029` belong to the Colossus seat. Homesick is the one actual obligation mixed into the encounter deck. `colossusShadowOfPast` reveals Juggernaut then Rampaging Juggernaut and shuffles the other three reserved physical cards. Repeated Shadows surges.
2. After opening-hand draw and mulligan, call `colossusSetup` at RRG1.8 Appendix II step16. Choose an actual Organic Steel still in the deck, move that physical piece to hand, then shuffle. A copy already in hand or discarded is ineligible. If neither copy remains, shuffle and continue without manufacturing one.
3. Route `colossusAbility`, `colossusEvent`, `colossusEncounterReveal`, `colossusBoost` and the `colossus:` effect namespace through existing native dispatch. Route actual PLAY/PUT entry through `colossusCardEntered`, which places Organic Steel's two Uses counters on its actual reset physical instance. Use `colossusPlayRestriction` and `colossusEventAction`; ordinary action PLAY cannot substitute for the Made of Rage or Armor Up reaction windows.
4. Add `colossusStats` to hero stats and `colossusResourceSources`/`colossusResourceSpent` to native payment. Titanium Muscles generates the actual current Tough count as Physical resources, exhausts the actual controlled upgrade and is Hero Resource only. It cannot fund payment while in alter ego. Native generation receipts preserve each generated Physical resource.
5. Apply `colossusToughLimit` to native status placement: two only on an unblanked hero face, one otherwise. On a change to alter ego and on loss of the constant ability through blanking, call `colossusEnforceToughLimit`. That removal is an actual discard and triggers Iron Will; it does not trigger Hero Response Organic Steel while alter ego. After an actual form change, call `colossusFormChanged`. Steel Skin and Aspiring Artist are optional, actual form-change responses; setup selecting alter ego is not a form change.
6. Native Tough discards must carry `ColossusToughDiscardReceipt`: token, actual target/seat, actual discarded count and form at the occurrence. Discard the entire simultaneous batch before any response. Costs, Tough damage prevention, Piercing, forced reduction of the legal Tough limit, Homesick and Rampaging Juggernaut all require receipts. Each actual discarded status is a separate ordinal within the shared response window; Iron Will can draw once for each. Organic Steel pays actual exhaust/counter costs, cannot repeat for the same ordinal and its Uses upgrade leaves play when its last counter is removed. Attempted discards of zero produce no response.
7. Pay Bulletproof Protector's Tough cost through `colossusAdditionalCost`, including discard responses, before its choice effects. RRG1.8 initiating-abilities timing allows responses immediately after paying a cost. Steel Fist's optional discard is an effect payment, offered only if the surviving actual target can be stunned or confused. Preserve all Steel Fist clauses inside the same native attack program; finish the optional status clause before Retaliate and other “after attack” responses. A Stalwart enemy can receive the damage but makes the optional Tough payment unavailable.
8. After initiating/exhausting a basic attack and checking Stun replacement, offer `colossusBasicAttackOptions`. No actual attack remains when Stun replaces it. Native `playReaction` pays and records the actual Made of Rage event, then `colossusReaction` discards Tough and modifies that attack by +6/Overkill. `resumeBasicAttack` inserts the retained modified packet after the event's native `finishResolution`, ensuring the interrupt completes before the interrupted attack resumes. More than one distinct Made of Rage may interrupt the same attack if costs remain payable; retain all earlier packet modifiers.
9. At the villain-would-activate boundary, offer `colossusActivationOptions` to every eligible surviving seat, after checking status replacement against the originally intended activation. Armor Up can be played for another player's villain activation without changing that player's type. For Colossus's own activation, changing alter ego to hero resets the now-attack activation to its initiation and rechecks Stun before attack interrupts or boost cards. A Confused villain whose original scheme is replaced has no activation to interrupt. The native flip opens optional Steel Skin before the resumed activation. Explicit extra `enemyAttack`/`enemyScheme` activations also permit Armor Up after their status replacement check; their printed attack/scheme kind remains fixed. The shared native activation receipt and handled flag prevent offering the same window twice when the ordinary `villainActivate` resumes through these functions.
10. Shadowcat ally ignores Guard/Patrol/Crisis only for her own actual attack/thwart. Use `colossusAllyModifiers` in the existing ally target selection; do not globally bypass these restrictions for Colossus or his other allies.
11. Native enemy stats include Unstoppable's printed +2 ATK. Its reveal chooses the greatest printed ATK among enemies without a copy already attached, permitting a player choice on ties. After Stun replacement, call `colossusEnemyAttackStarted` against the exact attack; it grants Overkill/Piercing and retains the attachment's discard in that attack's `afterActivation`. Execute those retained effects at the attack's end before optional responses. Do not also dispatch `colossusEnemyAttackEnded` if the native host already executes `afterActivation`, which would duplicate the discard. Canceled/replaced attacks that never begin do not consume the attachment.
12. Juggernaut is the original minion with Stalwart/Toughness; the generic keyword engine supplies both. Its boost grants Overkill/Piercing to the actual attack only. Slammed's star boost reveals its same physical instance through native encounter handling while preserving the activation's numeric boost receipt. Homesick and Rampaging Juggernaut act on actual seat/character Tough cards; Rampaging's additional threat counts the discarded batch and excludes replacement Tough gained by responses.

## Host adapter artifact

`output/mutant-genesis/colossus-host-fragment.ts` is a private-engine insertion fragment using the existing `requestPayment`, `resolveHandEvent`, `mill`, `niAttackProgram`, native status/encounter helpers and native queue context. It includes the required Piotr's Studio physical retrieval continuation after deck exhaustion, including the case where the found last card has already moved into the shuffled deck.

Incoming Piercing Tough-discard responses open after the native damage/defeat occurrence resolves, before attack aftermath. Organic Steel then grants a new Tough that is kept; it does not retroactively prevent the completed HP damage. This follows the specific official Q4 ruling together with RRG1.8 p45's shared window for one occurrence. Cost-based discards still open responses before the paid effect. Apply reductions before the RRG1.8 zero-damage check, remove all prior Tough simultaneously, mark Piercing handled for this actual attack, and retain receipts so responding cannot cause Piercing to repeat. The adapter helper has an optional `deferToAttack` argument for this incoming Piercing boundary. Optional receipts and continuations must survive JSON save/load and preserve their actual actor's seat.
