# The Inheritors modular encounter contract

This continuation covers only The Inheritors modular encounter set from the Spider-Ham Hero Pack: nine unique faces and nine physical cards, 30030–30038. Hunting the Spider-Totems and each of the eight minions have one printed copy. Spider-Ham's identity, signature cards, player pool, obligation and nemeses remain outside this scope.

The frozen [imported catalog](../src/data/catalog-cards.json), its printing codes and quantities are retained. The original English scans recorded in the catalog provide the printed contract, including [Hunting the Spider-Totems](https://cerebrodatastorage.blob.core.windows.net/cerebro-cards/official/30030.jpg) and the minion scans 30031–30038 from the same source. The import's pinned sources and cutoff remain documented in [the catalog import contract](content-import-2026-09-30.md). [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) supplies the current general timing, keywords, blanking, encounter-deck and ownership rules.

The module is appended after the eleven existing selectable modules, preserving their order, defaults and physical source compositions. Its displayed difficulty of five is an app estimate based on the reinforcing minion effects, rather than an official difficulty rating. No catalog, starter-deck or correction JSON changes are required.

## Native rules

Hunting the Spider-Totems starts with its fixed six threat and printed three boost icons. Its villain-phase-begins Forced Interrupt must resolve before phase-begins Responses and ordinary main-scheme threat. It discards the actual top three encounter cards through native encounter-deck handling. Each Inheritor minion discarded by that effect is put into play using that same physical card. Eligible engagement players control a Web-Warrior character; otherwise the first player receives the minion. When multiple players qualify, the first player chooses the engagement recipient for each physical minion through an explicit native selection. Put into play does not resolve the minion's When Revealed ability, and previously discarded cards must not be substituted for cards discarded by this effect. Encounter-deck exhaustion stops the discard instruction at its shuffle boundary, preserving only cards actually discarded by that instruction. Saved decisions, physical card routing and any resulting native entry effects retain the original instances.

The eight minions continuously reinforce every current Inheritor minion. These values derive from the present unblanked sources in play; they are not permanently copied onto recipients. A donor leaving play or losing its text removes only its own contribution. Blanking a recipient's printed text does not remove effects granted by another source. Printed traits, statistics and boost icons remain distinct from textbox effects.

| Card           | Continuous contribution to Inheritor minions | When Revealed, if a Web-Warrior character is in play                  |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bora (30031)   | One acceleration icon each                   | Place one threat on each scheme.                                      |
| Brix (30032)   | Patrol                                       | Place two threat on the main scheme.                                  |
| Daemos (30033) | Stalwart                                     | Stun a character controlled by the revealing player.                  |
| Jennix (30034) | Guard                                        | Give the same Jennix a Tough status card.                             |
| Karn (30035)   | Attacks gain Overkill and Piercing           | Discard an upgrade or support controlled by the revealing player.     |
| Morlun (30036) | +1 ATK                                       | The revealing player takes two damage.                                |

| Verna (30038)  | Retaliate 1                                  | Deal one damage to each character controlled by the revealing player. |

The Web-Warrior condition checks characters across all players, while choices, damage and discarded controlled cards route to the actual revealer. Guard and Patrol affect native target legality; Stalwart affects status grants; acceleration icons contribute to normal main-scheme threat; Overkill, Piercing, Villainous and Retaliate use the ordinary native attack sequence. Solus retains his attached physical boost card through reload and uses it during the actual activation. Verna's complete damage packet and resulting defeat/leave windows must preserve native ordering and physical ownership.
