# Ant-Man native integration — 7 October 2026

Ant-Man now has a native three-form identity adapter and native handlers for his retail pack. Scott Lang, Tiny Ant-Man and Giant Ant-Man are separate printed faces of one identity. The 40-card Leadership source preconstructed list retains all original `12` printing codes and physical card IDs, including the distinct Hank Pym ally. Four exact Core reprints reuse the installed Core rules; the other 31 faces have dedicated native registrations.

The collection remains a mixture of catalog imports and playable engine support. This change implements Ant-Man's 35 retail faces, including his nemesis set and four supplementary deckbuilding cards. It does not add Wasp's hero pack or campaign automation.

## Forms and timing

`player.form` remains `hero` or `alter`; `player.heroForm` records `tiny` or `giant`. Printed identity code, traits, basic stats and hand size follow the active face: Scott Lang has REC 3 and hand size 6; Tiny has THW/ATK/DEF 2 and hand size 5; Giant has THW 1, ATK/DEF 3 and hand size 4. A voluntary change opens an explicit choice of the other two forms and supports cancellation and saved decisions. Resize and Swarm Tactics change hero forms without consuming the once-per-turn voluntary change.

Identity responses, Helmet and each Giant Strength are optional. Non-attack damage and non-thwart threat removal keep their native timing and do not consume Stunned or Confused. Helmet's card draw and Resize's trailing draw preserve their physical top cards. Giant Strength lasts until the current turn ends; Moxie lasts through the round. Pym Particles responds to an actual spent resource card in hero form and remains optional, including across JSON reload.

Army of Ants and Wrist Gauntlets check the actual hero form. Gauntlets require the printed pair of matching resources; canceling its saved payment leaves the upgrade ready. Hive Mind counts controlled Army supports. Giant Stomp uses native attack damage, target legality, status replacement and defeat timing. Swarm Tactics checks the actual Ant-Man/Wasp team-up.

## Retail cards and encounters

Hank Pym's counters use actual resources overpaid and determine his hit points. Giant-Man's ATK uses remaining HP; Ronin recognizes actual attached upgrades; Stinger checks the identity's current Avenger trait and does not count against the ally limit. Call for Aid searches the physical deck through the native deck-exhaustion rule. Power Gloves and Reinforced Suit attach to eligible allies. Team-Building Exercise chooses a physical card sharing a current hero trait and binds its immediate discount to that play.

Moxie and Lay Down the Law are available in the form-change response window. Lay Down the Law checks the actual mental resource spent. Moment of Triumph uses actual excess damage from an identity attack defeat. Muster Courage uses the villain's current stage and explicit distinct Tough recipients. Assess the Situation affects the current phase's hand size and refill.

Care for Cassie routes to Scott Lang, allows the printed optional alter-ego change, and either removes the physical obligation after exhaustion or discards the chosen physical hand card and locks form changes for its printed duration. Yellowjacket derives Tiny ATK and Giant Retaliate from its engaged identity's current form. Size Increase attaches to Yellowjacket when able, otherwise the villain, and spends its three counters after actual activations. Yellowjacket's Plan reveals the exact physical nemesis card it discards. Tech Theft blanks player Tech text across native engines while retaining printed traits, icons and stats.
