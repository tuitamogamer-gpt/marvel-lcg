# The Hood native scenario and the Standard II and Expert II sets

This continuation starts from `6574234` and adds The Hood scenario from The Hood scenario pack, with the pack's alternative Standard II and Expert II encounter sets for every scenario. The pack's nine modular sets follow in the next continuation.

## Original source and scope

`src/game/hood.ts` registers the 26 original faces of The Hood set, Standard II and Expert II: The Hood (I), (II) and (III), the three double-sided main scheme cards Making Connections, Promised Prosperity and Crime State, Established Dominance, The Hood's Mantle, The Hood's Pistol, Madame Masque, Unbridled Ambition, Field Recruitment and Upper Hand; Formidable Foe (both sides), Dark Dealings, Mob Mentality, Overwhelming Force and Total Annihilation; and Cruel Intentions, Ruination, Seek and Destroy and Slug It Out. Standard II's Shadow of the Past (`24053`) reprints the core card and keeps its native rules. The imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged.

Standard uses The Hood (I) and (II), Expert The Hood (II) and (III), as Making Connections states. Each game uses 15 cards of The Hood set: two villain cards, the three main scheme cards and ten encounter cards.

## Native rules

- **Setup (Making Connections 1A):** the players choose seven modular encounter sets; they are set aside and one of them, chosen at random, is shuffled into the encounter deck. In Expert, The Hood (II) is revealed and shuffles another random set-aside set into the deck. Making Connections (1B) starts with 1 threat per player and completes at 5 per player; when revealed, each player resolves Foul Play in player order.
- **Foul Play:** The Hood (I) discards the top encounter card and deals it facedown to the player unless it belongs to The Hood set; The Hood (II) discards the top two and deals the first card from outside his set; The Hood (III) discards two and deals each card from outside his set. A dealt card is revealed with that player's other encounter cards.
- **The main scheme:** Promised Prosperity (2A) shuffles a random set-aside set into the deck and places an acceleration token; 2B starts with 2 threat per player, completes at 8 per player, and when revealed each player resolves Foul Play, with 2 threat for each player who was not dealt a card this way. Crime State (3A) recruits, places an acceleration token and makes each player resolve Foul Play; 3B starts with 3 threat per player and completes at 10 per player (the players lose), and after step one of each villain phase each player resolves Foul Play.
- **The villain:** The Hood (II) and (III) shuffle a random set-aside set into the deck when revealed. Defeating the last stage wins.
- **Encounter cards:** Established Dominance attaches to your identity and makes you resolve Foul Play after The Hood activates against you; its Alter-Ego Action exhausts your identity and places 2 threat on the main scheme to discard it. The Hood's Mantle gives him retaliate 1 and steady, and leaves through a Hero Action that spends an energy, a mental and a physical resource; The Hood's Pistol (+1 ATK, +1 SCH) leaves for a mental and a physical resource, and as a boost it is revealed and attaches to him. Madame Masque has guard and resolves Foul Play when revealed and, for the player who defeats her, when defeated. Unbridled Ambition hinders 2 per player and makes each player resolve Foul Play when the villain phase begins. Field Recruitment recruits a set, resolves Foul Play and is removed from the game; as a boost it resolves Foul Play after the activation. Upper Hand makes The Hood attack a hero or scheme against an alter-ego, then resolves Foul Play.
- **Standard II and Expert II:** the setup screen's Encounter sets choice replaces the core Standard set with Standard II and, in Expert, the core Expert set with Expert II, in every scenario that uses those sets (rules insert, Alternative Sets). Formidable Foe begins the game in play (Setup, Permanent): in Standard the villain gains steady; in Expert its other side gives each enemy steady and adds its acceleration icon to step one. Dark Dealings makes the villain scheme with +1 SCH (as a boost, another boost card); Mob Mentality makes the villain and each minion engaged with a hero attack (with surge) or puts the first minion among the top seven encounter cards into play against an alter-ego; Overwhelming Force discards your highest-cost upgrade or support or gains surge (as a boost, another boost card); Total Annihilation (surge) makes the villain attack a hero with overkill, and as a boost gives the villain's attack overkill. Expert II's four cards have peril. Cruel Intentions (surge) deals each player a facedown encounter card and the villain a facedown boost card for his next activation, and as a boost deals you a card; Ruination (incite 1) discards until a side scheme, reveals it and places 2 threat on each scheme; Seek and Destroy (incite 1) puts your nemesis minion into play from the encounter deck, its discard pile or the set-aside area; Slug It Out (surge) exhausts your identity and deals you 2 damage, and as a boost deals 1 damage to each character you control and gives the villain another boost card.

## Coverage boundaries

- Peril is not enforced: other players may still respond while a player resolves a peril card.
- Until the pack's nine modular sets are imported, The Hood recommends seven earlier modular sets; any seven of the selectable sets can be chosen.
- A nemesis minion that Seek and Destroy or Kang's Wrath takes from the catalog because the hero keeps no set-aside nemesis cards stays unique: a later Shadow of the Past brings the rest of the set without a second minion.
- The executable registry contains 2,745 faces, including 2,306 dedicated faces, with 1,806 imported faces still unsupported. There are 57 playable identities, twenty-eight scenarios and forty-three modular sets. The Hood scenario pack has 28 of its 74 faces registered; its nine modular sets account for the remaining 46. The inventory pins 146 source files.

The configured source matrix contains 123,136 cases in eight disjoint shards of 15,392. The Hood adds 4,472 cases (52 expansion starter identities × two difficulties × 43 modular sets, each with six more recommended sets), and Standard II with Expert II adds 2,808 cases (one per identity, difficulty and scenario that uses the Standard set, with its recommended modular set). Manifest hashes retain all 115,856 previous mission names and seeds; the hash of the earlier configurations was checked against the full grid of `6574234`. The mission sweep also fixed Wasp's nemesis Beetle with The Doomsday Chair's Biomechanical Upgrades attached: when Biomechanical Upgrades replaces Beetle's defeat after the defeating player chose to shuffle him into the encounter deck, Beetle stays in play, and a restored minion no longer keeps a pending defeat that made it impossible to defeat later.
