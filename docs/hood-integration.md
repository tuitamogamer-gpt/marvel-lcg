# The Hood scenario pack: The Hood, nine modular sets, Standard II and Expert II

The first continuation, `16962b1`, added The Hood scenario with the pack's alternative Standard II and Expert II encounter sets for every scenario. This continuation adds the pack's nine modular encounter sets, which can be chosen in every scenario that uses modular sets and are now The Hood's recommended seven. All 74 faces of The Hood scenario pack are registered.

## Original source and scope

`src/game/hood.ts` registers the 26 original faces of The Hood set, Standard II and Expert II: The Hood (I), (II) and (III), the three double-sided main scheme cards Making Connections, Promised Prosperity and Crime State, Established Dominance, The Hood's Mantle, The Hood's Pistol, Madame Masque, Unbridled Ambition, Field Recruitment and Upper Hand; Formidable Foe (both sides), Dark Dealings, Mob Mentality, Overwhelming Force and Total Annihilation; and Cruel Intentions, Ruination, Seek and Destroy and Slug It Out. Standard II's Shadow of the Past (`24053`) reprints the core card and keeps its native rules.

`src/game/hood-modular.ts` registers the 47 faces of the nine modular sets, 50 physical cards: Beasty Boys (4), Brothers Grimm (5), Crossfire's Crew (6), Mister Hyde (4), Ransacked Armory (7, with two Tech Gauntlets and two Armored Guards), Sinister Syndicate (7), State of Emergency (6, with two Citywide Crisis), Streets of Mayhem (4) and Wrecking Crew (7). The imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged.

Standard uses The Hood (I) and (II), Expert The Hood (II) and (III), as Making Connections states. Each game uses 15 cards of The Hood set: two villain cards, the three main scheme cards and ten encounter cards.

## Native rules: The Hood

- **Setup (Making Connections 1A):** the players choose seven modular encounter sets; they are set aside and one of them, chosen at random, is shuffled into the encounter deck. In Expert, The Hood (II) is revealed and shuffles another random set-aside set into the deck. Making Connections (1B) starts with 1 threat per player and completes at 5 per player; when revealed, each player resolves Foul Play in player order.
- **The seven sets:** mission setup preselects the sets the rules insert ranks 1 to 7 by difficulty (Streets of Mayhem, Brothers Grimm, Ransacked Armory, State of Emergency, Beasty Boys, Mister Hyde and Sinister Syndicate), its suggestion for a game of lower difficulty; any seven selectable sets can be chosen.
- **Foul Play:** The Hood (I) discards the top encounter card and deals it facedown to the player unless it belongs to The Hood set; The Hood (II) discards the top two and deals the first card from outside his set; The Hood (III) discards two and deals each card from outside his set. A dealt card is revealed with that player's other encounter cards.
- **The main scheme:** Promised Prosperity (2A) shuffles a random set-aside set into the deck and places an acceleration token; 2B starts with 2 threat per player, completes at 8 per player, and when revealed each player resolves Foul Play, with 2 threat for each player who was not dealt a card this way. Crime State (3A) recruits, places an acceleration token and makes each player resolve Foul Play; 3B starts with 3 threat per player and completes at 10 per player (the players lose), and after step one of each villain phase each player resolves Foul Play.
- **The villain:** The Hood (II) and (III) shuffle a random set-aside set into the deck when revealed. Defeating the last stage wins.
- **Encounter cards:** Established Dominance attaches to your identity and makes you resolve Foul Play after The Hood activates against you; its Alter-Ego Action exhausts your identity and places 2 threat on the main scheme to discard it. The Hood's Mantle gives him retaliate 1 and steady, and leaves through a Hero Action that spends an energy, a mental and a physical resource; The Hood's Pistol (+1 ATK, +1 SCH) leaves for a mental and a physical resource, and as a boost it is revealed and attaches to him. Madame Masque has guard and resolves Foul Play when revealed and, for the player who defeats her, when defeated. Unbridled Ambition hinders 2 per player and makes each player resolve Foul Play when the villain phase begins. Field Recruitment recruits a set, resolves Foul Play and is removed from the game; as a boost it resolves Foul Play after the activation. Upper Hand makes The Hood attack a hero or scheme against an alter-ego, then resolves Foul Play.
- **Standard II and Expert II:** the setup screen's Encounter sets choice replaces the core Standard set with Standard II and, in Expert, the core Expert set with Expert II, in every scenario that uses those sets (rules insert, Alternative Sets). Formidable Foe begins the game in play (Setup, Permanent): in Standard the villain gains steady; in Expert its other side gives each enemy steady and adds its acceleration icon to step one. Dark Dealings makes the villain scheme with +1 SCH (as a boost, another boost card); Mob Mentality makes the villain and each minion engaged with a hero attack (with surge) or puts the first minion among the top seven encounter cards into play against an alter-ego; Overwhelming Force discards your highest-cost upgrade or support or gains surge (as a boost, another boost card); Total Annihilation (surge) makes the villain attack a hero with overkill, and as a boost gives the villain's attack overkill. Expert II's four cards have peril. Cruel Intentions (surge) deals each player a facedown encounter card and the villain a facedown boost card for his next activation, and as a boost deals you a card; Ruination (incite 1) discards until a side scheme, reveals it and places 2 threat on each scheme; Seek and Destroy (incite 1) puts your nemesis minion into play from the encounter deck, its discard pile or the set-aside area; Slug It Out (surge) exhausts your identity and deals you 2 damage, and as a boost deals 1 damage to each character you control and gives the villain another boost card.

## Native rules: the nine modular sets

- **Beasty Boys:** Beast Mode adds 1 to each amount of damage a stunned or confused friendly character would take. Griffin (quickstrike) stuns each character his attack damages and, when defeated while a friendly character is stunned, is shuffled back into the encounter deck. Mandrill confuses each character you control when revealed and has retaliate equal to the confused characters in play, friendly or enemy. Double Trouble stuns one character you control and confuses one (also as a boost).
- **Brothers Grimm:** when Brothers Grimm activates against you, the encounter deck is discarded until an attachment, which is revealed. Each of the four items attaches to a Mystic minion (a choice among several), otherwise to the villain; after the attached enemy activates against you it is discarded to discard a random card from your hand (Blackbird Pellets), deal you 3 indirect damage (Corrosive Egg Bomb), stun your identity (Paralytic Stardust) or make you discard an ally, support or upgrade (Unbreakable Thread), and deal you a facedown encounter card. As a boost, Brothers Grimm enters play engaged with the first player after the activation.
- **Crossfire's Crew:** Out for Blood deals 1 damage to the friendly character with the fewest remaining hit points and repeats while that character is defeated (also as a boost). Controller's attacks add the damaged character's ATK. Corruptor exhausts each ally you control and places 1 threat on the main scheme for each; as a boost you exhaust a character you control. Crossfire (quickstrike) attacks the friendly character with the fewest remaining hit points, with overkill and ranged; a tie among several characters is the player's choice, and an attack on another player's character resolves against that player. Mister Fear makes the engaged player spend a mental resource to ready a hero or ally they control; as a boost you discard from your deck until an ally. Caught in the Crossfire discards until a Crossfire's Crew minion, reveals it and deals you indirect damage for each Crossfire's Crew minion in play.
- **Mister Hyde:** Self-Experimentation reveals Mister Hyde from the encounter deck or discard pile, and damage a Brute enemy would take removes that much threat from the scheme instead. Calvin Zabo, when revealed with Mister Hyde in play, is discarded and Hyde attacks you with +2 ATK and overkill; when defeated, he brings Hyde into play engaged with his player. Mister Hyde replaces an engaged Calvin Zabo, gains a tough status card and deals 1 damage to each hero and ally. Hyde Formula makes Zabo scheme with +3 SCH and take 4 damage, then gives Hyde (possibly just brought in by Zabo) a tough status card and an attack against you, or gains surge if neither is in play.
- **Ransacked Armory:** each attachment goes to the minion with the most remaining hit points (a choice among ties). Without a minion, Flamethrower and Holoshield Generator find one in the encounter deck or discard pile and put it into play engaged with you, while Jetpack and Tech Gauntlets gain surge. Flamethrower (+3 ATK) makes the attached minion's attacks deal indirect damage; Holoshield Generator gives +4 hit points and retaliate 2; Jetpack discards the top encounter card to reduce attack damage by its boost icons; Tech Gauntlets give +1 ATK, +3 hit points and overkill. Armored Guard has guard and toughness.
- **Sinister Syndicate:** Crime Pays puts a Criminal minion from the encounter deck into play engaged with you (a choice among several), otherwise gains surge. Beetle discards your lowest-cost upgrade after damaging you; Boomerang deals 1 damage to each ally you control after attacking you; Shocker stuns the character that attacks him; Speed Demon attacks a character that attacks him first; White Rabbit discards a random card from your hand when she attacks you. Their boosts discard an upgrade, deal 2 damage to an ally, stun your highest-ATK character, discard your lowest-cost support or discard an identity-specific card. Sinister Onslaught makes each Criminal enemy attack a hero or scheme against an alter-ego, otherwise gains surge.
- **State of Emergency:** Feisty Heist discards the highest-cost card in your hand, Disaster at the Docks deals 3 indirect damage, Offshore Inferno discards the lowest-cost card you control and Hot Pursuit discards until a minion and puts it into play engaged with you. Citywide Crisis resolves each When Revealed ability of each side scheme in play again, without its incite or hinder, or places 2 threat on each scheme if there is none (also as a boost).
- **Streets of Mayhem:** each Setting surges and discards the other Setting in play. Back-Alley Enclave gives each character +1 ATK; Secret Lair gives each enemy an acceleration icon and each hero and ally +1 THW; Sewer Tunnels gives each character retaliate 1, alter-egos included; Warehouse District gives each character steady.
- **Wrecking Crew:** Top Talent hinders 2 per player and gives the villain and each Elite minion retaliate 1. Wrecker, Bulldozer, Piledriver and Thunderball are villainous; Wrecker gets +2 ATK on an undefended attack, Bulldozer's attacks gain overkill, Piledriver has retaliate 1 and Thunderball deals 1 damage to each character you control after attacking you. Combined Effort activates each Elite minion against its engaged player, otherwise gains surge; as a boost it gets a boost icon for each Elite minion. Magic Muscle gives each Brute enemy a tough status card, otherwise discards until a Brute minion and reveals it.

## Coverage boundaries

- Peril is not enforced: other players may still respond while a player resolves a peril card.
- Mister Fear's cost applies to ready effects; the ready step at the end of the player phase is a game rule, not an effect, and readies as usual.
- Secret Lair counts the villain and the minions in play as enemies.
- Self-Experimentation replaces damage to a Brute enemy only while it has threat to remove; once it is being defeated, for example by Followed's interrupt, the damage is dealt.
- A nemesis minion that Seek and Destroy or Kang's Wrath takes from the catalog because the hero keeps no set-aside nemesis cards stays unique: a later Shadow of the Past brings the rest of the set without a second minion.
- The executable registry contains 2,791 faces, including 2,353 dedicated faces, with 1,760 imported faces still unsupported. There are 57 playable identities, twenty-eight scenarios and fifty-two modular sets. The Hood scenario pack has all 74 faces registered. The inventory pins 147 source files.

The configured source matrix contains 148,408 cases in sixteen disjoint shards of 9,275 or 9,276; CI runs the sixteen shards in parallel. The nine modular sets add 25,272 cases (52 expansion starter identities × two difficulties × nine sets in each scenario with modular sets, including 936 for The Hood). Manifest hashes retain all 123,136 previous mission names and seeds, checked against the complete grid of `16962b1`; The Hood's Standard II and Expert II configurations keep the recommended set they were named with. The first continuation's matrix grew to 123,136 cases with 4,472 Hood and 2,808 Standard II and Expert II cases.

The mission sweep of the first continuation fixed Wasp's nemesis Beetle with The Doomsday Chair's Biomechanical Upgrades attached: when Biomechanical Upgrades replaces Beetle's defeat after the defeating player chose to shuffle him into the encounter deck, Beetle stays in play, and a restored minion no longer keeps a pending defeat that made it impossible to defeat later. This continuation carries overkill excess from an attack against one specific ally, such as Crossfire's, to that ally's controller, as it already did for a defending ally. Its mission sweep also closed an optional response whose source left play before its window opened: Unbreakable Thread can discard Web of Life and Destiny after a Web-Warrior ally was defeated, and the support no longer offers its response afterwards.
