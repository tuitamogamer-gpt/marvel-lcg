# The Once and Future Kang native scenario

This continuation starts from `1600763` and adds The Once and Future Kang scenario pack: Kang the Conqueror arrives in the present, scatters the heroes through time into separate game areas, and returns for Kang's Wrath. The pack's three modular sets, Temporal, Master of Time and Anachronauts, are selectable in every scenario.

## Original source and scope

All 58 original faces of the pack are registered by `src/game/kang.ts`: the Kang set (Kang (I), the four Kang (II) villains Immortus, Iron Lad, Rama-Tut and Scarlet Centurion, Kang (III), the seven double-sided main scheme cards and 30 encounter cards), the six Expert Kang villains, and the Temporal (four cards), Anachronauts (seven) and Master of Time (five) modular sets. The imported catalog, original source decks and artwork remain unchanged.

Standard uses the six Kang villains of the Kang set and Expert swaps them for the six Expert Kang villains and adds the core Expert set, as the rules insert describes. Each game uses the 43 cards of the Kang set (the Expert villains replace its six villains). The scenario uses one modular slot, recommended Temporal. Kang's Arrival removes each player's obligation cards from the game, so the encounter deck holds the Kang set's own obligations instead.

## Native rules

- **Setup (Kang's Arrival 1A):** each Kang (II), Kang (III) and the four Kang's Dominion side schemes are set aside, with The Master of Time, the four stage 3 cards and Kang's Wrath. Kang (I) is the only villain (12 or 15 hit points per player). Kang's Arrival (1B) starts without threat, completes at 7 threat per player (the players lose) and deals each player an encounter card when revealed.
- **Kang (I) and Kang (III):** when Kang attacks you, you choose to place 1 threat on the main scheme or give him +2 ATK for the attack. Kang (I)'s defeat advances the main scheme at the end of the phase; until then no villain is in play: nothing can attack, damage, heal or target him and he does not activate, while his minions still do. Attachments and status cards do not pass to the next Kang (rules insert FAQ).
- **The Master of Time (stage 2):** one acceleration token for each side scheme in play, which are discarded; each player reveals a random stage 3A in player order and creates their own game area with its Kang (II) (18 or 22 hit points) and its stage 3B scheme, and is dealt an encounter card. The unused stage 3 cards and Kang (I) leave the game.
- **Separate game areas (stage 3):** each game area has its own villain, main scheme, side schemes and minions; the encounter deck, its discard pile, the turn order and the first player token stay shared. Cards in one area cannot affect another: "each player" on Kang's encounter cards means the players in the same area, defenders must share the attacked player's area, and hazard icons deal their extra cards to the players of their own area. In step one each stage 3B places its own threat (1, fixed); the acceleration tokens wait on stage 2B and add nothing in stage 3, then apply again to Kang's Wrath, because acceleration tokens stay in play when their main scheme leaves (Rules Reference 1.8, Acceleration Token).
- **Kang (II):** Immortus cannot take damage while a minion is in play; Iron Lad has retaliate 1; Rama-Tut gets +1 ATK for each obligation in his game area; Scarlet Centurion's attacks gain piercing. Defeating him removes his stage 3B scheme from the game, and at the end of the phase the players of that area join another area of their choice; side schemes move with them and engaged minions stay engaged.
- **A completed stage 3B** is not a loss: one set-aside Kang's Dominion goes facedown under Kang's Wrath, and at the end of the phase Kang (II) and the stage leave the game and the area combines with another. The stage is also completed when every player at it is defeated. When areas combine, duplicate unique player cards are discarded, keeping the first copy in player order.
- **Kang's Wrath (stage 4):** when no other game area remains, every player is in stage 2B's area. Kang (III) enters (20 or 25 hit points per player), each facedown Kang's Dominion is revealed as a side scheme (3 threat per player; Kang cannot take damage while one is in play; its defeat deals the defeating player an encounter card), and each player's nemesis minion enters play engaged with them from the encounter deck, its discard pile or the set-aside area. A later Shadow of the Past does not bring a second copy. Kang's Wrath completes at 10 threat per player (the players lose); defeating Kang (III) wins.
- **Attachments:** Temporal Shield prevents all damage from an attack on Kang and deals 1 damage to the attacker (one shield per attack). Future Weapon gives +2 ATK and overkill, stuns a hero the attack damages, and is discarded after the attack. Frozen in Time attaches to your identity and is discarded instead of readying it (also as a boost).
- **Obligations** stay in their player's play area until their Alter-Ego Action discards them: Weakened (after you use a basic hero power, attack, thwart or defense, take 1 damage; a physical resource), Stolen Memories (the top 8 cards of your deck facedown under it; a mental resource), Depowered (no hero-specific cards; a hero-specific card), Time-Travel Hijinks (your highest-cost card goes facedown under it; an energy resource) and Fear of Kang (you cannot attack an enemy titled Kang; a random card). The cards under an obligation go to their owner's discard pile with it. Corrupted Timestream blocks these actions.
- **Treacheries and side schemes:** Energy Blast (Kang attacks a hero; an alter-ego discards an ally or support or it gains surge), Manipulated Timestream, Time-Travel Tactics, Past Machinations (each player chooses and reveals a different obligation), Corrupted Timestream, Pinned Down, Rampage, and the boosts of Macrobots, Time-Travel Tactics and Frozen in Time.
- **Modular sets:** Ancient Warrior (stun boost), Chitauri Soldier (indirect damage for the boost icons of the discarded card), Tyrannosaurus Rex (piercing) and Time Portal (shuffled back into the encounter deck when defeated); Apocryphus, Deathunt 9000, Sir Raston, Terminatrix and Wildrun with their boosts that give more boost cards, The Anachronauts and Kang's Chosen; Kang (Master of Time), Time-Displaced Soldier, Fear of Kang, Light of Centuries Sphere and Ancient Grudge.

## Game areas in a one-area engine

The engine keeps one native villain, scheme, side scheme row and minion row. In stage 3 the active player's game area is native and every other area waits in the scenario state; a hook in `activateSeat` swaps the native area whenever the active seat changes, so every effect that already activates its player's seat (turns, activations, reveals, deferred effects) resolves in that player's area. Saving clears the stored copy of the native area, so a save never holds a card twice. Per-area step one, hazard dealing and the end-of-phase joins iterate the areas explicitly.

## Coverage boundaries

- Player cards whose effects reach every player ("each player draws a card") and optional interrupts that look at every player's cards, such as threat or damage prevention, still see players in other game areas.
- Per-player values in stage 3 use the number of players who started the game, as everywhere else.
- Kang's encounter cards that name Kang (Temporal Shield, Future Weapon, Energy Blast, Macrobots) use the villain; Fear of Kang also stops attacks on Kang (Master of Time).
- Stolen Memories takes the cards that remain in the deck when fewer than 8 do; the deck then refills as usual.
- Temporal Shield triggers when the attack would damage Kang.
- Player side schemes stay in play when The Master of Time discards the side schemes, and move into their controller's game area.
- The executable registry contains 2,719 faces, including 2,280 dedicated faces, with 1,832 imported faces still unsupported. There are 57 playable identities, twenty-seven scenarios and forty-three modular sets. The Once and Future Kang product has all 58 of its faces registered. The inventory pins 145 source files.

The configured source matrix contains 115,856 cases in eight disjoint shards of 14,482. Kang adds 4,472 cases (52 expansion starter identities × two difficulties × 43 modular sets) and the three new modular sets add 7,800 cases in the other 25 scenarios with a modular slot. Manifest hashes retain all 103,584 previous mission names and seeds; the hash of the earlier configurations was checked against the full grid of `1600763`. Each hero's block now holds 2,228 configurations, so it starts on shard 0 or 4 and the larger scenarios split 546 or 572 cases per shard.

Rules references: The Once and Future Kang rules insert (Setup, Creating Separate Game Areas, Playing with Separate Game Areas, Joining Another Game Area, Adjusting the Difficulty, Modular Encounter Sets, Rules Clarifications), [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) (Acceleration Token, Each Player, Elimination, Piercing, Retaliate, Overkill, Surge, Incite) and the original card texts and scans.
