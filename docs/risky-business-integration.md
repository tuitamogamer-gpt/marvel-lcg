# Risky Business and the remaining Green Goblin modules

`src/game/risky-business.ts` authors all **19 Risky Business faces** (`02001a/b` through `02013`). `src/game/goblin-modules.ts` authors all **13 faces** in A Mess of Things, Power Drain and Running Interference (`02037`–`02049`). Together with the existing 21 Mutagen Formula and four Goblin Gimmicks faces, the exact imported Green Goblin retail product is **57 faces**. Face counts include linked reverse sides and are different from physical printed-card quantities.

Native configuration, dispatcher hooks and environment display are separate from authored module coverage. No part of this document certifies arbitrary imported cards or automated campaigns. The live dependency/registration audit remains [engine-expansion-inventory.json](engine-expansion-inventory.json).

## Primary rules and source boundaries

The [official Green Goblin insert](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc02_green_goblin_rules_insert.pdf), pp. 4–5, specifies the special form-flip preservation rules, next-stage face, Goblin reveal abilities and replacement activations. [Rules Reference 1.8](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf) supplies current timing and general rules: encounter attachment ownership (p. 4), multiple enemy activations (p. 6), attacks and actual damage conditions (pp. 8–9), discard/exhaustion (pp. 16, 33), each-player ordering (p. 17), flips (p. 20), in-play source requirements and unnumbered keyword non-stacking (pp. 23, 25), main-scheme attachments (p. 27), player elimination and ability continuation (p. 34), Surge (p. 42), same-character villain stages (p. 47), wild resources (p. 48), and the Norman mid-attack boost exception (p. 59).

FFG-authored designer rulings are also available through third-party archival pages. [Alex's June 4, 2024 correction, ruling 202](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/) explicitly requires Gang-Up to resolve the villain first, then minions, including one created during the villain attack. [Alex's August 3 and September 22, 2026 rulings](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-7/) confirm that Surge is a When Revealed ability, can be canceled by Enhanced Spider-Sense, and shares timing with Incite and other When Revealed abilities. Older contrary Surge rulings do not describe current rules. [The archived Caleb ruling about Media Coverage](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/official-ffg-rulings/) excludes villain-stage reveals from its player-revealed encounter trigger.

The counter initialization on each newly faceup Criminal Enterprise/State of Madness face follows its printed constant text and the double-sided/in-play rules. The official insert explicitly preserves the physical villain's other state through form flips. No separate current FAQ specifically restating the environment initializer was found; the implementation records this as a reading of the printed cards and general rules, rather than inventing a quoted ruling.

## Goblin modular host contract

| Native point               | Hook                            | Required behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Generic entry, then reveal | `goblinModuleReveal`            | Enter minions/side schemes once with printed state; route attachments/treacheries to module text. Empty arrays are owned no-ability results; null means unowned.                                                                                                                                    |
| Boost star                 | `goblinModuleBoost`             | Tail Sweep stuns final attacked player's identity. Electro/Pulse/Bolt/Therapy discard exactly three physical encounter cards, stopping at first exhaustion; discarded stars do not resolve.                                                                                                         |
| Attack completion          | `goblinModuleAttackResponses`   | Record actual damaged characters, including ally and overkill identity separately. Scorpion stuns damaged characters; Electro works after a performed attack even if no damage; Tombstone needs identity damage. Use first-player forced-response ordering and revalidate source before initiation. |
| Actual side-scheme defeat  | `goblinModuleDefeated`          | Power Drain's When Defeated interrupts leaving play. Its acceleration stays active during resolution. Discard two encounter cards, count numeric boost icons, then each player discards that many printed hand resources, if able.                                                                  |
| Identity readiness/form    | `goblinIdentityLocked`          | All Tied Up prevents every identity ready/form change, including refill, event effects and optional obligation flips. Exhaustion still works. Discard/removal restores ordinary behavior.                                                                                                           |
| Player reveal text only    | `goblinWhenRevealedCopies`      | Each attached Media Coverage adds one resolution of each When Revealed ability, including current printed Surge/Incite. Recalculate conditions for each resolution; never repeat card entry, Quickstrike, Toughness, setup, boost or villain-stage reveal.                                          |
| Attachment actions         | `goblinModuleAttachmentActions` | Only attached identity controller can trigger/pay either attachment because their text uses you/your (RRG p. 4). All Tied Up costs mental+physical in either form; Media Coverage costs mental in alter ego. Optional canceled payment leaves attachment in place.                                  |

Gang-Up uses whole native activation callbacks. After the villain's complete activation it discovers eligible minions, allows their order, and preserves the original target player. Continuations contain serializable processed instance IDs and revalidate departed enemies. Tail Sweep checks a hero target and the actual performed result; Stunned/Webbed Up replacements cause its fallback, while Tough/zero damage after a real attack do not.

Electromagnetic Pulse must move the actual discarded Electro instance into play without revealing it, even if encounter exhaustion reshuffled that instance. Native uniqueness can prevent entry; having discarded Electro still prevents the card's no-Electro Surge condition. Each discard batch stops at its first empty-deck boundary; subsequent separate effects can use the reset deck.

Forced hand-resource discard is different from spending a cost. Power Drain counts printed resources of any type, including doubles and wild. Tombstone accepts printed mental/physical; wild-only cards cannot substitute outside resource generation for a cost (RRG p. 48). Exhaustible generators and conditionally enhanced generated resources do not satisfy these hand discards. Preserve whole selected cards and their owners.

## Verification and bounded timing support

`tests/risky-business.test.ts` has 41 port-level fixtures; `tests/risky-engine.test.ts` has 33 actual dispatcher fixtures. They verify setup before hands, replacement priority, both environment faces, all five boost stars, immediate mid-attack flip, packet replacement/no spill, actual IDs/attachments/statuses/retained boosts, same-face advancement, all stage reveal values, first-player indirect order including elimination during the first packet, main advancement/Then/attachments/hazard/loss, exhaustion and pending-decision JSON hydration.

`tests/goblin-modules.test.ts` and the 50 fixtures in `tests/goblin-module-engine.test.ts` cover all 13 faces with port and native fixtures. Native tests exercise physical module deck construction, Quickstrike, actual ally/identity damage and Tough/overkill, source suppression after first-player Retaliate, newly engaged Gang-Up minions, Tail Sweep cancellation, every discard boost, indirect packets, real Electro instances, Power Drain's still-in-play interrupt, forced/optional typed payment, exact hand resource restrictions, owner-only attachment actions, refill/obligation locks, repeated text with one physical entry, stacked Media Coverage, printed Surge and Enhanced Spider-Sense cancellation, and save/reload during each-player hand selection.

`src/game/reveal-window.ts` now schedules printed Surge, Incite and ordinary When Revealed text as separate groups in one serializable window. The first player chooses their order; the original revealer remains the recipient of player-specific effects. Native hooks cover Core treacheries and the authored Risky/Mutagen/Goblin treacheries and attachments. Attachment entry runs once before its window, with the Media copy count captured before the new attachment enters. An attachment's quoted timing reference is not a timing header. `tests/reveal-window.test.ts` supplies 12 scheduler fixtures, including Incite ordering; the native suite proves both Exhaustion orders, an observable Kree Manipulator loss boundary, original-revealer routing after JSON reload, and one physical cleanup after elimination.

Conditional gains register Surge in the same window instead of dealing immediately. RRG p. 25 prevents extra instances of this unnumbered keyword from stacking. Media repeats the resulting keyword ability according to the captured copy count, while independently recalculating each text resolution. Native probes cover alter-ego Gang-Up, Pulse's two distinct discard batches, blocked Glider entry and Overconfidence where only the first of two attacks satisfies its damage threshold. This applies the printed Media text and current general keyword rules; no explicit FFG ruling addressing every compounded conditional combination was found. The helper currently represents one applicable original text group per card. Future cards with several independently applicable text groups, broader reveal interrupts or new timing restrictions need additional authored groups/hooks and evidence. The registry and bounded fixtures do not certify the imported corpus.
